Place and Duration of Study: Department of Gynaecology and Obstetrics, Combined Military Hospital Rawalpindi, from Jun 2020 to Jun 2021. Methodology: A total of 124 pregnant obese women (BMI more than 30kg/m2), gestational age between 13-24 months, having fasting blood glucose and blood pressure within normal limits were enrolled in the study and were subsequently followed throughout pregnancy upon antenatal visits scheduled as per guidelines.
