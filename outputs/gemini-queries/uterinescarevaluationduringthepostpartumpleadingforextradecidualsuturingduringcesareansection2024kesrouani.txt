STUDY DESIGN: A retrospective observational study included cases of primary cesarean section. Hysterotomy sites closed using a running full-thickness technique including the uterine mucosa (group 1) were compared to hysterotomies operated by the same surgeon but with extramucosal suturing (group 2).
