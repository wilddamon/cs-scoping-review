"Objective: To assess the contribution of a new type of partogram, used in labor monitoring, in caesarean section rates. Material(s) and Method(s): The study included term singleton uncomplicated pregnancies divided into two groups. Two types of partogram were used in labor monitoring. In the first group, the classical WHO partogram (A) was used. In the second group, a new type of partogram, in which cervical dilatation and the position of descending head (B) (one line) were estimated and reported, was used. The labor duration and caesarean section rates were calculated and compared in the two groups. Result(s): A statistically significant decrease in labor duration (from the initiation of the active phase of labor to the delivery time) (dt1+dt2+dt3) (p<0.001, A: median: 318.4+/-10.4 min, B: 246.56+/-8.28 min) and in caesarean section rates was noted (p<0.001, A: 89 vs B: 49).
