Despite the fact that diagnosis of M. pneumoniae infection is based on sero-prevalence studies but molecular diagnostic techniques, such as PCR, have offered improvements in sensitivity, specificity and rapidity over the latest methods. Method(s): This study included 94 children between 2 and 13 years old; Fifty in-patient asthmatic children and 44 non-asthmatic children as control group who were out-patients of the same hospital.
