"Introduction: Recurrent Pregnancy Loss (RPL)-also known as recurrent miscarriage or habitual abortion-is a significant concern in reproductive health, affecting approximately 2% to 5% of couples. Traditionally, RPL is defined as the occurrence of three consecutive pregnancy losses before 20 weeks of gestation, measured from the last menstrual period. Established causes include anatomical abnormalities of the uterus, antiphospholipid syndrome, hormonal or metabolic imbalances, and genetic defects. Material(s) and Method(s): To assess the role of low dose aspirin (75 mg) in preventing recurrent pregnancy loss, a comparative, prospective interventional study was done. Inclusion criteria: all pregnant women in first trimester with history of atleast 2 pregnancy losses and patient with known APLA positive status (Antiphospholipid antibodies),endocrinological abnormality, cervical insufficiency, PCOS and with bleeding tendencies were excluded. Study duration was 6 month 25 patient were give aspirin and 25 were placebo. Result(s): In group a total of 18 (72%) patient have successfully completed the delivery while in group b only 3(12%) have completed.
