"Background: The current literature does not provide accurate information about the association between use of artificial nipples and sucking patterns in infants. Aims: We aimed to investigate whether pacifier use and/or bottle-feeding were associated with nutritive sucking (NS) and non-nutritive sucking (NNS) patterns. Study design: Cross-sectional study. Subjects: 429 full-term infants/mothers. Outcome measures: Data were collected through a questionnaire and observations of NS during breastfeeding and NNS (finger sucking).
