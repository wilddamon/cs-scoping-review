"BACKGROUND: Placenta accreta spectrum (PAS) describes the abnormal adherence of the placenta trophoblast to the myometrium and is associated with high foeto-maternal morbidity and mortality. METHODOLOGY: An analytical cross-sectional study of all recorded cases of placenta accreta spectrum managed at RSUTH from 1(st) January 2016 to 31(st) December 2021. RESULTS: There were 14195 deliveries, 137 cases of placenta praevia and 39 cases of placenta accreta spectrum. The modal age group was 35-39 years. The median blood loss was 650mls range of 450-2000mls. The majority of the study participants were booked 34(87.2%) and had secondary level education 17(43.6%). History of a previous caesarean section was statistically significantly associated with PAS P<0.001 while other factors did not attain significance. CONCLUSION: Placenta accreta spectrum is not uncommon among women with pregnancies complicated by placenta praevia at the RSUTH.
