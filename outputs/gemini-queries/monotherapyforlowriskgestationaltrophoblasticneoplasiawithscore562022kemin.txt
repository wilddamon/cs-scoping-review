Method(s): The low-risk GTN patients from 2012 to 2019 were enrolled. Result(s): 75 cases (33.5%) were included. Complete remission was in all patients. Among the 29 cases taking single-agent chemotherapy, 22 cases (75.9%) developed drug resistance.
