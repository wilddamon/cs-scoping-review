Material(s) and Method(s): This prospective study included all pregnant women after 28 weeks period of gestation who underwent caesarean section for the 1st time between December 2016 and June 2018 at Central Referral Hospital of Sikkim Manipal Institute of Medical Sciences (SMIMS), Gangtok, Sikkim - India.
