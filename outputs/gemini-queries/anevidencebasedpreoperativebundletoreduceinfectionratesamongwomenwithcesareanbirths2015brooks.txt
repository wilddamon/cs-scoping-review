"Objective: To address the following clinical questions: For women with cesarean births, does the use of a standardized antibiotic and timing reduce the incidence of postoperative infection? Design: The evidence-based project was designed to create guidelines for the selection of antibiotics with weight-based dosing and timing of administration and for the selection and standardized use of an intraoperative surgical skin preparation.
