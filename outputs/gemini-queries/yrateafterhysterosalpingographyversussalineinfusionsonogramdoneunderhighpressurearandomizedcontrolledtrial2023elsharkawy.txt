A prospective, randomized controlled clinical trial was conducted from March 2020 till April 2021 on 183 eligible women recruited from the infertility outpatient Clinic of El-Shatby University Hospital, Alexandria, Egypt. Patients with tubes patent under low pressure were assigned as "group B1" and patients with tubes which were occluded under low pressure were assigned as "group B2" and subjected to SIStreat.
