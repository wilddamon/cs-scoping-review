Material(s) and Method(s): This is a retrospective observational study in SMGS Jammu for a period of 2 years from july 2020 to june 2022. Patients undergoing cesarean sections with SSI during this period. Result(s): In the present investigation, a total of 800 instances of lower segment caesarean section (LSCS) were examined, revealing that 50 cases (6.25%) were afflicted with wound infection. The commonest clinical manifestation of SSI in our study was purulent discharge in 13 (26%) cases. Fever was present in 3(6%) cases. Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) was identified as the most prevalent microorganism isolated from wound exudates. Escherichia coli and methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) were among the frequently encountered microorganisms.
