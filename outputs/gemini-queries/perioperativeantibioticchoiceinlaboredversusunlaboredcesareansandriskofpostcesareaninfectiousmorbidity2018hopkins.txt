METHODS: We conducted a retrospective cohort analysis of the Maternal-Fetal Medicine Unit Cesarean Registry. RESULTS: Our analysis included 43,735 women who delivered via CD, 48% following labor. In both groups, 95% of women received b-lactam antibiotics. CONCLUSION: In unlabored CD, non-b-lactam antibiotics have a higher risk of wound complications compared with b-lactam regimens.
