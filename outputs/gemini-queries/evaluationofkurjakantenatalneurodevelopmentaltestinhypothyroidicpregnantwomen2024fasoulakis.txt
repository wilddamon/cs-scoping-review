Antenatal screening for early detection of fetal neurological well-being has provided valuable information. Material(s) and Method(s): Kurjak antenatal neurodevelopmental test (KANET) was applied from 28 weeks to 38 weeks in 85 gestations (group A) with hypothyroidism and 87 euthyroid pregnant women (group B).
