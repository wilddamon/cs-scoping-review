"OBJECTIVES: This study aims to estimate the association between pregnancy-associated maternal cancers, diagnosed both prenatally and postnatally, and birth outcomes.;DESIGN: Population-based register study.;SETTING: National registers of Denmark and Sweden.;PARTICIPANTS: A total of 5 523 365 children born in Denmark (1977-2008) and Sweden (1973-2006).
