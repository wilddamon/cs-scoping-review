Methods: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Controlled (RDBPC) trial was carried out on 200 healthy new mothers divided into an active group taking a supplement containing Limosilactobacillus reuteri PBS072 and Bifidobacterium breve BB077 (4 x 109 CFU/day) plus multivitamins and a control group (multivitamin complex only) for 90 days. Conclusions: Microbiota alterations could influence a post-delivery woman's mental state.
