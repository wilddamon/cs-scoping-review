This study evaluates the impact of the LaQshya initiative on maternal outcomes in a tertiary care hospital. Materials and Methods: A retrospective observational study was conducted over 8 months comparing outcomes from the pre-implementation phase (May-August 2024) and the post-implementation phase (September-December 2024) of the LaQshya initiative.
