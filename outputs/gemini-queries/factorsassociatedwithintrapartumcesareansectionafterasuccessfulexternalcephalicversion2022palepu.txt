Methods: This study was based on labor and delivery details, from the ECV registers, of 430 women who had successful ECV and they compared to those with spontaneous cephalic presentation. Conclusion: Women who had a successful ECV had an increased risk of intrapartum cesarean section compared those women with spontaneous cephalic presentation.
