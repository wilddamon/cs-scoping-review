METHODS: We performed a prospective study in 308 women with singleton pregnancies undergoing IOL at term. Logistic regression analysis was used to determine which maternal factors, Bishop score, method of IOL, and TPU parameters were significant predictors of cesarean section (CS) and CS due to no progress (CS-NP).
