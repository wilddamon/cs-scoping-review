The aim of the research was to find out the relationship between early breastfeeding initiation and involution uteri of postpartum mothers. The research was conducted in Nenemallomo Regional Public Hospital and ArifinNu'mang Public Regional Hospital of SidenrengRappang Regency from October to December 2014. The results of the research indicated that the variable of mother's age and education have a relationship with involution uteri, while continuous breastfeeding variable (exclusive breastfeeding) until day 7 has a relationship with involution uteri.
