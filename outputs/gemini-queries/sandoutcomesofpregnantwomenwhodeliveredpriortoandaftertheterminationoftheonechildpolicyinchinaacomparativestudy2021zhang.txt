"BACKGROUND: The new Chinese fertility policy has recently received widespread public attention. However, there are few studies available on the comprehensive epidemiology of maternal and infant health with respect to the characteristic changes of childbearing women. METHODS: This was a retrospective study. Data were collected from three representative hospitals in Zhejiang Province using stratified random sampling.
