"Aim:To determine the frequency of thyroid dysfunction in patients of pre-eclampsia and gestational hypertension in the second half of pregnancy. Material(s) and Method(s): The observational cross-sectional study was done in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Midnapore Medical College and Hospital, West Bengal, India, over a period of 18 months.This study incorporated 176 pregnant women with hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, fulfilling the inclusion and exclusion criteria of the study. Serum levels of TSH, FT4, and FT3 were measured. Result(s):The percentage prevalence of biochemical thyroid dysfunction was 40.8% and 18% among pregnant women in Pre-eclampsia Group and Gestational Hypertension group respectively. A statistically significant difference was observed between the two groups.Sub-clinical hypothyroidism was found in 18.8% of hypertensive pregnant women and was considered as the most common form of thyroid dysfunction in this study. It was more frequent in patients of pre-eclampsia.
