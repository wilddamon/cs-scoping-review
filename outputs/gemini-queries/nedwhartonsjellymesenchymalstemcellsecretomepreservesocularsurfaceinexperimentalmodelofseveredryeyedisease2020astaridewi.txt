"BACKGROUND: Secretome derived from Wharton's jelly mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSC) has a beneficial effect for ocular surface regeneration; however, the high amount of vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) remains a challenge for its application. METHOD(S): Pre-and post-experimental study composed of topical instillation of WJ-MSC secretome group, balanced salt solution control group, and normal control group.
