The present study aimed to identify the clinical and demographic predictors of CS in women classified in Robson 1 and 2a categories, based on deliveries conducted in 2019 at a private hospital in Athens, Greece. Methodology A retrospective analysis was conducted employing data from 8,572 deliveries in 2019 at a private hospital in Athens by classifying women based on the Robson classification. The presence of gestational diabetes (OR = 0.66, p = 0.019) and neonatal birth weight below 3,000 g (OR = 0.73, p = 0.027) were also protective factors.
