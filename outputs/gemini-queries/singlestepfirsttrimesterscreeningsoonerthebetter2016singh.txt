"Purpose: To evaluate the role of single-step universal screening in first trimester and its effectiveness. Material(s) and Method(s): Three thousand women attending antenatal clinic of Pt JNM Medical College, Raipur, were screened with 75 gm OGTT in their first trimester irrespective of their last meal, and those who were screened negative were again subjected to OGTT at 24-28 weeks.
