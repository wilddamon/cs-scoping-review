"Objective: Investigation of possible prognostic factors affecting survival in the congenital diaphragmatic hernia. Method(s): We included all congenital diaphragmatic hernia-diagnosed patients treated in neonatal intensive care units of two centers between 2016 and 2020. We recorded antenatal and birth histories, anthropometric measurements, and clinical features of the hernia. We assessed visceral herniation to the hemithorax, pneumothorax, severely decreased fetal lung volume (<15%), the need for emergency intervention due to pneumothorax in the operating/delivery room, and pulmonary hypertension effects on the survival status. The clinical conditions associated with high mortality were pulmonary hypertension (p=0.006), pneumothorax (p=0.009), severely decreased fetal lung volume (<15%) (p<0.001), hepatic (p=0.024) and gastric (p=0.029) herniations to the thorax.
