"Aim: To assess the effect of Color Doppler indices on intrauterine growth restriction diagnosis in high-risk pregnancies Study design: Across-sectional study Place and duration: This study was conducted at Al Shifa Hospital Quetta, Pakistan. from January 2020 to July 2020. Methodology: In this study, 100 patients with suspected intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR) in their 31 to 41 weeks of pregnancy have participated. The patients were studied till the babies were delivered. The Doppler indices and the grayscale results were correlated to the baby's weight. Result(s): The HC/AC ratio was found to be sensitive (84.4%). Whereas the most specific was oligohydramnios and C/U ratio that was 100% and 68.8% respectively.
