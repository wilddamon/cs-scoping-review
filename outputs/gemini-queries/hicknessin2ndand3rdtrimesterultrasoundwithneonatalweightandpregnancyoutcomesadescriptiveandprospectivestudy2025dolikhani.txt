"Background: Placental thickness (PT) is important in fetal growth and weight prognosis. Our study is designed to explore the correlation between placental dimensions/thickness and the baby's birthweight, APGAR score at one minute, and pregnancy outcomes. Material(s) and Method(s): This descriptive study was directed at 60 singleton pregnant women. Placental thickness was recorded during the 2nd and 3rd trimesters screening.
