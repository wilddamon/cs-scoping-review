Material(s) and Method(s): We conducted a retrospective matched cohort study in a tertiary care hospital from January 2014 to December 2015, among 182 nulliparous women at gestational age of 390/7 weeks or more who had single live pregnancy with known cervical status of <=5 at 380/7 to 386/7 weeks and were managed either by elective induction or expectant management.
