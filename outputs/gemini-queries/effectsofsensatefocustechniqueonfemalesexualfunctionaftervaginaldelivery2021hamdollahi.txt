"Background: Sexual function undergoes the changes during the postpartum period and after vaginal delivery. The women's baseline sexual function was assessed by the female sexual function index. Women were randomly assigned to two groups: Including intervention group where they received specific instruction on sensate focus technique and were followed up weekly for 2 months and control group where they received the information about recovery from childbirth and lactation.
