Methods We tested associations between clean delivery practices and maternal deaths, using a pooled dataset for 40, 602 home births across sites in the three countries. Conclusions Our evidence suggests that hand washing in delivery is critical for maternal survival among home deliveries in rural South Asia, although the exact magnitude of this effect is uncertain due to inherent biases associated with observational data from low resource settings.
