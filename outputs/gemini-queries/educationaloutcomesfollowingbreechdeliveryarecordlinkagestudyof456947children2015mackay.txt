We determined whether vaginal breech delivery was associated with educational outcomes.;METHODS: We linked three Scotland-wide administrative databases at an individual level: the ScotXed school census; Scottish Qualifications Authority (SQA) examination results; and Scottish Morbidity Record (SMR02) maternity database.
