"Objective. Analysis of the course of pregnancy and labour outcomes in women with gestational diabetes mellitus who received consultations at the Surgut Clinical Perinatal Centre. Patients and methods. A prospective examination of 100 pregnant women with gestational diabetes mellitus, assessment of pregnancy and labour outcomes, of adherence to prescribed therapy and recommendations. The average age of patients was 32.6 +/- 4.5 years. We analysed prenatal individual case records of pregnant women, birth histories, neonatal development records and findings of histopathological examination of the placenta after birth. All patients received consultations with a gynaecologist-endocrinologist of the Surgut Clinical Perinatal Centre. Results. The most common complications were: mild and moderate anaemia, preeclampsia, chronic genitourinary infection, isthmicocervical insufficiency, urinary tract infections, placentomegaly, placental implantation abnormalities, polyhydramnios, oligohydramnios, haemodynamic disorders in the mother-placenta-fetus system. Almost one third of patients had a compromised familial history of diabetes mellitus. 98% of women followed nutritional recommendations and 81% self-monitored their blood glucose levels using glucose meters, which significantly improved pregnancy outcomes. Insulin therapy was administered in 25% of patients. Term labour occurred in 79% of patients, preterm labour - in 13%, post-term - in 8%. Caesarean delivery was done in 66% of women. The proportion of newborns with the signs of asphyxia was 7%. 10% of newborns had birth weight >=4000 g, whereas 25% of newborns were large for gestational age. Conclusion. An important role in management of pregnancy complicated by gestational diabetes is played by the patient motivation to follow recommendations on lifestyle changes and nutrition. Early treatment of gestational diabetes mellitus permits to reduce the likelihood of both a complicated course of pregnancy and undesirable perinatal complications.Copyright (c) 2020, Dynasty Publishing House.
