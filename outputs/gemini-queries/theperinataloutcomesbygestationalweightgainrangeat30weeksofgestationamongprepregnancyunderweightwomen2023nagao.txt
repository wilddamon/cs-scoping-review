The underweight pregnant women (UPW; n = 3643) were divided into quartile groups based on the weight gain at 30 weeks of gestation: group Q1 <= 5.7 kg, 5.7 kg < Q2 <= 7.2 kg, 7.2 kg < Q3 <= 8.8 kg, and 8.8 kg < Q4. Multivariable analysis revealed that Q1 was significantly associated with preterm births (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] = 1.6; 95% confidence interval [CI] = 1.0-2.3), and Q1 and Q2 were significantly associated with SGA (adj. OR = 3.0; 95% CI = 2.2-4.3; adj. OR = 1.7; 95% CI = 1.2-2.5, respectively).
