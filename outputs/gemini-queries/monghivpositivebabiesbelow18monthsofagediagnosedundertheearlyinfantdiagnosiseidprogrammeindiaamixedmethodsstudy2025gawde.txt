Quantitative clinical data of 310 children on ART with HIV diagnosis before 18 months of age was collected from the treatment cards across 30 ART centres from 11 states of India using a retrospective cohort design. Finding(s): Out of 310 infants, 200 (64.5%) were on ART, and 36 (11.6%) were lost to follow-up, and 25 (8.1%) had died. Interpretation(s): Overall survival was good for those who continued on treatment, but mortality was high for those with poor immunological and clinical parameters.
