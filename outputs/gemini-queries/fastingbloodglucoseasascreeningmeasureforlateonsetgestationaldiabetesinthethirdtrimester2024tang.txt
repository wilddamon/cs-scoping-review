"Objective: To investigate the positive rate of late-onset gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) by additional fasting blood glucose (FBG) screening at 32-34 gestational weeks (GW) and analyse the perinatal outcomes of late-onset GDM after standard treatment. Design: An Prospective cohort study. Setting: Single centre in China. Population: 1130 singleton pregnancies with negative GDM screening in their first and second trimester. Methods: Additional FBG testing was performed at 32-34 GW. Pregnancies with FBG >=5.1 mmol/L were diagnosed as GDM and received standardized treatment. Perinatal outcomes were collected and compared.
