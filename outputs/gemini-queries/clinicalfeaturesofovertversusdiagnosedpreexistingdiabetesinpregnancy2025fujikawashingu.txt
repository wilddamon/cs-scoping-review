"Overt diabetes in pregnancy (ODP) was originally included in gestational diabetes mellitus, which was positioned between normal glucose tolerance and pre-existing diabetes mellitus (PDM) in perinatal risk. This retrospective study aimed to clarify the differences between women with ODP and those with PDM. Compared with women with PDM (n = 162), those with ODP (n = 76) had higher HbA1c levels especially in the first trimester (8.2 +- 1.5% vs 7.2 +- 1.5%; P < 0.001).
