"Aim: We carried this study to compare readymade 0.75% hyperbaric ropivacaine vs conventional 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine for elective C-sections. Method(s): Our study included 60 parturients aged 18-30 years with ASA grades II who were scheduled for caesarean section. The parturients were randomly split into two groups of 30 each. Parturients in Group B received 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine 2ml (10mg), whereas those in Group R received 0.75% hyperbaric ropivacaine 2ml (10mg) (15mg). Result(s): The onset of motor anaesthesia was quantified in seconds as the achievement of Bromage scale II. Group B had a time of 97.5+/-33.82 and Group R had a time of 102.63+/-23.18 seconds. Two segment regressionwas quicker in Group R than in Group B. Conclusion(s): In caesarean sections, hyperbaric ropivacaine is an acceptable substitute for hyperbaric bupivacaine.
