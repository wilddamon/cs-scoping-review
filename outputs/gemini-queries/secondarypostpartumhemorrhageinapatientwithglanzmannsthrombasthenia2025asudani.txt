This case report aims to describe the management of a patient with GT who experienced secondary postpartum hemorrhage (PPH) after a cesarean section, highlighting the multidisciplinary approach required for such high-risk cases. She was discharged after 13 days with stable hematologic values and prescribed oral tranexamic acid and depot medroxyprogesterone. Conclusion(s): This case highlights the challenges in managing secondary PPH in a patient with GT. Clinical significance: Glanzmann's thrombasthenia presents significant obstetric challenges, particularly during the postpartum period.
