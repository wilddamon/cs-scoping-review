"Background: The transversus abdominis plane (TAP) block is a regional anesthesia technique which provides analgesia to the parietal peritoneum and also the skin and muscles of the anterior abdominal wall. Methodology: The study was conducted on pregnant women who were posted for elective/emergency caesarean section in hospital. 60 patients were included in the study based on the inclusion and exclusion criteria. Patients were divided into two groups. Group A received TAP Block with 0.25% Bupivacaine 20 ml each side whereas Group B received TAP Block with 0.25% Ropivacaine 20 ml each side.
