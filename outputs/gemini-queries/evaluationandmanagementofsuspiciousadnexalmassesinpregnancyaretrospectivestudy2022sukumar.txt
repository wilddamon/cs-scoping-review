"Introduction: The incidence of suspicious ovarian masses in pregnancy is on the rise due to the increased use of ultrasonography in recent times. However little is known about their management during pregnancy due to the lack of large randomised trials. Aim(s): To evaluate the incidence, nature and management of suspicious adnexal masses diagnosed during pregnancy at our institution. Material(s) and Method(s): This was a retrospective study of 33 pregnant patients who presented with suspicious ovarian masses at our hospital from August 2018 to September 2020. Patients were evaluated with imaging studies as per the International Ovarian Tumour Analysis (IOTA) ultrasound rules along with MRI and tumour markers and surgical intervention were performed for cases with definite indications. Result(s): Mean age of the patients was 26.1 years (20-33 years). Most common gestational age of presentation was the 2nd trimester of pregnancy (mean-18.9 weeks). Surgical intervention was done for 18 cases (54.55%) and 15 (45.45%) patients were kept under observation. Out of 33 patients, 27 were benign (81.82%) and 6 (18.18%) malignant. Five were primary ovarian and one was metastatic from carcinoma stomach.
