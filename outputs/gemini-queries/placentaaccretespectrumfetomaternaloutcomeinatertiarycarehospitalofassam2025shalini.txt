Objective(s): The objective of this study was to ascertain the clinical presentation and evaluate maternal and fetal outcome in cases of Placenta Accreta Spectrum managed at a tertiary care hospital. Method(s): This prospective observational study included all patients presenting to the Antenatal Outpatient Department, Emergency Labour Room, High-Risk Pregnancy Room and Intensive care unit.
