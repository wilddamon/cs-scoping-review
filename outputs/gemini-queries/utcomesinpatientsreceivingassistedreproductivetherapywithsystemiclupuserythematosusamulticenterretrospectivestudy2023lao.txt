"Objectives: To evaluate the safety, efficacy, and maternal and fetal outcomes of assisted reproductive therapy (ART) in systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE). Results: A total of 322 ART cycles in 142 women were analyzed. Sixty-six intrauterine pregnancies out of 72 clinical pregnancies yielded 65 live infants, including 5 pairs of twins. Spontaneous premature delivery (9/21, 42.9%) and preterm premature rupture of membranes (PPROM) (6/21, 28.6%) were the leading causes for premature delivery.
