"Objective: The objective was to study the prevalence of sickle cell anaemia, its complication during pregnancy and its fetal outcome in all the women attending AVBRH OPD from may 2013 to September 2014. Methods: it was a case control study. all women were screened for sickle cell anaemia and the complications of the women with sickle cell disease and sickle cell trait were analysed and their fetal outcome was studied. Results: Out of all cases, 90% were sickle cell trait (AS) and 10 % were sickle cell anaemia (SS). According to this study, 80% of SS and 11.11 % of AS had preterm labour. Conclusion: Complications of sickle cell disease precipitate the onset of preterm delivery and more caesarean sections were carried out to improve pregnancy outcome.
