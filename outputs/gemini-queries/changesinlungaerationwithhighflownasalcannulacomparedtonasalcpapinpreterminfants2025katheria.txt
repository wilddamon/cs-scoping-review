"Objective: To compare the degree of atelectasis in preterm infants on nasal continuous airway pressure (nCPAP) versus high-flow nasal cannula (HFNC) at 8 L/min. Study Design: A cross-over study of infants <29 weeks gestational age (GA) receiving nCPAP and underwent 6-hours of HFNC at 8 L/min before returning to nCPAP. Electrical Impedance Tomography was used to measure lung aeration. Results: 78 infants with median GA of 27 weeks [26, 28] were studied. HFNC was non-inferior to CPAP across the 4 periods (overall mean 2.1 +- 2.9, lower bound of confidence interval -0.9). Conclusion: This study of premature infants <29 weeks GA at birth demonstrated that HFNC was non-inferior to nCPAP as measured by DSS.
