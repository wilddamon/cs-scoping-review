"Objective: Intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP) is associated with an elevated risk of adverse perinatal outcomes, including perinatal morbidity and mortality. Methods: A total of 127 Chinese women with ICP and 55 healthy pregnant women were enrolled in our retrospective study. TCA-3-S in the urinary bile acids had a strong positive correlation with the aspartate aminotransferase (AST) level (r = 0.617, p < 0.05). Conclusions: Maternal urinary bile acid profiles were prominent for the prognosis of maternal and neonatal outcomes of ICP.
