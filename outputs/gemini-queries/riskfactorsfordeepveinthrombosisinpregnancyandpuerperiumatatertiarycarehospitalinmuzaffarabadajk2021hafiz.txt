Study Design: Descriptive Cross-sectional study Place and duration of study: Departments of Obs & Gynae and General Medicine, CMH Muzaffarabad, AJK from 20th December, 2019 to 20th June, 2020. Methodology: A total of 385 females were included. Demographic data including age, parity, gravidity, BMI, obesity and timing of DVT occurrence was noted.
