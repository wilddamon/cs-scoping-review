METHODS: In this retrospective cohort study, 392 women with RIF (no pregnancy after two or more transfers of good quality blastocysts) and 119 women with RPL (two or more subsequent miscarriages) who underwent endometrial biopsy with CD138 immunohistochemistry between 2016 and 2024 were analyzed.
