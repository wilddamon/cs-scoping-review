"""abstract ID: effectofuterinearteryembolizationonendometriumandrepregnancy2023wang
"Objective: To explore the effect of uterine artery embolization on endometrium and re-pregnancy. Methods: The clinical data of 80 cesarean section scar site pregnancy (CSP) patients admitted to the gynecological department of Zhuozhou City Hospital from September 2018 to February 2020 were divided into study group and control group according to different treatment plans, with 40 cases in each group. The control group used ultrasound guided uterine clearance, and the study group used ultrasound guided uterine clearance combined with uterine artery embolization (uterine arterial chemo embolization, UAE). After 12 months of follow-up, the correlation between lower endometrial artery RI and blood-human villous gonadotropin (b-hCG) in the two groups was explored, and then the scar pattern, menstrual recurrence and pregnancy were explored. Results: All patients with CSP had successful surgery and preserved reproductive function. There was no significant difference in the RI and b-hCG values before treatment (P> 0.05), the study group was higher than the control group, and b-hCG was lower than the control group, with a statistically significant difference (P <0.05). Peason Line correlation analysis showed that RI and b-hCG were positively associated in both groups (r =0.876, P <0.05). After 12 months of follow-up, the scar length, width and depth in the study group were smaller than those of the control group, which was statistically significant (P <0.05). There was no significant difference in the previous menstrual cycles between the two groups (P> 0.05), and the menstrual recovery time in the study group was longer than that of the control group (P <0.05). The natural pregnancy rate of the study group was lower than the control group, and the abortion rate was higher than the control group. There was no significant difference in the proportion of term live birth and preterm birth in the two groups (P> 0.05). Conclusion: The pregnancy patients in the scar area of cesarean section can be judged by the RI value of the uterine artery embolization needs to grasp the changes of endometrium and reproductive function, and take corresponding measures to reduce the damage caused by treatment.
