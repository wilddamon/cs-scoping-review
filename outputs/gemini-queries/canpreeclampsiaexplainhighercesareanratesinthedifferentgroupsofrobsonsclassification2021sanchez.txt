METHODS: A retrospective cross-sectional study including all women who delivered in a referral maternity hospital in southeast Brazil from January 2017 to February 2018. Women were classified into 1 of 10 Robson groups and then further subdivided into pre-eclampsia (PE) and non-PE (NPE) groups. Frequency of cesarean was determined for each group and compared by using kh(2) and prevalence ratio. RESULTS: Overall, 3102 women were included, of whom 1578 (50.9%) delivered by cesarean. CONCLUSION: Pre-eclampsia was associated with a higher occurrence of cesarean delivery in some Robson groups.
