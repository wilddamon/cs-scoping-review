METHODS: Data of all deliveries (n=446536) in Estonia from 1992 to 2023 were obtained from the Estonian Medical Birth Registry. During the study period, 73960 births ended in CS. CONCLUSIONS: The increase in CS occurred primarily due to the increase in CS rates among nulliparous women with a singleton pregnancy at term.
