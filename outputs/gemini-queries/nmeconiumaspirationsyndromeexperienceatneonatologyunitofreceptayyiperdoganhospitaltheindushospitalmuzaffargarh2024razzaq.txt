"Objective: To compare the efficacy of Curosurf vs Survanta surfactant in Meconium Aspiration Syndrome (MAS). Study Design: Cross section study. Place and Duration of Study: Neonatology Department of Recep Tayyip Erdogan, Muzaffar Garh. All those newborns diagnosed with meconium aspiration syndrome from 1st July 2016 till 30th June 2021. Material(s) and Method(s): All neonates with MAS having gestational age more than 35 weeks and required FiO2 40% were included in the study.
