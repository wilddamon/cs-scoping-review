The authors evaluated the prevalence of cephalhematomas and craniosynostosis at their institution and reviewed cases where both coexisted. METHODS: The authors retrospectively reviewed craniosynostosis cases treated at a large children's hospital from November 2007 through September 2021. Epic SlicerDicer was used to identify instances of coexisting ossified cephalhematomas. RESULTS: Eight of 75 patients with ossified cephalhematomas had coexisting craniosynostosis.
