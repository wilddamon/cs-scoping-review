Material(s) and Method(s): After written informed consent, study included 60 patients of ASA I, II, aged >20 years, posted for cesarean section under spinal anaesthesia. Result(s): The time for onset, time to peak and time to duration of sensory blockade were significantly lesser in group B. Conclusion(s): It is concluded that Levobupivacaine had prolonged sensory blockade, prolonged analgesia, and earlier regression of motor blockade, stable hemodynamic and decreased incidence of adverse effect.
