The purpose of this study is to analysis signal of caesarean section and audit the rate by using Robsons Criteria. Material(s) and Method(s): A Retrospective analytical study was conducted at the department of obstetrics and gynecology at Arokya Womens Centre, Salem, Tamil Nadu, and South India. Result(s): Out of 3156 institutional deliveries, LSCS was 542 and 2614 was labour natural. The incidence of caesarean section rate was 17.1%.Primary caesarean section was 253.
