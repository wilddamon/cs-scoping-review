"OBJECTIVE: To identify maternal and clinical characteristics associated with sulprostone failure in the management of postpartum haemorrhage (PPH) following vaginal delivery. DESIGN: Retrospective cohort study using Bayesian multivariable logistic regression. SETTING: Two French university hospitals. RESULTS: Among 217 women treated with sulprostone, the failure rate was 25.8 % (56/217). A higher shock index at sulprostone administration was also associated with failure (ORa = 2.29, 95 % CrI: 1.31-4.07; Pr(OR > 1) = 0.99). CONCLUSIONS: Sulprostone failure was associated with specific maternal and haemodynamic factors. Improved identification of high-risk cases may help optimise treatment pathways and reduce the need for invasive procedures.
