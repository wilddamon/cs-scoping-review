"Objective: To compare the effect of 7.5mg versus 10mg of 0.75% hyperbaric bupivacaine in spinal anaesthesia for caesarean section. Study Design: Randomized controlled trial. Material and Methods: One hundred and thirty patients fulfilling the inclusion/exclusion criteria were included in this study and they were divided into two groups.
