METHODS: In this prospective cohort study, the association between categorical variables was assessed by kh(2) tests, temporal trends in the monthly percentage change of SSI were measured using the Joinpoint Regression Program v4.7.0.0. RESULTS: In all, 1682 women (mean age 33.1 +- 5.2 years) underwent either elective (53.9%) or emergency (46.1%) cesarean section.
