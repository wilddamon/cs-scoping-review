The patients were divided into the surgery group and the UAE combined with MTX group according to the treatment they received. RESULTS: The patients' clinical characteristics did not differ between the surgery group and the UAE combined with MTX group. Although the treatment success rate did not differ significantly between the two groups, the success rate was 100% for the surgery group and 73% for the UAE combined with MTX group.
