Understanding its prevalence among first-time mothers can guide better screening strategies and healthcare interventions. Objective(s): This study aimed to determine the prevalence of GDM among first-time mothers and identify associated demographic and clinical risk factors. Method(s): A cross-sectional analysis was conducted involving 160 first-time pregnant women at a tertiary care hospital. Participants were screened for GDM using the Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT) between 24 and 28 weeks of gestation.
