This study compares the efficacy of the sensitivity and specificity of DIPSI OGTT as against gold standard IADPSG OGTT criteria. Material and MethodA prospective Diagnostic Evaluation Study carried out on 450 Antenatal patients at a zonal hospital in Eastern India.All participants were subjected to OGTT test by both IADPSG& DIPSI criteria on two separate occasions 7 days apart in the 1st trimester as well as between 24 to 28 weeks.DIPSI results were compared between 75 patients who were positive & 75 patients who were negative by IADPSG criteria.
