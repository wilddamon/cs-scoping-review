We examined the association between maternal MAT, infants' pharmacologic treatment, and hospital utilization at birth. Results: We included N = 146 mother-baby dyads from six hospitals (74% match rate). Among matched dyads, 51% received maternal MAT, 60% pharmacotherapy (37% both).
