Method(s): The present study was conducted in the Department of obstetrics and Gynaecology, Gauri Devi Institute of Medical Science & Hospital, Durgapur, West Bengal, India for one year . A total of 200 patients aged between 18 to 45 years of ASA grade II who underwent elective or emergency caesarean section were selected for the study and randomly allocated into either group. Result(s): The demographic profile i.e. mean age, weight, height and BMI were comparable in both the groups. The demographic profile was comparable in both the groups. Backache, vertigo, nausea and vomiting each had an incidence of 1% in the fentanyl group as compared to 3% cases of backache, nausea and vomiting in control group. Pruritus was not reported in either of the groups. Conclusion(s): We concluded that the incidence and severity of post dural puncture headache (PDPH) was decreased with intrathecal fentanyl (25microg) in caesarean section in a non-significant manner.
