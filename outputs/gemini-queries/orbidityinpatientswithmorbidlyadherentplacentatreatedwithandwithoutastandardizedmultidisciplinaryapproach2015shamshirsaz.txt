"OBJECTIVE: The purpose of this study was to test the hypothesis that a standardized multidisciplinary treatment approach in patients with morbidly adherent placenta, which includes accreta, increta, and percreta, is associated with less maternal morbidity than when such an approach is not used (nonmultidisciplinary approach).;STUDY DESIGN: A retrospective cohort study was conducted with patients from 3 tertiary care hospitals from July 2000 to September 2013.
