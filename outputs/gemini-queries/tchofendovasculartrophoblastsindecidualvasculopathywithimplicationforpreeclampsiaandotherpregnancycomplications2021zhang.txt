"Background This study aims to investigate decidual vasculopathy in preeclampsia at term and its relationship to spiral artery remodeling at implantation sites. Study design: Implantations sites with endovascular trophoblasts were reviewed from abortions with immunostaining for CD56. Results: Endovascular trophoblasts were phenotypically switched to express CD56 upon entering the spiral artery at implantation. CD56 expression is identified in classic decidual vasculopathy at term with or without preeclampsia.
