MATERIALS AND METHODS: The cases of four infertile women with severe Asherman syndrome were analyzed retrospectively. The comprehensive therapeutic plan for the four women included (1) preoperative office hysteroscopy to confirm the diagnosis and evaluate the severity of disease; (2) the use of ultrasound-guided intraoperative abdominal procedures during the surgical procedure, including hysteroscopic adhesiolysis to ensure the entire the hysteroscopic dissection, and placement of a Hyalobarrier((r)) gel and an intrauterine balloon catheter at the end of the surgery; (3) postoperative oral estrogen supplementation to enhance endometrial proliferation, removal of the balloon catheter, and a second-look office hysteroscopy; and (4) in vitro fertilization and embryo transfer (IVF & ET) for three of the four patients.
