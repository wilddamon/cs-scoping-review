"This work studies the concentrations of proteins in human milk (HM) at different lactation periods and explores the influence of maternal-infant factors on the dynamics of HM proteins in a Chinese cohort. The cohort contained 153 mother-infant pairs, and 12 main proteins of 376 HM samples were analyzed.
