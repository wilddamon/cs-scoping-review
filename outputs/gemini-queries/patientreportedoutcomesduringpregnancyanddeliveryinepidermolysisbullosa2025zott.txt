"BACKGROUND: Research on pregnancy in women with epidermolysis bullosa (EB) and its effect on maternal disease activity is limited. OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to retrospectively characterise the spectrum and course of patient-reported symptoms and complications during pregnancy referring to the EB House Austria patient cohort. METHODS: Data on pregnancy outcomes, clinical symptoms, and EB disease activity before, during, and after pregnancy were collected using a structured questionnaire. Most reported parameters, including EB lesional burden, remained stable during pregnancy. Caesarean section rates increased with EB severity (0% in mild, 38% in moderate, and 50% in severe EB groups). Severe perineal tears were the most common perinatal complication, occurring in 15% of pregnancies and primarily in the moderate and severe cohorts.
