"Background: More than one-third of maternal deaths, half of stillbirths, and approximately a quarter of neonatal deaths result from delivery complications. To study the effect of WHO labor care guide on cesarean section rates in women delivering at OBGY department of medical college hospital of central India. Material(s) and Method(s): The present retrospective record based observational study was conducted in Obstetrics &gynaecology department at People's College of Medical Science and Research Centre. WHO labour care guide was used for monitoring of labour on study group and Modified Partograph was used on control group.The data was recorded and statistical assessed using SPSS software. Result(s): The caesarean delivery rate was 5.0% in study group where as 20.4% in the control group that was statistically significant.Duration of active phase of labour was found to be significantly short in the study group and statistically significant. Complications, duration of stay in days and oxytocin used, normal delivery show statistically non significant results when both groups were compared. Birth weight, Apgar score, live birth, still birth, NICU admission, NICU stay, Neonatal outcome discharged show statistically non significant results when both groups were compared. Conclusion(s): The present study concluded that next generation partograph, that is, WHO labour care guide is a simple labour monitoring tool that can reduce primary caesarean delivery rate and helps in better monitoring of labour.Copyright (c)2025Int.
