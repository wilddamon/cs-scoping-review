"Background: Pregnancy is an exceptional condition for a woman who will become a mother. Objective(s): To determine the differencein the prevalence of depression through the results of the EPDS examination before and after delivery in high-risk pregnant women at Unair Hospital. Method(s): This research will use an observational analytic study design with a one-group pretest-posttest design strategy-data retrieval using primary and secondary data, namely by questionnaires and medical records at Unair Hospital.
