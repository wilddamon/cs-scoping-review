"Objective: To evaluate whether the left myocardial performance index (MPI) changes in association with the fetal heart rate (FHR) tracing during labor. Study design: Women with term, singleton pregnancies, in labor were recruited to this prospective cohort study. Primary outcome: difference in left MPI between Category of fetal heart rate tracings. Secondary outcomes: differences in left MPI by FHR characteristics. Participants underwent ultrasound examination, during which fetal MPI was measured. Results: Twenty-four laboring patients were recruited. There were 13 patients with Category I FHR, 11 patients with Category II FHR, and 0 patients with Category III FHR.
