Method(s): This prospective, cross-sectional research was carried out in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology unit 1, Dr Ruth KM Pfau Civil Hospital Karachi from March 2024 to May 2024. The study involved 100 participants they were divided into two groups, fasting group with minimum 10 days of fasting (Group-I) and non-fasting group (Group-II).
