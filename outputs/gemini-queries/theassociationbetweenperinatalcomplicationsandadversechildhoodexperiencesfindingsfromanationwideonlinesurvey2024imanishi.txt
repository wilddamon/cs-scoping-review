Conclusion Maternal ACEs were associated with the risk of perinatal complications such as preterm birth, PIH, and PROM. Key Points Maternal ACEs are linked to higher risks of preterm birth, PIH, and PROM. Parental divorce, emotional abuse, and neglect were the most common ACEs among Japanese mothers.
