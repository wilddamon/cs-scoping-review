"Aim: Breast milk should be given alone for the first six months and after six months with supplementary food for at least two years. Material(s) and Method(s): This cross-sectional analytical study was conducted on 401 women. Fathers with higher levels of education were more positive and supportive of breastfeeding than the fathers with lower education levels (p=0.028).
