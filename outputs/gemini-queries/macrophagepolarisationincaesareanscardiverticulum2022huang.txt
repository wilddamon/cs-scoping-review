"Aims: To determine immunohistochemical features and correlations between M1/M2 polarisation status with disease severity of post-caesarean scar diverticulum (CSD). Methods: Histological and immunohistological stainings were performed and inflammatory (CD16, CD163 and tumour necrosis factor-a (TNF-a)), fibrosis (a-smooth muscle actin (a-SMA)) and angiogenic (CD31) markers were examined in uterine tissues collected from patients with uterine scar diverticula (CSD) (n=37) and caesarean section (CS) (n=3). Results: CSD tissues have higher expression of a-SMA, TNF-a, CD16 and CD31 and lower expression of CD163 than CS tissue (p<0.05). A more significant difference was observed between the expression of CD16/CD163 and a-SMA (R=0.556, p<0.001), TNF-a (R=0.633, p<0.0001) and CD31 (R=0.336, p<0.05). Conclusions: In this study, TNF-a, a-SMA, CD16 and CD31 proteins were overexpressed in all CSD cases, and CD16/CD163 was positively correlated with tissue inflammation, fibrosis and neovascularisation.
