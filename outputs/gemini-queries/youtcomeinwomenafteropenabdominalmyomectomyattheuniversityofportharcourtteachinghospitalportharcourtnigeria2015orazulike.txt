There are controversies regarding fertility outcome following open abdominal myomectomy. Objective: To assess fertility outcome after open abdominal myomectomy in women with previous infertility. Design: A retrospective study. Setting: University of Port Harcourt Teaching Hospital (UPTH), Port Harcourt, Nigeria. Subjects: Women who wished to conceive after open abdominal myomectomy and had only subserous and/or intramural fibroids at surgery. Results: Over the five-year period under review, there were 301 abdominal myomectomies performed. One hundred and twenty case notes were retrieved giving a retrieval rate of 39.9%.
