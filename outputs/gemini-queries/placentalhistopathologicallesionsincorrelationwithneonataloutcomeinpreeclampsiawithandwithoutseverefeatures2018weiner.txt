"OBJECTIVE: We aimed to compare pregnancy outcome and placental histopathology in women with preeclampsia (PE) with and without severe features.;METHODS: The medical records and placental pathology reports of all pregnancies complicated by PE during 2008-2016, were reviewed. Placental lesions were classified to maternal/fetal vascular supply lesions, and maternal/fetal inflammatory responses. Small for gestational age (SGA) was defined as neonatal birth-weight <=10th%. Higher rates of SGA (p=0.016), and composite adverse neonatal outcome (p=0.002) characterized the severe PE group.
