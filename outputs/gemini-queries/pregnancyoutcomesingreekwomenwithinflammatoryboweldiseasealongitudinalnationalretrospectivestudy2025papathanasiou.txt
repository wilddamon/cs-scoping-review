RESULTS IN TOTAL: 223 pregnancies in 175 IBD patients [122 Crohn's disease (CD)] were included. Mean age at diagnosis was 26 years (12-44) with a mean duration of 7.4 (0-23). Forty-nine cases (22%) of IBD flares were recorded: Two-thirds ( n = 30) were in clinical remission at the onset of pregnancy, whereas treatment with corticosteroids was required in 22 (45%). In 147 cases (67.1%), c-section was performed. Two late fetal deaths (0.9%) were reported, both in patients with persistently active disease. After delivery, 75 patients (34%) presented with a disease flare, associated with active disease at the beginning of pregnancy ( P < 0.001).
