Materials and methods: The research was a cross-sectional study that compared the unit cost of the caesarean section (c-section) between the hospital tariff, the INA-CBGs rate, the clinical pathway tariff, and the actual unit cost. Conclusion: The unit cost of c-section based on clinical pathway using the ABC method was lower than the INA-CBGs tariff.
