Methods This single-center observational study included 44 pregnant women scheduled for cesarean delivery under spinal anesthesia. The HFVI/ANI was measured for five minutes before LAI using a Root(r) monitor (Masimo Corp., Irvine, CA). The primary outcome measure was the mean HFVI/ANI before LAI during spinal anesthesia.
