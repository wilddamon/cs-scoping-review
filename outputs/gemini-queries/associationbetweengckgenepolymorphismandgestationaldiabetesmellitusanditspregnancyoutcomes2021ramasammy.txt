"Gestational diabetes mellitus is defined as glucose intolerance which is first recognized during the pregnancy. The prevalence of gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM), one of the most common pregnant complications, has increased by more than 30% within one or two decades in a number of countries including developing countries. Glucokinase enzyme plays a key role in the regulation of glucose homeostasis. Our study is aimed to explore the association of GCK gene polymorphism and its pregnancy outcomes in GDM. Methodology: 210 pregnant women with GDM and 209 pregnant women without GDM were selected for study based on The Diabetes In Pregnancy Study group India criteria (DIPSI). DNA extraction was done using kit (Helini Biomolecule SNP) and was analyzed by Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR). Pregnancy and fetal outcomes were collected from patient case sheet. Result(s): AA genotype and A allele are found to have higher risk of developing GDM. Pregnancy outcomes of respiratory syndrome were significant with GCK gene polymorphism. There was no association between cardio metabolic risk factor and GCK gene polymorphism. Conclusion(s): Pregnancy outcomes of respiratory syndrome were associated with GCK gene polymorphism and the polymorphism of GCK gene is associated with GDM. AA genotype and A allele were at higher risk of developing GDM. Cardio metabolic risk factor was not associated with GCK gene polymorphism.Copyright (c) 2021"""
