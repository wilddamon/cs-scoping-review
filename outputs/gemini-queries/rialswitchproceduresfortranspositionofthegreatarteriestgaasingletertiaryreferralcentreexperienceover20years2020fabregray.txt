"Repair of transposition of the great arteries usually involves an atrial switch or arterial switch operation, which can complicate physiological adaptation to the demands of pregnancy and adversely affect the fetus. We retrospectively compared outcomes of 48 completed pregnancies in 23 women with surgically corrected transposition of the great arteries (38 atrial switch/10 arterial switch operation) under joint cardiac-obstetric care in our tertiary referral clinic between 1997 and 2017. Most women delivered vaginally (85%). The pre-term delivery rate was high (atrial switch 39%; arterial switch operation 40%). Small for gestational age occurred in 56% of babies, significantly more in the atrial switch group (66%) than arterial switch operation (20%), p = 0.013.
