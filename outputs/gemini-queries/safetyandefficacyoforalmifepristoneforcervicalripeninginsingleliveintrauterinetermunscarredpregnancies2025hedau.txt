Aim(s): The present study aimed to assess the safety and efficacy of oral Mifepristone for cervical ripening in single-live intrauterine term unscarred pregnancies. Method(s): The present study assessed 200 single live-term intrauterine unscarred pregnancies. All were induced using oral mifepristone in 400 mg dose after assessment of BS.
