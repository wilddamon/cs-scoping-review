"Objectives: Manual uterine aspiration (MUA) is a currently underused management option for early pregnancy loss (EPL) in the emergency department (ED). The mean time to procedure was 5.1 hours for MUA and 23.1 hours for EVA (p=0.002). Conclusions: Integrating MUA in the ED has the potential to reduce health care resource utilization while improving patient care.
