"Aim: Age at delivery has increased in the past decades, leading to a continuous growth of the number of women planning a pregnancy after the age of forty. Material(s) and Method(s): This was a retrospective study conducted in the 1st Clinic of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Cluj- Napoca in a 3-year period.
