"Background Maternal clinical thyroid disorders can cause reproductive complications. However, the effects of mild thyroid dysfunctions are not yet well established. The aim was to evaluate the association of maternal thyroid function during the first half of pregnancy with birthweight and preterm delivery. Methods We analysed data on 2170 pregnant women and their children from a prospective population-based cohort study in four Spanish areas. Results An inverse association of fT<inf>4</inf> and TSH with birthweight was found, the former remaining when restricted to euthyroid women. High fT<inf>4</inf> levels were also associated with an increased risk of SGA [odds ratio, 95% confidence interval (CI) 1.28 (95% CI 1.08, 1.51)].
