"Objective: to evaluate fetal growth in pregnancies complicated by placenta accreta spectrum (PAS) and to compare fetal growth between cases stratified by ultrasound stage of PAS. Methods: This was a prospective multicenter cohort study of women diagnosed with PAS between January 2018 and December 2021. Fetal growth centiles at three timepoints with median gestational ages of 21 +- 1 weeks (interquartile range [IQR], 20 +- 1-22 +- 0 weeks), 28 +- 0 weeks (IQR, 27 +- 0-28 +- 5 weeks), and 33 +- 0 weeks (IQR, 32 +- 1-34 +- 0 weeks) and birth weight centiles were compared between cases and controls and between those with PAS stratified by ultrasound stage.
