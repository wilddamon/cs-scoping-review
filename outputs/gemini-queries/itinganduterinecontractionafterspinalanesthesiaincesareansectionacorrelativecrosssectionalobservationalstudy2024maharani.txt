Peripheral Perfusion Index (PPI) is a noninvasive monitoring tool using an oximeter and can be used to indicate a patient's Systemic Vascular Resistance (SVR). This study aimed to find the correlation between pre-anaesthesia PPI and total vasopressor administration, nausea and vomiting, and uterine contraction using Linear Analogue Scale (LAS) after spinal anaesthesia for caesarean section. Results: The results of this study showed that PPI had a strong positive correlation (r = 0.795) with total vasopressor administration, a moderate positive correlation (r = 0.506) with nausea and vomiting, and a moderate negative correlation (r = 0.574) with LAS. The AUC analysis revealed an excellent hypotension prediction with a cut-off value of 3.5 (AUC 0.868; CI 95%). Conclusion: We conclude that PPI can be used as a predictor of vasopressor administration, nausea and vomiting, and uterine contractions. (c) 2024"""
