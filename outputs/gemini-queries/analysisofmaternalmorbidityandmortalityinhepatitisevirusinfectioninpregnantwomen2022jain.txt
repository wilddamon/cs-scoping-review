Method(s): A prospective study was conducted over a period of 1 year in a tertiary care hospital of Delhi. Result(s): The mean age of women was 23.5year.The mean gestational age of women was 32 week. Mean gravidity was of the total 48 pregnant women; 38 delivered and 10 undelivered. All these patients had hepatic encephalopathy at the time of admission. 34 babies were born alive, among them 20 (41.6%) were shifted to newborn intensive care unit (NICU). Conclusion(s): Maternal prognosis is poor in HEV positive patient particularly in postnatal period.
