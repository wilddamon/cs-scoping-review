This study aimed to assess the maternal mortality ratio in one of the largest hospitals in Baghdad over eight years including the time of the threat of the so-called Islamic State of Syria and Iraq. Material(s) and Method(s): A cross-sectional study was undertaken by reviewing the records of mothers who passed away in the hospital from February 2011 to February 2018. Conclusion(s): The maternal mortality was high and obstetrical hemorrhage was the main cause of death.
