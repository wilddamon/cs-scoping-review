METHODS: A retrospective cohort study analyzed 1,896 parturients receiving LEA at Zhengzhou Central Hospital (2022-2023). Participants were categorized into vaginal delivery group (n = 1, 541) and cesarean delivery group (n = 355). The original dataset was randomly split into training/testing sets (7:3 ratio) for analytical purposes.
