Methods: Retrospective study, between 2009 and 2020 at the CHRU of Limoges, type 3 maternity hospital, on patients with a breech foetus at term. Neonatal morbidity was compared for planned vaginal delivery and planned cesarean delivery groups. Results: We included 923 patients.
