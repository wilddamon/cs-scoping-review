On the other hand, very little information is available on the outlook for children who are born very prematurely in Pakistan and other developing nations. Aim(s): In Pakistan throughout the course of the last decade, to discuss the rates of survival and serious illness among children who were delivered very prematurely. Method(s): This retrospective research of cohorts has been carried out beginning on October 2, 2020 and continuing through September 30, 2021. Babies with a gestational age of fewer than 32 weeks who had been released from two of Pakistan's 72 neonatal critical care units spread throughout the country's five provinces were part of the study. The period of time beginning in October 2020 and ending in September 2021 was covered by the data analysis. Result(s): Out of a total of 8565 newborns that have been included in the study, 5315 (64.3%) were male, and 120 infants (3%) were considered to be underweight for their gestational age.
