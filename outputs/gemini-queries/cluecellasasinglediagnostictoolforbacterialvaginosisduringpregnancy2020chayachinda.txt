"Objective: To demonstrate the diagnostic accuracy for bacterial vaginosis (BV) by using the sole detection of clue cells Material(s) and Method(s): All pregnant women who received antenatal care (ANC) at Siriraj Hospital between October 2017 and March 2018 were invited. Vaginal discharge was obtained, air-dried, gram-stained, and sent to two microbiologists. Nugent's scoring system, which was done by one microbiologist, was used as the diagnostic gold standard.
