Different factors have been found to increase the risk of developing postnatal depression including antepartum depression, psychiatric history, low self-esteem, stressful life events, and a lack of social support. Materials & Methods: It was a comparative cross-sectional study, conducted in Gynae department of Lady Reading Hospital Peshawar. The study was conducted from 10th April 2023 to 10th November 2023.
