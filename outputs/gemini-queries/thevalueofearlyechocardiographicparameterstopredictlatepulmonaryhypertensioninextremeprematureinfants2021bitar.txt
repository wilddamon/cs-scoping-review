Objective(s): To test the predictive ability of echocardiographic markers; pulmonary artery acceleration time to right ventricle ejection time ratio and eccentricity index for subsequent development of pulmonary hypertension in extreme premature infants. Method(s): This is a retrospective matched case control study of extreme premature infants <29 weeks gestational age, admitted to a level 3 neonatal intensive care unit between 2013 and 2017.
