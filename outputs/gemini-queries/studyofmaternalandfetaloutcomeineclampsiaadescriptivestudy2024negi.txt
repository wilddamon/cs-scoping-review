"Background: The aim is to study maternal and fetal outcome in eclampsia. Eclampsia is an acute and life-threatening complication of pregnancy and is a disease unique to pregnancy. It is characterised by preeclampsia complicated with appearance of generalised tonic-clonic convulsions with or without coma. It is an obstetric emergency associated with serious maternal and perinatal complications. Material(s) and Method(s): This is a descriptive study and has been conducted in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, tertiary care center.
