"Background: Ectopic gestation is a gynecological emergency which culminates in pregnancy loss and causes significant maternal morbidity, mortality besides jeopardizing future conception. Objective(s): To know the age group, parity, risk factors, clinical features, modalities of treatment employed and their efficacies, morbidity and mortality associated with ectopic pregnancy. Method(s): This retrospective study was conducted over a period of one year from 1st June 2021 to 30th June 2022 in the department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Sher-I-Kashmir Institute of Medical Sciences, MCH Srinagar during which a total of 80 cases were reported with ectopic pregnancy. Information was collected in a structured proforma, tabulated and descriptive analysis was carried out. The case sheets of patients with ectopic pregnancy were traced through hospital records mainly labour room registers and operation theatre registers. Result(s): Total number of deliveries conducted during study time frame either vaginally or through cesarean section were 3809 of which 80 (2.1%) were diagnosed as ectopic pregnancy. The mean age for this study was 27.5 +/- 5. The incidence of tubal ectopic was maximum (96.25) with ampulla being the most common site of ectopic implantation (73.75%). Majority of the cases were ruptured ectopic pregnancies (78.20%). Linear Salpingectomy was done in majority of women (82.5%). History of one or more risk factor was elicited in 72.5% of patients of which abdominopelvic surgeries was the commonest risk factor (21.25%). No mortality and acute renal failure were found in our study. Conclusion(s):: A high index of suspicion is needed in the diagnosis of ectopic pregnancy especially in women with high risk factors for overcoming the crisis with minimal invasive procedure to help in reducing morbidity, mortality and more importantly preserving fertility for those who desire it. Primary prevention such as improved access to family planning services, sex education, treatment of PID and STDs etc. ameliorate risk factors and hence reduce ectopic pregnancy.Copyright (c) 2022, Anka Publishers.
