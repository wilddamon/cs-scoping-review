These covered a total study cohort of more than 2.5 million people in eight countries: Australia, Brazil, Denmark, Finland, Germany, Sweden, Turkey and the UK. Conclusion: The statistically significant association between C-sections and ADHD in children can be partially explained by unmeasured confounding.
