"Background: Metabolic bone disease (MBD) is a morbidity of multifactorial etiology with a high incidence in very preterm infants. We planned to study the incidence of MBD after implementation of bone health focussed nutritional strategy (BNS) in those <30 weeks gestation at birth. Methods: This prospective cohort study including preterm newborns (<30 weeks) who received nutrition that incorporated (a) Early initiation of intravenous potassium phosphate; (b) Early enteral supplementation with multicomponent human milk fortifier at enteral feed tolerance of 40 mL/kg/day feeds itself; and (c) Weekly phosphorus measurements with optimization of enteral intakes. Results: Of the 67 included neonates receiving BNS, 20.9% were classified as MBD. There was a low rate of hyperphosphatemia (4.5%) and hyperkalemia (2.9%).
