The objective of this study was to evaluate obstetric outcomes in patients with a scarred uterus resulting from a cesarean delivery prior to 32 WG. MATERIALS AND METHODS: This was an observational, retrospective, single-center study conducted between January 1, 2011 and December 31, 2021 at the University Hospital of Rennes, France.
