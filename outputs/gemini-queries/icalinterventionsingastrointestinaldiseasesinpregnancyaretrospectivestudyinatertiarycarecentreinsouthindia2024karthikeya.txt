Material(s) and Method(s): Retrospective study done over a period of one year from January 2023 to December 2023 All pregnant women who underwent surgical intervention for gastrointestinal conditions were included. The mean age of 19 patients was 24 years +/-2 years and the mean gestational age was 15 weeks+/-2 weeks. Two patients were operated in the first trimester (10.5%), 17 patients in the second trimester. Laparoscopic surgery was performed in four patients (21.1%).
