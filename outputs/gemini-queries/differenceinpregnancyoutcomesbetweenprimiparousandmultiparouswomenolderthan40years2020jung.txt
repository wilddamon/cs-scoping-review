Material(s) and Method(s): This retrospective cohort study included women older than 40 years with singleton pregnancy, who delivered after 24 weeks of gestation. Result(s): This study included 432 women, with primiparous women (n=111) comprising a quarter among them. On analyzing multivariable logistic regression-Adjusted confounding factors, small for gestational age (SGA) neonates (OR, 2.525; 95% CI, 11.407 4.529) were associated with primiparous women. Conclusion(s): There are different pregnancy outcomes between primiparous and multiparous women.
