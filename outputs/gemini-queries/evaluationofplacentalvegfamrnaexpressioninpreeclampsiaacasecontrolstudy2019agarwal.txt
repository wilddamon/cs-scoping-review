"Objective: The aim of our case-control study was to determine expression of VEGFA mRNA in placentae of preeclamsia (PE) versus uncomplicated pregnancy to further clarify its differential expression in pregnancy hypertensive disorders. Result(s): Out of 42 PE cases, 23 (55%) were non-severe and 19 cases (45%) were severe PE. Conclusion(s): Our results showed a lowered VEGFA expression in PE placentae compared to uncomplicated controls.
