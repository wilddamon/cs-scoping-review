Aim of the Study: To identify heart disease in asymptomatic pregnant women with screening echocardiography. Material(s) and Method(s): This is a prospective study conducted on pregnant women attending AN OPD in the Department of obstetrics and Gynaecology at Govt. Rajaji Hospital, Madurai.
