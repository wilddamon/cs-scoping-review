Method(s): This observational, cross-sectional study enrolled preterm infants admitted to the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) over an 18-month period. Conclusion(s): This study highlights differences in thyroid hormone profiles between preterm SGA and AGA infants, with implications for growth and neurodevelopment.
