"Objective: This study aims to evaluate the effect of the work shift on the obstetrical outcome on a tertiary hospital. Materials and methods: We retrospectively reviewed the files of 1,622 parturient who delivered in our department from 01/01/2011 to 30/06/2011. Statistically significant differences were observed in the case of induction of labor (p < 0.01), mode of delivery (p = 0.024) and oxytocin usage duration (p = 0.014). Conclusions: The increased caesarean section and vacuum delivery rate during the morning shift are probably explained by the increased rates of labor induction.
