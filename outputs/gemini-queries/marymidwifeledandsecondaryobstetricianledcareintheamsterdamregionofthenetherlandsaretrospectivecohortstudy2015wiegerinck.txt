DESIGN: Retrospective cohort study. SETTING: Amsterdam region of the Netherlands. MEASUREMENTS: Analysis of linked data from the national perinatal register, and hospital- and midwifery record data. We assessed (unadjusted) relative risks with confidence intervals.
