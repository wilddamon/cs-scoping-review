"Background: Mothers' perceptions of high-quality hospital care may improve breastfeeding outcomes, yet postnatal care in hospitals is often rated poorly by mothers, highlighting the need to focus on the quality and maternal perceptions of care, not just its provision. Methods: A cross-sectional study was conducted at a Finnish maternity hospital in 2022-2023. Data were collected through an online survey of mothers within 12 weeks of childbirth.
