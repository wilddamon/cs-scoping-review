The current study provides a descriptive assessment of water availability, delivery methods, and quality in 96 public schools across four New Jersey school districts. Methods: As part of the New Jersey Child Health Study, we administered an online and paper survey to school nurses at 96 schools to evaluate the availability, delivery, and quality of water in the school cafeteria during lunchtime. Univariate and bivariate statistics were used to analyze the data. Results: In school year 2019-20, 83 (86.5%) schools reported providing free drinking water in the cafeteria during lunch. Issues related to water quality, specifically cleanliness, temperature, pressure, and taste, were present in 20-30% of schools.
