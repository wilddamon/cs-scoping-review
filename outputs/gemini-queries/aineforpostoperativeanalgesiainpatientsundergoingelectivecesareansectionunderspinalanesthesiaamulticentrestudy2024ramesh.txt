"Introduction: Postoperative pain is a miserable factor and can delays patient's recovery and mobilization. QLB, TAP Blocks are most frequently used nerve blocks for postoperative analgesia. These blocks reduce the requirement of opioids and other analgesics. In QL Block the local anesthetic agent extends beyond the transversus abdominis plane to the thoracic paravertebral area causes significant analgesia and prolongs the effect of analgesia. Aim(s): The study is aimed to compare the efficacy of ultrasound guided QL Block and TAP Block for providing post-operative analgesia in patients undergoing Cesarean section under spinal anesthesia. Material(s) and Method(s): This is a prospective multicenter clinical comparative study done at GGH Guntur, GGH Madanapalli. Studied on total number of 60 patients who were posted for elective cesarean section under spinal anesthesia. They are randomly divided into 2 equal groups QLB Group (n=30), TAP Group (n=30). Both groups received bilateral Ultrasound guided block with O.25% ropivacaine and they were monitored for MAP, HR, VAS, Time for first rescue analgesia, Total fentanyl requirement. Result(s): The MAP at 6th, 12th, 24th Post-operative period in TAP Group was significantly higher (97.67 +/- 6.42, 93.43 +/- 7.84, 95.16 +/- 7.32) than QLB Group (87.46 +/- 6.4, 86.42 +/- 8.8, 82 +/- 3.6) which was statistically highly significant P<0.001. The HR at 6th, 12th, 24th Post-operative period in TAP Group was significantly higher (78.42 +/- 8.8, 79.24 +/- 9.8, 79.36 +/- 9.2) than QLB Group (69.36 +/- 5.8, 71.24 +/- 4.6, 74.14 +/- 3.2) which was statistically highly significant P<0.001. The QLB group shows less VAS Score at 2nd, 4th and 6th postoperative period than TAP group which was statistically highly significant (P<0.001). The QLB group required 100 micrograms fentanyl, TAP group required 200 micrograms of fentanyl which was statistically highly significant (P<0.001). The time for first rescue analgesia in TAP Group was 7.36 +/- 1.6 hr. whereas in QLB Group it was 11.24 +/- 3.8 hr which was statistically highly significant (P<0.001). Conclusion(s): We concluded that, the analgesic efficacy of Ultrasound guided QL Block is better than TAP Block as it provides prolonged duration of analgesia, less VAS scoreless opioid consumption, prolonged time for first rescue analgesia in post-operative period.Copyright (c) 2024 Dr. Yashwant Research Labs Pvt.
