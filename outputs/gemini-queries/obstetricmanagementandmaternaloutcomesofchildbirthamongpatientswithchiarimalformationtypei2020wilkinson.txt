"BACKGROUND: A range of opinions exist in the literature regarding obstetric management of pregnant women with Chiari malformation type I (CM-I).;OBJECTIVE: To examine obstetrical practices and outcomes with childbirth of women with CM-I.;METHODS: We examined insurance claims data from a large, privately insured health care network and identified admissions for childbirth from 2004 to 2014.
