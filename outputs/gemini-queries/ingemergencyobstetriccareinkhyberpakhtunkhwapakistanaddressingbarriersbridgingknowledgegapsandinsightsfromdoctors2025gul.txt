Result(s): Significant disparities were observed in EmOC knowledge between consultants and WMOs, with consultants demonstrating superior understanding of EmOC population, complications, C-sections, and mortality, while WMOs excelled in the birth domain. Geographical challenges, such as working in remote areas, and institutional issues, including lack of political support, were also highlighted as barriers to effective EmOC. Conclusion(s): Our study reveals critical gaps in EmOC knowledge and performance among healthcare providers in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
