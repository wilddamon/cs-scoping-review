Meconium excretion occurs naturally within the first 24 to 48 hours after the birth, although the fetus may excrete meconium into the amniotic fluid for various reasons during pregnancy. Amniotic fluid impregnated with meconium before week 37 of gestation is uncommon, but its occurrence increases with gestational age.
