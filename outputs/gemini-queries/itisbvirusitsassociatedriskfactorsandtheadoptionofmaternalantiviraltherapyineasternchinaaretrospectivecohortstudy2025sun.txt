Our study aims to analyze the effectiveness of preventing MTCT of HBV and the associated risk factors. Methods: This retrospective cohort study comprised 16,223 pairs of hepatitis B surface antigen-positive mothers and infants from 2021 to 2023 in geographical regions replete with coverage by preventive measures.
