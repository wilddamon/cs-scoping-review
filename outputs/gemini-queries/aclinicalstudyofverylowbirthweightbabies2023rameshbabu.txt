The study of maternal factors influencing VLBW, use of antenatal corticosteroids &their influence on fetal outcome in terms of NICU admission, duration of stay in NICU &further neurodevelopment of babies is essential to reduce the morbidity & mortality among VLBW babies. Materials And Methods: This was a prospective study conducted on all live newborn infants with birth weight in range of 500 - 1500 gms who required immediate NICU admission after birth from October 2021 to September 2022 at Adichunchanagiri Institute Of Medical Sciences. All babies were admitted to NICU immediately after birth, 83% babies survived to discharge, 6% died, 11% got discharged against advice. Among NICU admission 12% babies stayed for <10 days,48% for 10-20 days & remaining 40% needed prolonged stay for >30 days.
