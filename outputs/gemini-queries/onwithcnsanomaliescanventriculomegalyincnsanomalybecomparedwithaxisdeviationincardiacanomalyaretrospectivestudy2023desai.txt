Material(s) and Method(s): Over a period of 5 years, all cases of CNS anomalies with or without ventriculomegaly with gestational age more than 14 weeks were reviewed for the size of lateral ventricles, other CNS findings, extra-CNS structural anomalies. Conclusion(s): From our study, we can conclude that ventriculomegaly does not predict the presence of structural CNS abnormality, and a normal size ventricle rules does not rule out the presence of structural CNS anomaly.
