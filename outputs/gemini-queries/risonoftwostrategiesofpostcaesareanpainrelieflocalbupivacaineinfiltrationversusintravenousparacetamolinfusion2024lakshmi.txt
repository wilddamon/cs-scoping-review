"Post-caesarean pain being moderate to severe in nature necessitates the requirement of optimal analgesia for early autonomy and mobility of mother to care the newborn. Pain relief can be provided either locally or systemically. 66 post-spinal caesarean parturients were randomized to 2 groups (B&P). At the time of wound closure, Group B received Local Infiltration with 20ml of Bupivacaine (0.25%) and Group P received IV infusion Paracetamol (1G stat) and continued 8th hourly. The first request for rescue analgesia in Group B was between 5-8 hrs in 29 patients (26 patients within 4hrs in Group P).
