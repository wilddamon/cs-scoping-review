"Background: Treatment of postpartum hemorrhage relies primarily on uterotonics, but early use of tranexamic acid has become part of several recommended algorithms. However, the role of intravenous tranexamic acid has not been effectively evaluated in reducing blood loss during caesarean section, hence the need for this study. Method(s): Patients with singleton pregnancy and gestational age >= 35 weeks were included in the study. Women were randomized into 2 groups-Group I and Group II, for two arm study with 75 subjects in each group. Preoperative haemoglobin was estimated in both the groups. 1 gram dose was chosen for this study. Tranexamic acid was not given to Group 1 while Group 2 were given tranexamic acid injection prepared by diluting 1 gram (10 ml) tranexamic acid in 100 ml of normal saline. Result(s): Tranexamic acid was found to effective in reducing blood loss in study group. Mean percentage fall in haemoglobin (gm/dl) in control group was 12.19 +/- 3.64 which was significantly higher as compared to tranexamic acid group (8.93 +/- 4.25) with p value of < 0.0001. Conclusion(s): Tranexamic acid was found to be safe and effective in reducing blood loss after lower segment caesarean section. In the present study, there was less drop in haemoglobin and haematocrit in tranexamic acid group. It was concluded that Tranexamic acid, plays a beneficial role in preventing postpartum hemorrhage and maternal mortality.Copyright (c) 2022 Japan University of Health Sciences."""
