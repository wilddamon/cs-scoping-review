"Refractory cardiogenic and vasoplegic shock after congenital heart surgery is a threatening condition leading to high morbidity and mortality. Control of hemodynamic and inflammatory response is fundamental in medical strategy. We report the case of a newborn with cardiogenic and vasoplegic shock secondary to cardiopulmonary bypass for atrioseptostomy and prostaglandin treatment in the context of hypoplastic left heart syndrome, successfully treated with a combination of mechanical circulatory support and cytokine hemoadsorption column (CytoSorb(r)). The use of CytoSorb(r) cartridge inserted in an extra-corporeal circuit even in a newborn is easy and feasible, as long as some precautions are considered.
