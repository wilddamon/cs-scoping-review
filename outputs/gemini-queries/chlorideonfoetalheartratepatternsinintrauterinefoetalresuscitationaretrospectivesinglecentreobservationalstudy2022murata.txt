"No consistent recommendations concerning the preferred tocolytic agents for intrauterine foetal resuscitation are available. We evaluated the effects of acute tocolysis (AT) using ritodrine hydrochloride on foetal heart rate (FHR) patterns and neonatal outcomes. We retrospectively analysed the data of patients undergoing emergency caesarean section because of non-reassuring foetal status indicated by foetal scalp electrodes. Patients were classified into AT (ritodrine hydrochloride approximately 500 ug/min) and control groups with 15 and 12 participants, respectively. FHR patterns, Apgar scores, umbilical arterial analysis, and neonatal admission were compared. All participants had FHR category II; decelerations disappeared in all foetuses in the AT group, with no significant difference in neonatal outcomes. The AT group had a higher baseline FHR and lower short-term FHR variability than the control group, indicating foetal autonomic responses.
