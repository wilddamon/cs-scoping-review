Material(s) and Method(s): The study was done in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Raichur Institute of Medical Sciences, Raichur. 140 patients with blood pressure >= 160/110 mm of hg with proteinuria or Blood pressure >= 140/90 mm of hg with proteinuria of >= 2+ were involved in the study. Result(s): Out of 140 women with severe preeclampsia, 66.4% had no complications, 20.7% had eclampsia and 15% had other complications (19.1% renal failure, abruption 10.6%, 8.5% HELLP, one each of PPH, cardiomyopathy and cerebral hemorrhage). Serum uric acid >7U/l was found to have significant correlation with eclampsia as well as other complications related to preeclampsia.
