However, the effect of PROM on EPIs remains contradictory. METHODS: Medical data of 470 EPIs at gestational age < 28weeks who received prenatal examination in our hospital between January 1, 2015 and December 31, 2020 were analyzed retrospectively. However, there was no significant difference in the in-hospital mortality and the incidence of other major complications between the two groups (all P > 0.05).
