Objective(s): Determining the pregnancy outcome in patients with borderline AFI is the study's goal. Study Design: A Descriptive Cross-Sectional Study Duration and place of study: From January 1, 2023, to August 1, 2023, research was carried out in the Department of Obs & Gyne MMC, Mardan Methods: The Mardan Medical Complex Obstetrics & Gynecology Department conducted a descriptive cross-sectional study.
