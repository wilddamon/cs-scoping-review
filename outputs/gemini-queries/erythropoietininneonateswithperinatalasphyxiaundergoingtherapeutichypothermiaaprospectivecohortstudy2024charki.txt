"Aims: To assess the safety and feasibility of erythropoietin (EPO) in asphyxiated neonates undergoing therapeutic hypothermia (TH). Subjects and methods: This study enrolled 60 neonates with HIE undergoing TH. 30 neonates were divided into EPO with TH group, where neonates received EPO (dose), at a dose rate of 1000 IU/kg/48 hours, for four doses and the remaining 30 neonates undergoing TH were into control group. Magnetic resonance imaging brain was undertaken between 10 and 14 days of life in surviving neonates. The Bayley Scales of Infant (BSI) Development IV was performed at regular intervals up to the age of 12 months. Result(s): Amplitude-integrated electroencephalogram (aEEG) showed burst suppression (19% vs. 11%), low voltage (10% vs. 4%), and flat trace (7% vs. 3%) in control group in comparison with EPO concurrent with TH group which was statistically significant. At 12 months, neurodevelopment outcomes in EPO with TH group neonates showed a favorable outcome.
