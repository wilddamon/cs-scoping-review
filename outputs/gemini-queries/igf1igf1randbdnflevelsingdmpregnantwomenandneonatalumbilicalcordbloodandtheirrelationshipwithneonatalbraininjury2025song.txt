"Objective To investigate the serum level of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1), IGF-1 receptor (IGF-1R), and brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) in pregnant women with gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) and neonatal umbilical cord blood, and their effects on neonatal brain injury. Methods A total of 87 GDM pregnant women hospitalized for delivery at Yan'an University Affiliated Hospital from September 2020 to September 2023 were selected as the observation group. Maternal serum IGF-1 and BDNF levels were lower, while IGF-1R levels were higher in the observation group than in the control group (P<0.05). Neonatal umbilical cord serum IGF-1 and BDNF levels were lower, and IGF-1R levels were higher in the observation group than in the control group (P<0.01). Conclusion Pregnant women's serum and neonatal umbilical cord serum levels of IGF-1 N BDNF and IGF-1R are correlated with neonatal brain injury.
