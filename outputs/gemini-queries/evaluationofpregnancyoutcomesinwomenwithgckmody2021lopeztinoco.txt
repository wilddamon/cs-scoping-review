"AIMS: To determine the fetal and maternal outcomes in pregnant women with Glucokinase-Maturity onset diabetes of the young (GCK-MODY).;METHODS: We studied the obstetric and perinatal outcomes in 99 pregnancies of 34 women with GCK-MODY.
