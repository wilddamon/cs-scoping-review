"Objective To evaluate fasting plasma glucose (FPG) as a screening test for gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) among Mexican adolescents using International Association of Diabetes and Pregnancy Study Groups criteria. Design Retrospective cohort study. Setting Level-three medical institution in Mexico City. Participants The study population comprised 1061 adolescent women aged 12-19 years with singleton pregnancies, who underwent a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) between 11 and 35 weeks of gestation. Primary and secondary outcome measures The sensitivity (Sn), specificity (Sp), positive and negative predictive values (PPV and NPV, respectively), and positive and negative likelihood ratios LR (+) and LR, respectively) with 95% CIs for selected FPG cut-off values were compared. Secondary measures were perinatal outcomes in women with and without GDM. Results GDM was present in 71 women (6.7%, 95% CI 5.3% to 8.4%). No significant differences in perinatal outcomes were found between adolescents with and without GDM. Conclusions An FPG cut-off of >=90 mg/dL (5.0 mmol/L) is ideal for GDM screening in Mexican adolescent women.
