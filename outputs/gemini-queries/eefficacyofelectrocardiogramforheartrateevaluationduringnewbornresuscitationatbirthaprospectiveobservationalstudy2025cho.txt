"Objective This study assessed the efficacy of electrocardiogram (ECG) compared to pulse oxime-try (PO) in detecting heart rate (HR) during high-risk newborn resuscitation. Methods A prospective observational study was performed with high-risk delivery cases to mea-sure the time required for HR detection. A conventional PO and a standard ECG monitor were used for HR assessment. Results Forty-one infants were analyzed in the study, and 11 needed resuscitation. The study population was divided according to gestational age (GA): 9 were GA <32 weeks, 28 were GA 32-35 weeks, and 4 were GA >=36 weeks. Time from ECG placement to HR detection (median, 30 seconds; interquartile range [IQR], 20-43.5 seconds) was significantly faster than that from PO placement (median, 125 seconds; IQR, 100-175 seconds; P<0.001).
