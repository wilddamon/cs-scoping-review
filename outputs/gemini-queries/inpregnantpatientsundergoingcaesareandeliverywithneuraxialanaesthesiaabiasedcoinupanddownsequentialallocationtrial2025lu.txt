This study aimed to determine the optimal dose (90% minimum effective concentration [MEC90]) of intraoperative oxycodone to prevent shivering during caesarean section. Methods: This study was designed by the biased-coin up-and-down method. We recruited pregnant women who underwent caesarean section under combined spinal-epidural anaesthesia.
