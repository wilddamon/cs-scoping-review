"Objective: Aim of this study was to evaluate the incidence, potential risk factors and the respective outcomes of pregnancies with placenta praevia.Methods: Data were prospectively collected from women diagnosed with placenta praevia in 10 Austrian hospitals in in the province of Styria between 1993 and 2012. We analyzed the incidence, potential risk factors and the respective outcomes of pregnancies with placenta praevia. Differences between women with major placenta praevia (complete or partial placenta praevia) and minor placenta praevia (marginal placenta praevia or low-lying placenta) were evaluated.Results: 328 patients with placenta praevia were identified.
