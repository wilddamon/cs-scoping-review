"BACKGROUND: It is vital to assess methods of reducing the most severe labor pain. Paracetamol infusion is an efficient therapy available to eliminate labor pain in the active phase. AIM: The present study aimed to assess the efficacy of intravenous infusion of 1000 mg paracetamol as an intrapartum labor analgesic. METHODS: In 220 nulliparous pregnant females undergoing vaginal delivery, after active labor phase onset, subjects were divided into two groups. Group I subjects were given 300cc normal saline and Group II subjects were given 300cc normal saline and 1 gram paracetamol. In two groups, Apgar scores, delivery type, duration of first and second labor stages, VAS scores, and maternal pain scores were assessed and compared. RESULTS: Mean first- and second-stage labor length was significantly lower in Group I compared to Group II with P = 0.001 and 0.037. Also, mean pain scores were significantly lower in paracetamol Group II compared to a control group with P < 0.05.
