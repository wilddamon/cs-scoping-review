Parturient in active labour requesting painless labour included in the study. All cases divided into two groups based on cervical dilatation(Group A: <=4cm Group B: > 4cm ) 50 parturient in each group and both groups received Bupivacaine 0.5% (3 ml) + fentanyl 20 mug and N.S to make up to 10 ml and top-up the same dose.
