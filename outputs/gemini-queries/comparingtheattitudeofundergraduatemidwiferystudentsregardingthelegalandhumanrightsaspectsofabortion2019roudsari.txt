"Objectives: This study was carried out to compare the attitude of first and final year undergraduate midwifery students about the legal and human rights aspects of abortion. Method(s): In this cross sectional study, all 82 midwifery students in their first and final year studying were included in the study through census method. Data collection tools was The Abortion Attitude Survey questionnaires. Data analysis was carried out by SPSS version 16 using the descriptive and inferential statistics tests at the significance level of P< 0/05. Result(s): The mean score of attitude towards abortion in the first year and final year students were 56.66 +/- 9.5 vs 64.37 +/- 6.28, (95% CI:-11.04,-4.28; p=0.0001).
