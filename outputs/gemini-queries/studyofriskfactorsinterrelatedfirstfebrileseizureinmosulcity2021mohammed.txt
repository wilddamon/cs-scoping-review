"Background: Febrile seizures are the most prevalent type of pediatric neurological problem that occurs among children of age 6 months-5 years. In this study, we aimed to determine the risk factors for the first episode of febrile seizure in the Mosul area and its surrounding regions. Method(s): This hospital based case-control study was conducted at Al-Khansaa Teaching Hospital from 1st December 2020 to 1st June 2021. Hundred and ten children aged 6-60 months were enrolled in the study. Half of them (n=55), were admitted to the hospital with a first episode of febrile seizure ( case group), were compared with another equal number (n=55) of children admitted to the hospital at the same time, suffering from febrile illnesses, but without seizure attack (control group) , regarding the possible risk factors for the febrile seizure. Analysis of this data was performed using the Statistical Package for Social Sciences (SPSS) version 26, descriptive statistics computed, Odd ratio, Chi-square test, and independent t-test. Result(s): Significant associations were noted between; low birth weight, bottle-feeding, family history of FS, low mean hemoglobin level, Cesarean section, passive smoking, and the occurrence of the first febrile seizure. The main underlying cause of fever was the upper respiratory tract infection. Conclusion(s): Of noteworthy, low birth weight, formula milk feeding, positive family history of febrile seizure, anemia, caesarean section, passive smoking during pregnancy, could predispose to the development of first febrile seizure.Copyright (c) 2021 Turkish Physiotherapy Association.
