ICU admission is on rise in pregnant women due to factors including increasing maternal age, increasing caesarean rates, levels of obesity and other comorbidities. We retrospectively studied the peripartum admissions (pregnant or within 6-weeks postpartum) in obstetric ICU in SMGS hospital, GMC Jammu over a period of one year (from Jan, 2023 to Dec, 2023) in terms of demography, diagnosis and comorbidities at time of admission, length of stay, ICU care and interventions given, outcome and ICU admission rate. Their mean age was 30.12+/-5.0 years. Out of 23804 obstetric admissions during the study period, a total of 305 patients were admitted to ICU with 1.28% as the rate of ICU admission. 42.47% had an obstetric cause of admission, whereas 14.1% had a non-obstetric cause of admission in the ICU. Among non-obstetric causes, anemia was prevalent in 7.2% followed by epilepsy in 3.9%, cardiac disorders in 1.3% and others (like chronic or acute renal failure, Diabetes) in 1.3% cases.
