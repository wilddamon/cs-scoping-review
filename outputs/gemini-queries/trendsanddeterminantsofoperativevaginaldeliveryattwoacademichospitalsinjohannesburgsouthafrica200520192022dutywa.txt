We evaluated the trends and determinants of OVD rates using join point regression at Charlotte Maxeke Johannesburg (CMJAH) and Chris Hani Baragwaneth (CHBAH) Academic Hospitals from 1 January 2005-31 December 2019 and conducted a comparative study of OVD (n = 179) and normal delivery (n = 179).
