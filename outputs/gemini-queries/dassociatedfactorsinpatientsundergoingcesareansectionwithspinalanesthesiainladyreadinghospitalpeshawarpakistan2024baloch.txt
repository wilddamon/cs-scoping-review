"Objective: To determine the prevalence of Post-spinal anesthesia shivering and associated factors in patients undergoing Cesarean section Surgery in Lady Reading Hospital Peshawar. Methodology: This observational study was conducted in Lady Reading Hospital Peshawar. Most of the post-spinal anesthesia was also associated with Hypothermia (92.5%) and hypotension (96.2%).
