In each previous c/s the risk of placenta previa and abruption increase. The aim of this study is to find out the associated between the previous caesarean section and placenta previa and abruption in this pregnancy. A retrospective study was carried out during the period between 1st July 2018 and 20th august 2019 in Al-Zahraa Teaching Hospital. Another 49 patients were without complication. Pregnant women with APH due to other causes were excluded. Data were collected through detailed history.
