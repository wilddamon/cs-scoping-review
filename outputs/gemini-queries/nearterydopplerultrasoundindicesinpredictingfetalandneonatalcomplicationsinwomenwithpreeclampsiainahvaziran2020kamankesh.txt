"Background: According to studies, increased resistance and detectable hemodynamic changes in uterine vessels are associated with an increased risk of progression to preeclampsia and fetal growth restriction within uterus. Early and prenatal diagnosis and timely management of preeclampsia could improve the outcomes and reduce maternal and fetal complications, including low birth weight (LBW). Aim(s): To evaluate the value of uterine artery Dopplerultrasound indices in predicting neonatal complications in women with preeclampsia. Method(s): In this prospective cohort study, 80 pregnant women with gestational age of 26-38-week who diagnosed with preeclampsia referring to Imam Khomeini hospital in Ahvaz were recruited. Factors of gestational age, fetal biometrics, Doppler resistance index (RI) and pulsatility index (PI) of the maternal uterine arteries were evaluated for each woman. The obtained data were analyzed by SPSS version 22 and the level of significance was considered <0.05. Result(s): The mean age of patients was 31. The early diastolic notch was observed in 28 patients (35%). Gray-scale sonographyshowed that all variables including biparietal diameter (BPD), head circumference (HC), abdominal circumference (AC), femur length (FL),and estimated fetal weight (EFW) were significantly higher in patients with normal uterine artery Doppler. Doppler ultrasound results also showed that the mean PI and RI were significantly higher in the group with abnormal uterine artery Doppler (P <0.0001). The mean RI showed a significant inverse relationship with birth weight (BWT) in patients with abnormal uterine artery Doppler. Conclusion(s): The results of this study showed that the abnormal uterine artery Doppler and especially RI is significantly associated with fetal growth restriction and causes several complications in mother and infant. The results also suggest that the RI can be used as an important diagnostic tool in predicting the incidence of LBW.Copyright (c) 2020 Lahore Medical And Dental College. All rights reserved."""
