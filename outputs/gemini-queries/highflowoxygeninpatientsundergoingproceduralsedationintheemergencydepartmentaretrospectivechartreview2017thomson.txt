"OBJECTIVE: Hypoxia is a recognised complication of procedural sedation. This study sought to determine whether there was an association between the use of high-flow oxygen delivery by a non-rebreather (NRB) mask during ED procedural sedation and decreased rates of hypoxia when compared with alternative oxygenation methods.;METHODS: Records of all procedural sedations performed over a 12 month period in an Australian tertiary ED were reviewed retrospectively. Two hundred and five (27.1%) patients were administered oxygen via NRB mask from the outset of their sedation.
