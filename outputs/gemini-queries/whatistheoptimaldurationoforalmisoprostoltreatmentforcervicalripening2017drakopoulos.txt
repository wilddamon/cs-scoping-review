METHODS: Retrospective study of women with term singleton pregnancies and a BS < 6 taking oral misoprostol (20 mg first 2 doses followed by 40 mg every 2 h) for cervical ripening. RESULTS: We included 400 women, 72% nulliparous, mean age of 31.3 +- 5.9 years and 70% with a baseline BS <= 2.
