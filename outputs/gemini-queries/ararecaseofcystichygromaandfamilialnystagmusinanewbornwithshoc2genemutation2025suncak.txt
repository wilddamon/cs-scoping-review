"Cystic hygroma (CH) is a lymphatic malformation commonly associated with various genetic disorders, including RASopathies-syndromes caused by mutations in the RAS-MAPK signaling pathway. We present a neonate referred to our center due to CH and dysmorphic facial features. During follow-up, interventricular septal hypertrophy and nystagmus were identified. Molecular analysis revealed a pathogenic c.4A>G (p.Ser2Gly) variant in the SHOC2 gene.
