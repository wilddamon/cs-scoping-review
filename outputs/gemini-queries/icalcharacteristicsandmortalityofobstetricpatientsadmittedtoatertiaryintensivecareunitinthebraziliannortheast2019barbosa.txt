"Objectives: This study aimed to analyze the determinants of hospitalization and death in an intensive care unit (ICU) between pregnant and postpartum women. Material(s) and Method(s): This is a quantitative and retrospective documentary research performed at Dr. Cesar Cals Hospital. Result(s): Regarding the type of delivery, 41 (73.2%) women had a cesarean section (CS) and, considering the outcome, the majority of the patients (51%) were discharged from the hospital.
