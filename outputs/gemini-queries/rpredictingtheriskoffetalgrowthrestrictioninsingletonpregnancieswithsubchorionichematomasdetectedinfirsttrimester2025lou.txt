"Objective: To investigate the relationship between first trimester subchorionic hematoma (SCH) and fetal growth restriction (FGR), and to develop a nomogram for predicting the risk of FGR in patients with SCH. Logistic regression was employed to identify risk factors for FGR in patients with SCH, and the nomogram was constructed based on the regression coefficients of relevant variables. Results: A total of 1,055 individuals were enrolled in the study, with 504 identified with SCH during the first trimester ultrasound examination.
