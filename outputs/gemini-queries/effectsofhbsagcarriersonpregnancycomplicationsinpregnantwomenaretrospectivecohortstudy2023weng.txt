"Objective: Hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection is a major health threat worldwide, especially in developing countries. We aimed to investigate the impact of hepatitis B carrier on pregnancy complications in pregnant women, in China. Method(s): This retrospective cohort study was conducted by using data from the EHR system of Longhua District People's Hospital in Shenzhen, China, from January 2018 to June 2022. Binary logistic regression was used to evaluate the relationship between HBsAg carrier status and pregnancy complications and pregnancy outcomes. Result(s): The study included 2095 HBsAg carriers (exposed group) and 23,019 normal pregnant women (unexposed group). Pregnant women in the exposed group were older than the pregnant women in the unexposed group (29 (27,32) vs. 29 (26,32), p < 0.001). Conclusion(s): The prevalence rate of HBsAg carriers in pregnant women in Longhua District of Shenzhen was 8.34%.
