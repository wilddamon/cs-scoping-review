Results: Of 184 children with EA, 46 (25%) developed EoE during this period. Thirty-eight EoE participants were matched to 76 controls. Conclusions: Prolonged duration and high cumulative dose of PPI exposure were significantly associated with subsequent EoE development in children with EA.
