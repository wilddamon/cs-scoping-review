"Background: Rates of maternal morbidity and mortality in the United States lag behind other high-income countries. Purpose: To evaluate if collaborative activities between free-standing birth centers and their collaborative obstetric departments are associated with birth outcomes. Methods: The American Association of Birth Centers manages a large prospective perinatal registry, and also performs surveys of all known birth centers that collect data on practice characteristics.
