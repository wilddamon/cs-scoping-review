METHODS: Three hundred eighty-five thousand, eight hundred twenty-five nulliparous, term, singleton, vertex births among Black, White, and Biracial individuals in California (2011-2019) were included from a statewide administrative birth cohort of birth certificates linked to hospital records. RESULTS: The risk of CD was greatest in Black individuals (30.71%), followed by Biracial (25.47%) and White (24.98%).
