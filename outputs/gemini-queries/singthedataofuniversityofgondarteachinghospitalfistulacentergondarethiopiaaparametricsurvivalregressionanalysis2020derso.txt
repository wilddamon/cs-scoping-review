Better knowledge of the risk factors for obstetric fistula could help in preventing its occurrence. The purpose of this study was to assess the characteristics of obstetric fistula patients. A retrospective and cohort study was used and 289 patients was taken from a hospital records. The study includes obstetric fistula patients who were recorded in the medical record room of cards which have the vital data and the patients with missing main data for the research were excluded.
