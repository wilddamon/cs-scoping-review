Analysis of data regarding obstetric high dependency unit (HDU) admissions helps in identifying the preventable outcomes and deficits in the healthcare system. Material(s) and Method(s): This was a retrospective observational study of obstetric HDU admissions in a teaching institute from May 2024 to June 2024. Result(s): The total number of obstetric admissions was 1,130 and the total obstetric HDU admissions was 100 in number, the admission rate being 11.3 per 100 obstetric admissions. Interventions included close vital monitoring, antihypertensives, magnesium sulfate, blood transfusion, and inotropes. There was a high recovery rate of 89% despite having 82% unbooked cases. Conclusion(s): Analysis of obstetric HDU admissions is important to understand the cause and outcome of maternal complications.
