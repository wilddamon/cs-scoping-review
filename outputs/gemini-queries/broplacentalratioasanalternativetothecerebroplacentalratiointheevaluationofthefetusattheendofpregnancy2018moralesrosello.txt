"Objective: Low cerebroplacental ratio (CPR) near term has emerged as a marker of fetal adverse outcome. Methods: This was a prospective cohort study of 1470 pregnancies undergoing an ultrasound assessment of the umbilical artery, middle cerebral artery and VA Doppler beyond 34 weeks' gestation within 14 days of delivery.
