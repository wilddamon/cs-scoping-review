"Objective: To find the association of grandmultiparity with intrapartum complications as well as perinatal morbidity and mortality. Methodology: This prospective, case control study was conducted in the department of Obstetric and Gynecology, Sir Syed Hospital, Karachi, Pakistan from January 1, 2008 to December 31, 2012. Sampling technique was convenient, non probability. Detailed record of labor management, including progress of labor, operative delivery, third stage complications and fetal outcome were obtained. Result(s): Maternal characteristics were similar in grandmultipara (GMP) and low multipara (MP). There were 96 GMP and 104 low MP. Prolonged labor was seen in 19/96(19.8%) GMP vs 5/104(4.81%) low MP (OR: 4.8857). Cesarean sections were seen in 26(27.08%) vs 1/104 (OR: 38.2571), which is statistically significant. Obstructed labor, failure to progress and meconium stained liquor were the main indications for cesarean section. Perinatal mortality was significantly increased in GMP as compared to low MP (OR: 5.5291). Conclusion(s): Grandmultiparity was a risk factor for prolonged labor, obstructed labor and emergency cesarean section, meconium aspiration syndrome and perinatal mortality. Women need to be informed of the dangers of high order births and advised to confine in tertiary care hospital and to limit their family size by effective contraceptive methods.Copyright (c) 2015, Pakistan Medical Association.
