MATERIALS AND METHODS: A prospective cohort study enrolled 135 US adult women (median age, 30 years, body mass index [BMI], 47.2 kg/m(2)) who underwent RYGB or SG (2006-2009) and subsequently reported >= 1 pregnancy within 7 years.
