"OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the effect of early versus late amniotomy on delivery outcomes in pregnancies complicated by fetal macrosomia. METHODS: A retrospective cohort study was conducted at a tertiary university-affiliated medical center between 2014 and 2020. Participants were classified into two groups according to cervical dilatation at the time of amniotomy: early amniotomy (<=4 cm) and late amniotomy (>4 cm). Delivery and neonatal outcomes were compared, and multivariable logistic regression was performed to adjust for potential confounders. RESULTS: A total of 789 patients were included (early amniotomy, n = 353; late amniotomy, n = 436). Baseline estimated fetal weights and neonatal birthweights were similar between groups.
