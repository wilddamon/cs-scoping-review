Methods: We divided 276 cesarean scar pregnancy patients into three groups. Group A underwent direct hysteroscopy; Group B received uterine artery embolization plus hysteroscopy; Group C received the systemic administration of methotrexate prior to hysteroscopy. Results: The patients in Group A lost significantly more blood than those in Groups B (P < 0.05). Conclusion: Direct hysteroscopy is a reliable treatment option for most early type I cesarean scar pregnancy patients with a gestational sac.
