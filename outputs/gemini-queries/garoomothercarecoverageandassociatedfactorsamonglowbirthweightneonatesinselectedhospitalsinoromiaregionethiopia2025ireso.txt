"Background: Initiating Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC) immediately after birth, rather than waiting for stabilization, can reduce neonatal mortality by 25%. Method(s): A cross-sectional study design was conducted using existing data to determine effective early KMC (eKMC) coverage. Conclusion(s): Our study found a low coverage of effective eKMC, with only 8.9% of eligible neonates receiving it. Caesarean section delivery and very low birth weight (< 1500 g) were associated with lower odds of receiving effective eKMC.
