AIM: The aim of this study was to compare the efficacy of carbetocin and oxytocin in promoting placental separation and controlling PPH following normal vaginal delivery (NVD) and lower segment cesarean section (LSCS). RESULTS: The mean time to placental separation and third-stage duration were significantly shorter in the carbetocin group compared to the oxytocin group.
