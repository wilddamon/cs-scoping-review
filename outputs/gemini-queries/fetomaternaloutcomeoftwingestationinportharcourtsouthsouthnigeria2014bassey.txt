"BACKGROUND: Twin pregnancy is a high risk pregnancy with significant implications for both mother and baby. OBJECTIVE: To determine the prevalence, fetal and maternal outcomes of twin gestation at the University of Port Harcourt Teaching Hospital (UPTH). MATERIALS AND METHODS: This was a 5-year retrospective analysis of hospital records of mothers who had twin gestation and delivered at UPTH between January 1st 2008 and December 31st 2012. RESULTS: The prevalence of twin gestation was 16 per 1000 deliveries. The Mean age and parity were 29.63 +- 4.1 and 1.65 +- 1.3 respetcively. Premature delivery occurred in 30.8% of cases. The perinatal mortality rate was 55.85/1000 total birth while the maternal mortality ratio was 281.09/100,000 live birth. CONCLUSION: Twin gestation in Port Harcourt is still associated with significant maternal and perinatal morbidities and mortality.
