"Objective: To examine the combined impact of migraine and gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) on the risks of premature (persons aged <=60 years) major adverse cardiovascular and cerebrovascular events (MACCE) based on a composite endpoint of fatal and non-fatal myocardial infarction (MI) and stroke. Methods: In a Danish population-based cohort longitudinal study, we established four cohorts among women with at least one pregnancy during 1996-2018: women with migraine, women with GDM, women with both migraine and GDM, and women free of migraine and free of GDM. Conclusions: Migraine and GDM were each independently associated with an increased risk of MACCE.
