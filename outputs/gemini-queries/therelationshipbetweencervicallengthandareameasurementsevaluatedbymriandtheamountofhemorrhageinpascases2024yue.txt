This study aims to identify whether cervical length and cervical area measured by magnetic resonance imaging correlate with massive hemorrhage in patients with placenta accreta spectrum. Methods: The study was conducted at our hospital, and 158 placenta previa patients with placenta accreta spectrum underwent preoperative magnetic resonance imaging examination were included. The cervical length and cervical area were measured and evaluated their ability to identify massive hemorrhage in patients with placenta accreta spectrum. Results: The cervical length and area in patients with massive hemorrhage were both significantly smaller than those in patients without massive hemorrhage.
