Main outcome measures: We investigated patterns of one-week blood pressure changes post-admission, explored factors contributing to diverse patterns using ANOVA and Chi-square tests, and assessed the correlation between these patterns and a composite adverse maternal outcome defined as severe maternal morbidity, mortality, post-partum hemorrhage and cesarean section.
