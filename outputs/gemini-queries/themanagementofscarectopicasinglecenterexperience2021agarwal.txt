"PURPOSE: This study aimed to highlight the clinical features, diagnosis, and different modalities of the treatment of cesarean scar pregnancy (CSP). METHODS: This study was done in the tertiary referral hospital of India for one year. A total of 11 cases were enrolled prospectively. The treatment was given based on the hemodynamic status of the patient and desire for future fertility. Various treatment modalities used were medical, surgical, or interventional digital subtraction angiography to control hemorrhage. RESULTS: Out of 11 patients, six had a history of two cesarean sections in the past, four patients had a history of one cesarean section and one patient with a previous three low segments cesarean section (LSCS). The success of the medical management was monitored by serial b- HCG values. The mean time for the resolution of these 10 patients was 86.7 +- 53.6 days. Three patients underwent emergency uterine artery embolization due to uncontrolled bleeding and one patient required laparotomy. CONCLUSION: CSP is a life-threatening condition that can be diagnosed with the help of transvaginal ultrasonography.
