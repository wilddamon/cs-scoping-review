Objective(s): To determine the level of maternal satisfaction with BEmONC services and associated factors among mothers visiting public health facilities in Gedeo zone, Southern, Ethiopia 2020. Method(s): Institution-based cross-sectional study was conducted among 413 mothers selected via a systematic random sampling technique. Conclusion(s): The overall satisfaction of mothers with BEmONC service in this study was sub-optimal. Maternal age and the presence of birth companions were factors that were significantly associated with mothers' satisfaction with BEmONC service.
