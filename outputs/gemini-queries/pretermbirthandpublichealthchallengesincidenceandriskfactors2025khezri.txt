This study determined the incidence and risk factors associated with preterm birth in rural areas of sardasht, Iran. Study design: Case-control study. Conclusion: Preterm birth was significantly associated with education level, antenatal care visits, premature rupture of membranes, gestational diabetes mellitus, preeclampsia, maternal anemia, parity, history of abortion and maternal age.
