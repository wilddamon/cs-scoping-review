"Introduction: oxytocin is a potent utero-tonic drug that is used in females during cesarean section. However, as this drug is widely use for the prevention of post-partum hemorrhage, the evaluation of its hemodynamic implications is the need of the hour. Objective(s): the purpose of this study is to look into effects of oxytocin on cardio vascular system of females undergoing cesarean section. It tries to elaborate the implications observed in female patients after the intravenous administration of the drug. Methodology: it is a prospective cohort study in which 264 patients undergoing cesarean section were observed during the course of their surgery. It is a 23 months long study that will evaluate the impact of intravenous administration of oxytocin on heart rate and mean arterial pressure in females undergoing cesarean section. Conclusion(s): it was found that intravenous bolus administration of oxytocin leads to a rise in heart rate in females undergoing cesarean section. It was also found that there is a reduction in mean arterial pressure in females under study after the intravenous administration of the drug.
