Material(s) and Method(s): This is a retrospective study conducted from March 2022 to February 2023. Out of 3926 women, 3441 (87.64%) had undergone emergency cesarean section. 372(9.47%) patients had postpartum haemorrhage, four (0.10%) had peripartum hysterectomy.
