"Aim: We aimed to determine the relationship between first trimester placental volume and pregnancy, birth, and perinatal complications. Material(s) and Method(s): This prospective observational study was performed between August 2022 and March 2023 at the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Tekirdag Namik Kemal University Faculty of Medicine. Discussion(s): Although the first trimester placental volume was positively related to maternal weight and gestational age, it was not associated with other variables.
