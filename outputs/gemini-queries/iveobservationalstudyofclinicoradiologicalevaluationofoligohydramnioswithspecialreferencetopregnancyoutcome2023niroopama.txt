Material(s) and Method(s): The present study was carried out in the Department of obstetrics and Gynecology, Narayana Medical College, Nellore, AP for a period of 1 year, January 2021 to December 2021. Conclusion(s): Our study shows that isolated decreased AFI after 34 weeks of gestation is associated with satisfactory perinatal outcome.
