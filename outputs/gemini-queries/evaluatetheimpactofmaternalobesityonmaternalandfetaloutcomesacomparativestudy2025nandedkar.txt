"Background: Obesity is affecting many antenatal women in recent years., Mothers who are overweight or obese during pregnancy and childbirth are known to be at risk of significant antenatal, intrapartum, postpartum and neonatal complications. Therefore, the present study was conducted to study the impact of maternal obesity on maternal and fetal outcomes in a tertiary care centre. Material and Methodology: The present study was single centre prospective observational comparative study conducted in Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology in a tertiary care centre from August 2022 to December 2023. A total of 100 pregnant females were enrolled and assigned into group 1 (BMI <25 kg/m2) and group 2 (BMI >=25 kg/m2) The data was collected from patients regarding demographic profile, clinical spectrum, lab investigations, Gynecological examination. Data entry and data analysis was done using the SPSS (Statistical package for the social science) version 23 for window. Result(s): The mean age of patients in Group 2 and Group 1 was 31.20 +/-3.87 and 28.44 +/-3.87 years respectively with statistically significant difference. The vaginal mode of delivery was more in Group 1 (76%) compared to Group 2 (42%) with statistically significant difference. The mean length of hospital stay of patients in Group 2 was more (5.59 +/-1.49) compared to Group 1 (2.77 +/-1.09) with statistically significant difference.
