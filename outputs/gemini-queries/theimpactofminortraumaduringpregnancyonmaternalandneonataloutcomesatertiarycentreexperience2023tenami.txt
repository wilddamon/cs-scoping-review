Material(s) and Method(s): A retrospective single centre case-controlled study was performed between 2005 and 2017 in a university affiliated tertiary obstetric and trauma centre. All pregnant women of 13-36 weeks gestation that presented to the department of emergency medicine with an Injury Severity Score of <9 were identified. Maternal and neonatal outcomes were compared with a control group of non-trauma pregnant women during the study at a ratio of 1:4.
