"Subcutaneous fat necrosis of the newborn (SCFNN) is a rare form of panniculitis classically affecting healthy full-term infants. There are a number of predisposing factors including perinatal asphyxia. The condition generally has a benign course with spontaneous resolution, but monitoring for metabolic complications, in particular the potentially life-threatening complication of hypercalcaemia, is critical.
