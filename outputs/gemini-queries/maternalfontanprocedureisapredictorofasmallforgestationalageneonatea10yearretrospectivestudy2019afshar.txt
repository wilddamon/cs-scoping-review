STUDY DESIGN: This single-center retrospective cohort study involves pregnant women with a Fontan palliation who delivered at UCLA Medical Center over a 10-year period (2007-2017). Forty percent of neonates were born small (<10th percentile) for gestational age; 44.0% of all neonates were admitted to the neonatal intensive care unit. CONCLUSION: Women with a single ventricle and Fontan circulation can have a successful pregnancy, although they are at increased risk for arrhythmias and heart failure.
