"Background: Odontogenic abscesses may significantly affect maternal health during pregnancy. Aim(s): This study analyzes three cases of pregnant patients with odontogenic infections, comparing them to a control group of non-pregnant women, and reviews recent literature. Material(s) and Method(s): Between January 2020 and April 2025, 3 pregnant and 70 non-pregnant women with odontogenic abscesses were treated. Clinical presentation, pathogens, therapy, and outcomes were compared. Result(s): Severe sequelae, such as rapid abscess spread and systemic inflammation, were more frequent in pregnant women, though not statistically significant (p = 0.068).
