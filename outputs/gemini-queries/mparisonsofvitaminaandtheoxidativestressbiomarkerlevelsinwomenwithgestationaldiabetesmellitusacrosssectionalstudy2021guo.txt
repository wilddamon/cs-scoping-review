"OBJECTIVE: Studies examining the link between oxi-dative stress and serum levels of vitamin A in patients with gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) are lim-ited. The objective of this study was to compare the levels of maternal oxidative stress markers and vitamin A between normal pregnant women and women with GDM. STUDY DESIGN: The study consisted of healthy pregnant women and patients suffer-ing from GDM. Maternal blood samples for oxidative stress markers and vitamin A of each subject were col-lected at delivery. The serum levels of oxidative stress markers and vitamin A were compared within the groups. RESULT(S): Higher serum levels of malondialdehyde (MDA) and lower serum levels of superoxide dismutase (SOD), catalase (CAT), and glutathione (GSH) were observed in GDM cases as compared to controls (all p<0.001). In addition, GDM women with vitamin A deficiency had higher serum levels of oxidative markers and lower serum levels of antioxidative markers than did those with vitamin A sufficiency (all p<0.01). CONCLUSION(S): Antioxi-dant defense mechanisms might be impaired in patients with GDM.
