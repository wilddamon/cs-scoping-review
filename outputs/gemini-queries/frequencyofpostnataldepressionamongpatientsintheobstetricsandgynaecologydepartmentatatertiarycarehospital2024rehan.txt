"Objective: To identify the percentage of postnatal depression and risk factors related to the condition among female patients. Method(s): Three hundred and sixty-nine postpartum women within six weeks of delivery, using the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale to identify depressive symptoms were enrolled.
