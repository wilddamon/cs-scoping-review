"Objective: To describe a single center's experience with the management of neonates born at 22 weeks of gestation. Data on antenatal management was collected from maternal charts. Result(s): Nine mothers delivered due to preterm labor, 5 due to preterm pre-labor rupture of membranes with intra-amniotic infection, and 2 after antepartum hemorrhage.
