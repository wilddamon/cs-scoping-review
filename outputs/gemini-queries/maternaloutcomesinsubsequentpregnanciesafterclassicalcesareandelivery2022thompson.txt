METHODS: We conducted a cross-sectional analysis of patients with live singleton births at or after 24 weeks of gestation who had a prior classical cesarean delivery or a low transverse cesarean delivery in the 2016-2019 National Inpatient Sample database. Outcome measures included mode of delivery, uterine rupture, and severe maternal morbidity (SMM), as defined by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Multivariable regressions further assessed how patients' sociodemographic and hospital characteristics might influence the differences in maternal outcomes between the two groups. RESULTS: The sample included 1,671,249 patients: 25,540 with prior classical cesarean delivery and 1,645,709 with prior low transverse cesarean delivery.
