"Purpose: The aim of this study was to evaluate whether contrast-enhanced ultrasound (CEUS) could be used as a method for diagnosing cesarean scar pregnancy (CSP). Material(s) and Method(s): This retrospective study reviewed medical records, CEUS results, and clinical outcomes of 30 women with CSP, admitted to the Hospital of Obstetrics and Gynecology. The CSP lesions were classified into three types: first-trimester gestation sac type, first-trimester mass type, and second-trimester gestation sac type.
