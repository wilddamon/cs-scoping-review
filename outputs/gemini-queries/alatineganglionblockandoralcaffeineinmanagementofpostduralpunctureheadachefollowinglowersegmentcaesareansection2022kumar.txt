Aim(s): To evaluate the efficacy of sphenopalatine ganglion block in management of PDPH and assess the efficacy of oral caffeine 300 mg in management of PDPH. RESULT - The mean VAS score at 72 hours after LSCS was 2.00+1.512 in Group-1, in Group-2 .67+.976, which was statistically significant (p<0.05).
