We searched PubMed and Medline with keywords "determinants of caesarean birth/caesarean section" and "caesarean birth/caesarean section trend" in the Region during 2000-2017. Social, cultural, individual and institutional factors affecting women's choice for childbirth have contributed to the high caesarean birth rate.
