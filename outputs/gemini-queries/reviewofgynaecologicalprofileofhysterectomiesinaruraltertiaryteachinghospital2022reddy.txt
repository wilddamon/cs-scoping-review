"Background: Hysterectomy is the most frequently performed major gynaecological operation next to caesarean section. It remains the chief modality of treatment for various gynaecolgical conditions. Routes of hysterectomy include abdominal, vaginal, laparoscopic and combined approaches. Aim of the Study: To analyze the clinical profile, indications and postoperative complications of hysterectomies performed at a rural tertiary teaching hospital. Methodology: This retrospective study was conducted in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Mediciti Institute of Medical Sciences, a tertiary teaching hospital at Medchal. Medical records of 240 cases of hysterectomies that were performed from January 2015 to December 2021 were retrieved from Medical Records Department. The socio-demographic profile, indications and postoperative complications of these hysterectomies were collected using a predesigned proforma. Result(s): Out of the total 240 hysterectomies, 51.25 % were in the age group of 40-49 years followed by the age group of 50-59 years i.e 19.5 %. Hysterectomy was commonly performed in para 2 and para 3. Common presenting symptoms were menstrual irregularities (55%), followed by mass per vagina (27.5 %). Common indications for hysterectomy were AUB (36.25%), Fibroid (34.58%) and UV prolapse (29.16%). Abdominal hysterectomy(62.5%) was performed more commonly than vaginal hysterectomy (29.16%). Conclusion(s): Indications for hysterectomy should be clearly evaluated and patient should be properly counselled about conservative options, risks and benefits of the procedure.
