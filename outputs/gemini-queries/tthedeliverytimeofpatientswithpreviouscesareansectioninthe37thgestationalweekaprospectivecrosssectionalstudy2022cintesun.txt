Two different regression models were created to predict different CS times after the 37th week of pregnancy. Weight gain was found to be independently associated with CS time within the first seven days after admission (OR: 1.267, 95% CI: [1.003-1.599]; p = .047). Vaginal birth history and estimated fetal weight were found to be independently associated with CS time in the 8th day and further after admission (OR: 0.244, 95% CI: [0.089-0.675]; p = .007 and OR: 1.002, 95% CI: [1.000-1.003]; p = .047).
