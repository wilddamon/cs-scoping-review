"BACKGROUND: Early-life exposures including diet, and the gut microbiome have been proposed to predispose infants towards multifactorial diseases later in life. To test this, we developed a metabolic modeling workflow targeting the infant gut microbiome. METHODS: The AGORA2 resource of human microbial genome-scale reconstructions was expanded with a human milk oligosaccharide degradation module. Trillions of microorganisms live in the digestive system of humans, with those within the intestine being described as the intestinal microbiome. Intestinal microbes perform important metabolic functions such as digestion of the diet (e.g., breast milk) and production of metabolites such as B-vitamins.
