METHODS: We conducted a retrospective cohort analysis of women with only one prior pregnancy who delivered between February 1, 2006, and May 31, 2013. We analysed data from the Zambia Electronic Perinatal System. Maternal and infant characteristics were analyzed for association with stillbirth using Pearson's kh(2) test or the Wilcoxon rank-sum test. We calculated risk ratios for the relationship between stillbirth (antepartum vs intrapartum) and prior CD, with a log Poisson model to adjust for confounding. RESULTS: Of 57 320 women in our cohort, 1933 (3.4%) reported a prior CD. There were 1012 (1.8%) stillbirths in the no prior CD group and 81 (4.2%) in the prior CD group (P<0.001). The difference between groups was most apparent at 36-37 weeks' gestation (log-rank P<0.001). CONCLUSION: Prior CD was associated with increased risk of stillbirth.
