"OBJECTIVE: To describe the management and outcomes of placenta accreta, increta, and percreta in the UK.;DESIGN: A population-based descriptive study using the UK Obstetric Surveillance System (UKOSS).;SETTING: All 221 UK hospitals with obstetrician-led maternity units.;POPULATION: All women diagnosed with placenta accreta, increta, and percreta in the UK between May 2010 and April 2011.;METHODS: Prospective case identification through the monthly mailing of UKOSS.;MAIN OUTCOME MEASURES: Median estimated blood loss, transfusion requirements.;RESULTS: A cohort of 134 women were identified with placenta accreta, increta, or percreta: 50% (66/133) were suspected to have this condition antenatally.
