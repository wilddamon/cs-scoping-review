Neonatal hyperbilirubinemia is the most commonreason for readmission after early hospital discharge. Objective(s): To find out critical value of Cord Serum albumin in predicting the subsequent development of significant neonatal hyperbilirubinemia requiring interventions like phototherapy or exchange transfusion.. Methodology: It is a Hospital based prospective observational studying a sample of 100 perterm babies with birth weight <2.5 kg selected by simple random sampling.
