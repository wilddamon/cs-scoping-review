"Aim: To investigate the long-term quality of life and surgical outcome of female patients with congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH). Methods: Questionnaires for health-related quality of life (HRQoL) outcome (WHO-5, SF-36), lower urinary tract symptoms (ICIQ-FLUTS) and sexual outcome (ICIQ-FLUTSsex) were administered to adult CAH patients. Paediatric CAH patients and their parents were invited to complete WHO-5, PedsQL-4.0-SF15-Generic Core Scales and ICIQ-CLUTS. Results: Six (46%) adults and 7 (54%) children with 21-hydroxylase-deficiency (age ranged from 8 years to 53 years) were recruited. Eleven (85%) had undergone a feminising genitoplasty. Conclusions: Our data did not show an impaired HRQoL nor long-term lower urinary tract symptoms in CAH patients' self-reports.
