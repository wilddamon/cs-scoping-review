"Objective To determine the risk on brain lesions according to gestational age (GA) in neonates with neonatal encephalopathy. Design Secondary analysis of the prospective national French population-based cohort, Long-Term Outcome of NeonataL EncePhALopathy. Setting French neonatal intensive care units. Patients Neonates with moderate or severe neonatal encephalopathy (NE) born at >=34 weeks' GA (wGA) between September 2015 and March 2017. Main outcome measures The results of MRI performed within the first 12 days were classified in seven injured brain regions: basal ganglia and thalami, white matter (WM), cortex, posterior limb internal capsule, corpus callosum, brainstem and cerebellum. Conclusion WM is the main brain structure affected in late-preterm and early-term infants with NE, with fewer WM lesions as GA increases.
