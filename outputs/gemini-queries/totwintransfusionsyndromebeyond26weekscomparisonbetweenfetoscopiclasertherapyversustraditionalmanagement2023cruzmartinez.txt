"INTRODUCTION: A proportion of monochorionic diamniotic (MCDA) twin pregnancies complicated by twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome (TTTS) can present after 26 weeks of gestation. The aim of this study was to compare perinatal outcomes of late TTTS treated by fetoscopic laser coagulation versus traditional management with amniodrainage and/or emergency preterm cesarean delivery (CD). METHODS: Retrospective cohort from January 2012 to January 2023 of consecutive MCDA twin pregnancies complicated by TTTS after 26 weeks and evaluated in our referring centers. We analyzed perinatal outcomes of cases treated with fetoscopic laser surgery at our national referral fetal surgery center in Queretaro, Mexico, and compared them with those managed with traditional management (amniodrainage and/or emergency preterm CD).
