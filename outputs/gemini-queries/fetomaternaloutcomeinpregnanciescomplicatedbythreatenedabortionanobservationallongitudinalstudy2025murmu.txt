Aim(s): To investigate the effect of the 1st trimester vaginal bleeding on maternal and perinatal outcome. Conclusion(s): Threatened abortion in early pregnancy is associated with increased risk of adverse fetomaternal outcomes, including preterm delivery, low birth weight, and cesarean section.
