"Background: In a non-forensic hospital setting, neonatal death within the first week of life is often related to premature birth and/or lung diseases. Our study aimed to assess the correlation between the clinical diagnosis and post-mortem findings in early neonatal deaths. Methods: The retrospective study included autopsy cases with neonatal deaths within the first 7 days of life (arbitrary time interval 2006-2021).
