"Objectives: To study oxygen saturation trends in newborns at birth and up to 15 min of life. Material(s) and Method(s): A prospective observational study was conducted on 300 healthy term and preterm newborns (those who did not require supplemental oxygen) delivered by SVD and LSCS at a tertiary care Centre. The convenience sampling technique was used based on the fact about 3-4 deliveries take place daily, and only a proportion of these met the study inclusion criteria. Result(s): At 5 min of life, 0.7% of male newborns and 0.63% of female newborns had SpO2 levels above 90%.
