The 160 pregnant women were selected from those who havevisited at antenatal care and family planning department inAmnat Charoen Hospital and Sub-district Health Promoting Hospitals, Mueangdistrict, Amnat CharoenProvince,in a calendar year. Data were collected from demographicinformation.
