"Background: About 50% of fetal deaths occurs in low risk mothers without apparent cause. The health of fetus is the basis of life of each individual and variety of methods have been developed for monitoring fetus in high risk mothers, we should not under estimate the fetus of low risk mothers as they are precious. Continuous electronic monitoring is not possible throughout labor due to lack of manpower and resources. Objective(s): To evaluate role of NST as a screening method in low risk pregnancy. Method(s): In this prospective study, 200 low risk pregnant women with period of gestation 37 to 41 weeks attending labor room in department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, SIMS Hapur, in early labor or prelabor phase were subjected to NST and results were analyzed .All high risk pregnancies were excluded. Result(s): Non Stress Test as a screening test for antenatal fetal surveillance has high specificity and high negative predictive value. Fetal distress,meconium stained liquor and caesarean section were higher in category 2 and 3 NST.
