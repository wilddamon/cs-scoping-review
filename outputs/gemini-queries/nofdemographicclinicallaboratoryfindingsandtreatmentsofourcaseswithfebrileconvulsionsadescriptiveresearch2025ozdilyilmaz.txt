Material(s) and Method(s): 200 patients who applied to Trakya University Faculty of Medicine, Department of Pediatrics Outpatient Clinic and Child Neurology Polyclinic between January 2014-December 2018 and were diagnosed with febrile convulsion were evaluated retrospectively. Conclusion(s): Epilepsy was observed in %13 of febrile convulsions. The rate of epilepsy diagnosis was high in those with abnormal EEG (81%). As a result; monitoring of febrile convulsions is important due to the increased risk of epilepsy and frequent recurrence compared to the general population.
