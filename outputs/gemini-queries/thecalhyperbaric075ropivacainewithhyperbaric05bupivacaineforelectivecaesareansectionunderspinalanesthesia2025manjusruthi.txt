The early recovery from motor blockade with Ropivacaine aids in early ambulation of patient. Conclusion(s): Our findings suggest that patients receiving 0.75% hyperbaric Ropivacaine had faster onset of sensory block and shorter duration of sensory and motor block duration than those receiving 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine..
