We investigated the relationship between preoperative anxiety and changes in arterial pressure after spinal anaesthesia for caesarean delivery. Material(s) and Method(s): A hospital-based prospective observational study with 90 pregnant women separated into three groups (Group A n=30, Group B n=30, and Group C n=30) was carried out.
