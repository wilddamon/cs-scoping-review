"Objective. To assess the safety and efficacy of immediate intrauterine contraceptive copper device (IUD) insertion following delivery of the placenta in women undergoing caesarean delivery. Methods. In this randomised clinical trial, 66 women were evenly divided into two arms, with 33 patients in each arm. The first group had an IUD inserted 10 minutes after delivery of the placenta (post-placental group), while the second group had their IUD inserted at 6 weeks following delivery (interval group).
