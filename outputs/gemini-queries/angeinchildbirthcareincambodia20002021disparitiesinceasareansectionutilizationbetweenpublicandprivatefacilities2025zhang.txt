This study investigated the utilization of childbirth care across public and private health facilities, with a particular focus on the use of caesarean section (C-section). METHODS: This study used cross-sectional data from the Demographic and Health Surveys conducted in Cambodia in 2000, 2005, 2010, 2014 and 2021-22. C-section use was significantly higher in 2021 compared to 2010 (Adjusted OR 3.32, 95% CI [2.72, 4.07]).
