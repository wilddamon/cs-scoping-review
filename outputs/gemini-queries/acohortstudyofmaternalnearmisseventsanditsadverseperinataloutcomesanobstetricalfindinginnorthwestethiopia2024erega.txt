OBJECTIVE(S): This study aimed to assess the incidence, determinants, and maternal near-miss effects on perinatal outcomes among women at public hospitals in the South Gondar zone in 2021. STUDY DESIGN: A facility-based prospective cohort study was conducted from January 10, 2021, to May 10, 2021.
