Method(s): The clinical data of 80 puerperae undergoing natural delivery in the Department of Obstetrics of our hospital from December 2020 to December 2021 were selected for the retrospect ive analysis, and the patients were divided into the study group (SG, n=40, intrathecal blocking anesthesia) and the reference group (RG, n=40, non-invasive delivery technique), so as to compare the puerperia's degree of perineal laceration, occurrence of postpartum complications, etc. between the two groups.
