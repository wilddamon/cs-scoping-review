Our goal was to analyze the association between maternal depressive symptoms at 6 months after birth and child's neurodevelopmental disorders at 18 months-old, in a homogeneous population characterized by low socioeconomic and cultural level. Patients and Methods: A prospective cohort study was conducted. There were included 127 healthy postpartum women and their infants.
