"Background: Nutrition is of paramount importance during early development, since suboptimal growth in this period of life is linked to adverse long- and mid-term outcomes. This is particularly relevant for preterm infants, who fail to thrive during the first weeks of life and develop extrauterine growth restriction (EUGR). This group of premature babies represents an interesting population to investigate using a metabolomic approach to optimize nutritional intake. Aim(s): To analyse and compare the urinary metabolomic pattern at birth of preterm infants with and without growth restriction at 36 weeks of postmenstrual age or at discharge, searching for putative markers of growth failure.
