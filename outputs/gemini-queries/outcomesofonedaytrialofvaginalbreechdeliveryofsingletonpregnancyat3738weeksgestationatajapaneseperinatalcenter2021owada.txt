Materials and methods: A planned induction of vaginal breech delivery at 37-38 weeks' gestation was performed in 72 women who got consent form April 2006 to March 2016 as follows: (1) insertion of a balloon catheter into the uterine cervix filled with a sterile saline fluid of 400-500 mL, (2) intravenous administration of oxytocin after confirming of the natural falling out by itself of the balloon catheter, and (3) selection of cesarean delivery when vaginal delivery cannot be expected by evening.
