"Aims: To examine the associations between undocumented pregnant migrant women and the risk of experiencing stillbirth or preterm birth. Methods: A retrospective case-control study based on nationwide registers from Statistics Denmark and hospital journals from the seven largest hospital wards in Denmark from 1 January 2011 to 31 December 2018. Logistic regression models were used to estimate the risk of undocumented pregnant migrant women experiencing (a) stillbirth and (b) preterm birth compared with the control group. Results: Of the undocumented pregnant migrant women, 33.3% were EU citizens, 16.2% were applicants for residence and 50.5% had an unknown basis for residence. Conclusions: We found a higher risk of stillbirth and preterm birth among the undocumented pregnant migrant women than in the control group.
