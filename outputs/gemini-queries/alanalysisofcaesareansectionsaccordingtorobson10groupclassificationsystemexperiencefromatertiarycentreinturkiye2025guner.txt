The Robson 10-group classification system, which is based on obstetric features, is the global standard for monitoring, evaluating, and comparing CS rates in various healthcare settings. Documenting CS rates in tertiary referral centre using the Robson 10-group classification system was the goal of this study. METHODS: This retrospective study was conducted on women with deliveries over four years, from June 1, 2020, to October 1, 2024, and categorised according to the Robson 10-group classification system.
