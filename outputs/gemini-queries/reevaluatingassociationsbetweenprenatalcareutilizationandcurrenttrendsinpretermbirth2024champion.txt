Study Design This was a retrospective cohort study of singleton, viable nonanomalous deliveries from 1991 to 2018. We evaluated for effect modification by year of birth. Results Of 92,294 patients, 14,057 (15%) had PTB. Conclusion Despite an increase in adequate PNC, there was a rise in PTB associated with inadequate and suboptimal PNC.
