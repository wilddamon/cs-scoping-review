Method(s): This was a hospital-based prospective comparative study conducted in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Jhalawar Medical College, Jhalawar. 400 antenatal women at term and singleton pregnancy posted for cesarean section from October 2018 to September 2019 for 1 year duration. Conclusion(s): Manual method seems to decrease the time interval between delivery of baby and that of placenta.
