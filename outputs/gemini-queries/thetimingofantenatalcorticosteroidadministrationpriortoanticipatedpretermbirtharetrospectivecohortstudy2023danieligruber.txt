"Objective: To examine suboptimal antenatal corticosteroid (ACS) administration based on indications and maternal-obstetrical factors. Methods: Women who prematurely delivered (24 + 0 to 36 + 6 gestational weeks) a viable neonate following ACS treatment between 2012 and 2019 were identified by a retrospective database review.
