The purpose of this study was to assess the perinatal outcome and pregnancy problems in twin pregnancies. Result(s): The age range of 20 to 29 years and multigravida had the highest prevalence of twin pregnancies at 2.09%. The most frequent foetal presentation was vertex-vertex. Birth hypoxia (35%), intrauterine growth restriction (13.3%), hyperbilirubinemia (10%), and newborn sepsis (3.3%) were perinatal complications. The LBW status of the newborns was 85%.
