MATERIALS AND METHODS: This descriptive and cross-sectional study was conducted between January 2018 and May 2019 in a research and training hospital in Izmir, Turkey. Those mothers who complied with the acceptance criteria, who gave birth via caesarean section and who had preeclampsia (n=73) were accepted into this study. Data were collected using the Personal Identity Form, the Edinburgh Postpartum Depression Scale and the Breastfeeding Self-Efficacy Scale (BSES) in two follow-ups.
