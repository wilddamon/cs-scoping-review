"Background: Magnesium sulfate (MgSO <inf>4</inf> ) is the standard therapy for the treatment of preeclampsia and prevention of eclamptic seizures in labor. Methods: Sixty women undergoing caesarean section were randomly divided into the MgSO <inf>4</inf> group and the control group. The MgSO <inf>4</inf> group received 50 mg/kg MgSO <inf>4</inf> (total 50 ml) infused intravenously within 15 min and the control group received the same volume normal saline, then spinal anesthesia was administered using a standardized technique.
