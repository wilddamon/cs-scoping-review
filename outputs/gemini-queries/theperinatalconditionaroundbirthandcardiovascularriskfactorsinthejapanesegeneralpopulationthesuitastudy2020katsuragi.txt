"Aim: To investigate the relationship between perinatal condition around birth and cardiovascular risk in later life. We also selected and studied a total of 28 individuals (12 men and 16 women) specifically about the conformity rate of the breastfeeding method between participants' memories and what was written in the maternal and child health handbooks.
