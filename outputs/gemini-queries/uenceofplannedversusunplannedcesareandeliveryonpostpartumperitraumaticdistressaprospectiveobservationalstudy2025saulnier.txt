We hypothesized that peritraumatic distress scores, measured using the peritraumatic distress inventory (PDI), would differ between planned and unplanned cesarean deliveries. METHODS: In this observational prospective study, patients completed the PDI and patient perception score (PPS) 24 hours after cesarean delivery.
