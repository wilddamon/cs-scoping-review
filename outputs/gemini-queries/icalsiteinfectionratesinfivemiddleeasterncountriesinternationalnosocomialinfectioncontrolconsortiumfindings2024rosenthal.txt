Centers for Disease Control and Prevention/National Healthcare Safety Network (CDC/NHSN) definitions were applied for SSI. Surgical procedures (SPs) were categorized into 12 types according to the International Classification of Diseases, ninth revision (ICD-9) criteria, 9(th)edition. RESULTS: From 2014 to 2023, we collected data on 304 SSIs associated with 21 322 SPs.
