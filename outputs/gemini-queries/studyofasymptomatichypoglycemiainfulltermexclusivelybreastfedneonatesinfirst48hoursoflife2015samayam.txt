Episodes of asymptomatic hypoglycemia may occur in term neonates without apparent risk factors. AIM: This study was conducted to estimate the incidence of hypoglycemia in healthy, intramural, singleton full term neonates and to correlate the above incidence with maternal factors like parity, mode of delivery and time of initiation of breast feeding after birth. MATERIALS AND METHODS: A hospital based, prospective longitudinal study involving healthy, term, asymptomatic neonates. Blood glucose values were measured at 1, 6, 24 and 48 hours of life. RESULTS: The overall incidence of hypoglycemia was 10 % in asymptomatic, healthy term newborns.
