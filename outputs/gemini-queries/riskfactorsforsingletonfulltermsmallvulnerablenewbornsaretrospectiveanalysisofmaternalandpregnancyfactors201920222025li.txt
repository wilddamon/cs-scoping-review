RESULTS: From 2019 to 2022, the incidence rates of SVN, SGA, and LBW in the study population showed a modest increase, with the SVN incidence in the population rising by 0.73 % in 2022 compared with 2019. CONCLUSION: Improving maternal nutritional status and enhancing surveillance of fetal growth in high-risk populations may help reduce the incidence of full-term SVN.
