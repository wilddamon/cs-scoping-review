Objective(s): The objective of the study was to find out incidence and to determine fetomaternal outcome in elderly primigravida. Material(s) and Method(s): This was a cross sectional study conducted in the Department of obstetrics and gynaecology,MGMMC,Indore from march 2019 to march 2020 for a period of 1 year.
