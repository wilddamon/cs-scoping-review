METHODS: Patients hospitalized in Germany between January 1, 2005, and December 31, 2018, with OBPI as their primary diagnosis were identified using the ICD-10 codes P14.0 (Erb palsy), P14.1 (Klumpke palsy), and P14.3 (other brachial plexus birth injuries).
