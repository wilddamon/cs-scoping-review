"Objective: This study aimed to detect the proteins and metabolites in the cervicovaginal fluid to observe their relationship with the occurrence of placenta accreta spectrum and the potential biomarkers with predictive value. The severity of PAS was evaluated by ultrasound and MRI scoring system and confirmed by the intraoperative findings or pathological examination. We used 4D label-free quantitative proteomics and untargeted metabolomics to identify the proteins and metabolites in cervicovaginal fluid, and analyzed the functions of differential expressed proteins or metabolites in PAS by multi-omics combined with bioinformatics analysis.
