Method(s): An institution-based case-control study was conducted in Dilla town public health facilities, Southern Ethiopia. Data were collected through face-to-face interviews and record reviews. Consecutive and systematic random sampling was used to recruit cases and controls, respectively. Conclusion(s): This study found that no antenatal care follow-up, instrumental mode of delivery, pregnancy-induced hypertension, prolonged second stage of labor, and non-vertex presentation were all independently associated with low fifth-minute Apgar scores.
