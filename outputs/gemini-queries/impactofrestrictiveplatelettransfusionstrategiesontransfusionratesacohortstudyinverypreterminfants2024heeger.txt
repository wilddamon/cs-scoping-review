Study Design and Methods: This retrospective observational cohort study included all very preterm infants (born <32 weeks' gestation) admitted to a neonatal intensive care unit between 2004 and 2022, divided into three epochs. The percentage of infants receiving at least one transfusion above 50 x 109/L in Epoch I, II, and III was 51.3% (59/115), 17.7% (17/96), and 20.0% (5/25; p <.001).
