"Introduction: Investigation of the maternal to fetal transfer of oxytocin across the dually perfused term human placenta. Methods: Human placentae obtained from term singleton pregnancies were utilized in a dual recirculating model of ex vivo placental perfusion. Six placentae from women delivering by elective cesarean at term were perfused, one blank and five with the test substance synthetic oxytocin (0.8 ng/mL) (OX) added to the maternal perfusate for 180 min. Antipyrine was used as positive control to validate overlap of the maternal and fetal circuits. The concentration of OX was determined by radioimmunoassay. Results: A fall in maternal concentration of OX was seen throughout the experiment.
