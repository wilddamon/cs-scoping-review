"Background: Out of 100 pregnancies, approximately 15 to 25 women have vaginal bleeding in first trimester. Aim(s): The present study evaluated the maternal and perinatal outcomes in women with first trimester vaginal bleeding. Material(s) and Method(s): The present study is a cross sectional study which was conducted between February 2017 to March 2019. 50 pregnant women with vaginal bleeding in the first trimester of pregnancy were admitted in Department of Gynecology and Obstetrics.
