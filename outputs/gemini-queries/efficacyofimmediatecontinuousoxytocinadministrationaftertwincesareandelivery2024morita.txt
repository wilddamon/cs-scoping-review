METHODS: This retrospective case-control study was conducted between April 2014 and March 2024, and it included 156 women with twin pregnancies. There was no significant difference in the total blood loss within 24 hours after CD and the incidence of any adverse events within 24 hours after CD, such as PPH, shock, additional procedures to control bleeding, and blood transfusion. Although the difference was not significant, the incidence of lochia exceeding 500 g was lower in the oxytocin versus the control group (4.8% and 26.4%, respectively; OR: 0.14 (95% CI, 0.02 to 1.17); P = 0.07).
