Medical records of 250 women diagnosed with GDM who delivered at the hospital from 2017-2022 were retrospectively analyzed. Data included maternal demographics, glycemic control strategies and pregnancy outcomes. Statistical analyses were performed to compare outcomes between GDM patients and the general obstetric population.
