This study was conducted to determine the incidence of successful vaginal birth after one CS and the associated factors in public health facilities in southern Ethiopia. METHODS: An institutional-based cross-sectional study was conducted from June 10, 2020, to December 10, 2021, on laboring women, and the data were collected using a pretested interviewer-administered questionnaire and a data extraction sheet.
