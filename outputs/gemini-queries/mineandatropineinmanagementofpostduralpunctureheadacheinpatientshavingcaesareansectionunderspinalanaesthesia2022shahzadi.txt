"Introduction: caesarean section under spinal anaesthesia is one of the most common procedures performed in obstetrics. Post dural puncture headache is one of the most common complications of this form of anaesthesia and impacts the life of patients in post-partum period. Objective(s): the purpose of this study is to find out efficacy of intravenous administration of neostigmine/atropine in the management of post dural puncture headaches in females undergoing caesarean section under spinal anaesthesia. Method(s): it is a randomized control, prospective, double blinded study which held over a span of 26 months. Sample of the study was divided into P and N group. N group was intravenously administered a specific dose of neostigmine/atropine while P group was given a placebo. The patients were observed for 72 hours and the intensity of pain was assessed using visual analogue pain scale. Result(s): this study has suggested that neostigmine/ atropine has rapid onset of action in providing analgesic effect to patients. Moreover, this therapy provides early relief from pain.
