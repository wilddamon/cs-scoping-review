The literature regarding this issue is not consensual, and the available studies are relatively small. All women who delivered vaginally >=37 weeks, at our institute, between 2008 and 2017 were included. Patients who underwent cesarean delivery or who were treated with prophylactic antibiotics, or had a fever >=38.0 degC prior to the first VE were excluded. Cases of intrauterine fetal death, known malformations, or missing data were excluded as well. The cohort was divided according to the number of VEs performed: up to 4 VEs (n = 9716), 5-6 VEs (n = 4624), 7-8 VEs (n = 2999), and 9 or more VEs (n = 4844). RESULTS: Overall, 22,183 women were included in the study.
