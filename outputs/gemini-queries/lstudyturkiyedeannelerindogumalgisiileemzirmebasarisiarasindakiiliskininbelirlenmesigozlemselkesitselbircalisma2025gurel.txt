"Objective: To investigate the relationship between birth mothers' perceptions of their birth experiences and postpartum breastfeeding success. Method: The sample of this descriptive and observational study consisted of mothers who underwent either vaginal delivery or unplanned cesarean section in a maternity hospital in Ankara between April 1, 2022 and May 1, 2022.
