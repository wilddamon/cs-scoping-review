Material(s) and Method(s): In this prospective randomized comparative study conducted at the department of obstetrics and gynecology, we aimed to evaluate the potential advantages of ropivacaine over bupivacaine in terms of obstetric outcomes for parturients undergoing labor epidural analgesia.
