METHODOLOGY: Seventy-six pregnant women with severe preeclampsia (BP >=160/110 mmHg) received IV labetalol for hypertension control, with continuous CTG monitoring for foetal well-being. CONCLUSION: While IV labetalol is already widely recommended in guidelines, this study adds value by evaluating its impact on real- time foetal monitoring through CTG in a local clinical context.
