Logistic regression analysis was used to develop a risk score, which was derived from b coefficients of the significant variables. A receiver-operator curve was plotted to determine the optimal cut-off score for predicting NICU admission. RESULTS: A total of 98 infants (21.5%) in the development cohort were admitted to the NICU.
