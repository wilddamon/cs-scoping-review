Aims and Objectives: We aimed at to evaluating the safety and efficacy of prostaglandin (PGE2) gel for induction of labour in previous c-section delivery and correlate Bishop's Score with pregnancy outcome in women attempting VBAC. Materials and Methods: This is a prospective study of women admitted in Rajkiya Mahila Chikitsalya, Ajmer over a period of one year from July 2011 to June 2012 with singleton live pregnancy of >34 weeks of gestational age with previous one caesarean delivery and willing for VBAC.
