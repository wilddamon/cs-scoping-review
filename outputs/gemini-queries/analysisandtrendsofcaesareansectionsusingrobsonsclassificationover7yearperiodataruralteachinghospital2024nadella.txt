The objective of this study was to determine the caesarean section rates to analyse trends of caesarean section and classify according to Robson's categories. METHODS: This cross-sectional study was conducted from 2016 to 2022. A total of 3176 women who underwent caesarean section were grouped under Robson's ten-group classification system. CONCLUSION: Pregnant women with prior uterine scars contribute more to caesarean section rates according to Robson's categories.
