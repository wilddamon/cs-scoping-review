"OBJECTIVE: Our objective was to study the differences in haemoglobin (Hb) at birth in dichorionic (DC) versus monochorionic (MC) twins in relation to birth order and mode of delivery. METHODS: All consecutive DC twin pregnancies and uncomplicated MC twin pregnancies with two live-born twins delivered at our centre were included in this retrospective cohort study. RESULTS: A total of 300 DC and 290 MC twin pairs were included. CONCLUSIONS: Second-born DC and MC twins delivered vaginally have higher Hb levels at birth compared with first-born twins.
