Aims: The purpose of this study was to assess the impact of psychoprophylaxis courses on childbirth outcome and its contribution to initiation of breastfeeding. Methodology: This was a cross-sectional study. A representative sample of mothers who gave birth in two public and three private maternity hospitals in the city of Larissa, central Greece was used. Results:The majority of the sample was aged 30-39 years old.
