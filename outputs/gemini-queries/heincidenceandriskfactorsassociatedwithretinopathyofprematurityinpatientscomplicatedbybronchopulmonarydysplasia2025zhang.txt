"""abstract ID: analysisoftheincidenceandriskfactorsassociatedwithretinopathyofprematurityinpatientscomplicatedbybronchopulmonarydysplasia2025zhang
"Background: Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) and retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) are common disorders in preterm infants. This study aimed to analyze the difference in the incidence of type 1 or type 2 ROP between the BPD and non-BPD groups in preterm infants of different gestational ages and birth weights, and to explore the risk factors for the combination of type 1 or type 2 ROP in infants with BPD. Methods: A retrospective analysis was performed on the clinical data of preterm infants hospitalized from January 2019 to December 2023. All preterm infants were divided into BPD and non-BPD groups based on the 2018 BPD diagnostic criteria; ROP was classified according to the Early Treatment for Retinopathy of Prematurity (ETROP). Results: The incidence of infant type 1 and type 2 ROP was significantly higher in the BPD group than in the non-BPD group (all p < 0.001). BPD infants with gestational age < 28 weeks (OR12.25) and birth weight < 1000g(OR9.67) have the highest risk of type 1 or type 2 ROP. Logistic regression analysis showed that lower gestational age and birth weight, longer mechanical ventilation, and more blood transfusions within the first 28 postnatal days increased the risk of combined type 1 or type 2 ROP in infants with BPD. Conclusion: BPD has a significant impact on the occurrence of ROP. Low gestational age, low birth weight, the duration of mechanical ventilation and the number of blood transfusions within the first 28 postnatal days are all risk factors for BPD infants with combined type 1 or type 2 ROP.
