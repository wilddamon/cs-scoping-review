In Saudi Arabia, the diversity of birthing women underscores the need for midwives to provide culturally competent, respectful, and individualized care. Method(s): A quantitative cross-sectional study was conducted using a validated cultural sensitivity questionnaire. Data were collected online through purposive sampling from women who had given birth in the past 12 months. Result(s): women reported moderate to high perceptions of cultural sensitivity. Age and nationality significantly influenced perception scores (p < 0.05).
