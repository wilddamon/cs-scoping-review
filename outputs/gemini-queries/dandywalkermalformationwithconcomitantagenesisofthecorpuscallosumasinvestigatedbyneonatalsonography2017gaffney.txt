"Dandy-Walker malformation and agenesis of the corpus callosum, although uncommon among all births, are commonly seen in association with one another due to the malformations' effects on the central nervous system. However, sonography also plays a valuable role in the postnatal follow-up evaluation of the brain and other body systems affected by Dandy-Walker malformation and its associated abnormalities, thus making it imperative for all sonographers to be familiar with their postnatal sonographic appearance.
