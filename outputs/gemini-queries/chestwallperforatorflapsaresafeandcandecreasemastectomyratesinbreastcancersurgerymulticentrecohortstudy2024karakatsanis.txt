The aim of this international multicentre collaborative study was to explore practice patterns and outcomes in high-volume centres from different countries. Methods: Patients with both pre-invasive and invasive breast cancer treated at the Uppsala University Hospital in Uppsala, Sweden, the Royal Marsden Hospital in London, UK, and the Westmead Breast Cancer Institute in Sydney, Australia, were included in this study.
