"Background: Antepartum hemorrhage is one of the leading causes of obstetrical hemorrhage and maternal death.APH is defined as any bleeding from female genital tract between fetal viability, and delivery of fetus. Fetal viability in India is taken as gestation of more than 28 wks. About 2-5% of all pregnancies are complicated by APH. Maternal and perinatal morbidity and mortality associated with APH can be reduced significantly by aggressive expectant management. Objective(s): 1) To study the prevalence of APH 2) To study the maternal and fetal outcome in APH. Method(s): The retrospective study was conducted in SMGS hospital, Jammu from January, 2022 to August, 2022. 62 cases of APH with gestational age >28 weeks were included. Result(s): Out of 62, 83.87% was PP followed by 16.13% was AP. Maximum patients belonged to 20-29yrs of age group (56.45%). Out of 62 cases, 7 were IUD, 5 stillbirth and 5 required NICU admission. Anemia was the most common complication in APH. Conclusion(s): PP was the most common cause of APH, followed by AP. Early intervention and available resuscitation facilities can reduce maternal mortality and morbidity.
