"Objective: To assess the diagnostic performance of obstetric two-dimensional ultrasound in placental implantation-related diseases and its impact on clinical decision-making. Method(s): Seventy women with suspected placental implantation and placenta previa (August 2022-August 2024; mean age 28.5 +/- 4.2 years; mean parity 3.0 +/- 1.1) underwent routine 2D ultrasound; 28 cases with inconclusive ultrasound were supplemented by MRI. Ultrasound criteria included placental overlap of the internal cervical os, lower-edge coverage, heterogeneous echogenicity, anechoic zones, indistinct placental-myometrial interface, and abnormal vascular signals. Final diagnoses were confirmed by surgical pathology or clinical follow-up. Result(s): Ultrasound achieved 86.0 % sensitivity, 72.0 % specificity, 84.1 % positive predictive value, and 75.0 % negative predictive value for placental implantation. Thirty-seven true implantation cases were identified, of which 77.8 % required surgical management. Key ultrasound signs-internal os coverage, heterogeneous echotexture, and abundant blood flow signals-showed correlation rates >86.5 %. Diagnostic agreement between ultrasound and MRI was 75.0 %. Conclusion(s): Obstetric ultrasound delivers high-accuracy detection of placental implantation by delineating placental-cervical relationships and vascular features.
