The present study was designed to identify the clinical efficacy and other heath related outcome of oral and intravenous labetalol for treating preeclampsia on maternal and fetal distress. This study was performed on 75 pregnant women in which 50 women were suffered with preeclampsia and 25 women were included as controlled group. Subjects were selected on the basis of inclusion and exclusion criteria. Result(s): Oral and IV formulation of labetalol reduced mean systolic blood pressure and mean diastolic blood pressure significantly (P<0.05) but there is no significant difference in their clinical effect.
