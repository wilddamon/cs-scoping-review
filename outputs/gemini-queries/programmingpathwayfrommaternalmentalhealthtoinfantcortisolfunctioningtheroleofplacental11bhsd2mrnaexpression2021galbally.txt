This study examined the pathway from antenatal mental health via placental 11b-HSD2 mRNA to cortisol regulation in the infant offspring. At term, placental tissue was collected within 30 min of birth from 52 participants meeting current criteria for a depressive disorder, and 184 control participants. Depressive disorders were diagnosed using the SCID-IV. In addition, antidepressant use, depressive and anxiety symptoms were measured in early and late pregnancy. Our findings suggest that the relationship between maternal antenatal depression and anxiety and infant cortisol reactivity is mediated through placental 11b-HSD2 mRNA expression.
