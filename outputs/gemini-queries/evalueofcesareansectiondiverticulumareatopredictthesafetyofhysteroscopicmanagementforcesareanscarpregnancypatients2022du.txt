Patients were divided into control group and testing group according to surgical outcomes. Preoperative indicators were compared between the two groups, including a novel indicator, cesarean section diverticulum (CSD) area. RESULTS: Univariate analysis identified five statistically significant (P < 0.05) factors associated with hysteroscopy failure including a large CSD area.
