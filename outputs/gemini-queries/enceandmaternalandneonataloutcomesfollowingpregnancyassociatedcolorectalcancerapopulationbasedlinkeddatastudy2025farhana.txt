The study group comprised all women diagnosed with colorectal cancer during pregnancy (gestational CRC) or postpartum (postpartum CRC). RESULTS: A total of 123 women were diagnosed with PACRC (22 gestational, 101 postpartum), and 1 786 078 women were in the no-cancer group. The incidence of PACRC was 6.9/100 000 women giving birth.
