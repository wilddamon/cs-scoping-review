Method(s): The study was carried out in the villages of the rural district of Tapi and slum areas of Surat Municipal Corporation in India utilizing a cross-sectional study design. A standard 30 cluster sampling technique was utilized to select total 216 rural and 377 urban slum mothers delivered in last one year. Data analysis was done by Epi Info 2007.
