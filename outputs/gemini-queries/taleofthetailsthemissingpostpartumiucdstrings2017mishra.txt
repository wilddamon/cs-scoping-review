OBJECTIVE: To study the incidence, management, clinical outcome of missing strings cases in post-placental and intra-cesarean IUCD. MATERIALS AND METHODS: This study was a retrospective observational study, carried out in the district of Balangir, Odisha, India. Status of women who had post-placental and intra-cesarean IUCD insertion in various institutions between January 2010 and December 2012 having follow-up as per the protocol was taken for the study.
