"Objective: To assess stabilization, respiratory care and survival of extremely low birth weight (ELBW, <1000 g at birth) infants requiring emergency transfer to tertiary NICUs on the first day of life. Study design: Retrospective cohort study of 55 ELBW infants transported by a dedicated neonatal transport service over a 65-month period. Ventilator data were downloaded computationally. Results: 95% of infants were intubated and received surfactant prior to transfer. Median expired tidal volume was 5.0 mL/kg (interquartile range: 4.6-6.2 mL/kg).
