Study design A prospective, observational study was conducted assessing a cohort of women undergoing benign gynaecologic surgery between April 2015 and June 2015. The ultrasound assessments were validated against direct visualization during surgery. The effectiveness of the slide test as a screening technique was examined by recording the presence, extent, and severity of adhesions observed intraoperatively; this was scored using a modified version of the American Fertility Society (mAFS) system. Results In total, 144 women completed the study, 33 (22.9%) of whom had previously undergone intra-abdominal surgery.
