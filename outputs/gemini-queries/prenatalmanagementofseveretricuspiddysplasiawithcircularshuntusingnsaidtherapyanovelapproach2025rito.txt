"Tricuspid valve dysplasia (TVD) is a rare congenital heart defect that can lead to a life-threatening fetal circulation compromise. We report a case of a fetus with TVD diagnosed at 22 weeks gestation, characterized by severe tricuspid regurgitation, retrograde flow through the ductus arteriosus, and pulmonary regurgitation (circular shunt). Prenatal management with nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), both ibuprofen (400-600 mg, every 8 h) and indomethacin (50 mg, every 8 h), was initiated at 28 weeks gestation, and continued until birth, to promote ductal constriction and antegrade flow through it, thus terminating or reducing the circular shunt. Despite the challenging management with NSAIDs, it was possible to achieve a near term delivery and conservative neonatal management.
