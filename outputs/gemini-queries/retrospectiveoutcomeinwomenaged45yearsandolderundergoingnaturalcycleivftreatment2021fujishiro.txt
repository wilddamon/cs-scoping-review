Design: Retrospective investigation of outcomes in women aged 45 years and older, who underwent natural cycle IVF treatment between 2009 and 2018 in a single assisted reproduction clinic with the aim of reporting several successful outcomes. No significant difference was found in serum FSH level on day 3 between those with and without positive beta-HCG test results. Conclusions: These findings suggest that natural cycle IVF treatment could be an option for older poor responders in countries that do not permit egg donation.
