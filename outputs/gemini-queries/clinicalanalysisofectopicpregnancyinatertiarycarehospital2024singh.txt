Aims of this study to determine Clinical presentation and risk factors of Ectopic pregnancy to highlight and drawing attention for early diagnosis and prompt management. Method(s): Present retrospective study was conducted in Obstetrics and Gynaecology department of Darbhanga Medical College and Hospital, Laheriasarai, Bihar from September 2023 to August 2024.
