"Objectives: To investigate whether, in pregnancies complicated by spontaneous preterm delivery, fetal fraction of cell-free DNA (cfDNA) in maternal plasma at 11-13 weeks' gestation is altered and if this measurement could be useful in the prediction of preterm delivery. Methods: Fetal fraction of cfDNA was measured at 10 + 0 to 13 + 6 weeks' gestation in 3169 pregnancies, 3066 (96.7%) that delivered at >= 37 weeks and 103 (3.3%) with spontaneous delivery at < 37 weeks, including 21 that delivered at < 34 weeks and 82 that delivered at 34-37 weeks.
