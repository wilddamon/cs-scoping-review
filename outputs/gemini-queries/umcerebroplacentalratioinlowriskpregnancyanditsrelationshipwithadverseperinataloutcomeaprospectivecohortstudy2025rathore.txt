METHODS: This was a prospective cohort study at tertiary center in western India which included low risk singleton pregnancies between 35 and 37+6 weeks with cephalic presentation. Ultrasound was done between 35 and 37+6 weeks gestation including fetal biometry and Doppler for CPR calculation. Lower birth weight was observed in 45.45 % of women in the abnormal CPR group, while 21.36 % of women had low birth weight in the normal CPR group (p-value=0.006).
