Maternal infection/chorioamnionitis is associated with neonatal neurodevelopment, cerebral palsy, and periventricular leucomalacia. In our study, we aimed to find out the rate of PROM associated-histologic chorioamnionitis (HCA) and corioamnionitis associated early neonatal brain damage findings with cranial USG. Material(s) and Method(s): One hundred and fifteen neonates born in Zeynep Kamil Maternity and Children Hospital were enrolled to the study.
