Method(s): A cross-sectional study was conducted among 600 toddlers aged 16-30 months and their caregivers in urban and rural parts of Tumkur. The Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers-Revised (M-CHAT-R) questionnaire was used to assess all participants. Result(s): Out of 600 children screened, six were positive with ASD-like symptoms. Conclusion(s): ASD signs can appear early, with diagnoses possible by 12 months. Identifying risk factors can facilitate early detection for at-risk toddlers.
