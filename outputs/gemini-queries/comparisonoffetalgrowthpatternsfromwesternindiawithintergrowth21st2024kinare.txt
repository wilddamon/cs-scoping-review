"Objective To generate longitudinal fetal growth data in an Indian population and compare it with Intergrowth-21st. Mean AC Z-score was lower at 18-22 weeks [-0.32 SD (-0.41,-0.23)] but comparable at 32-35 weeks [0.004 SD (-0.07, 0.07)]. These findings were similar, though measurements were slightly larger, in the 'low-risk' sample. Conclusions This data from healthy and low-risk pregnant women in urban western India indicates that some fetal dimensions and growth trajectories differ significantly from the Intergrowth-21st.
