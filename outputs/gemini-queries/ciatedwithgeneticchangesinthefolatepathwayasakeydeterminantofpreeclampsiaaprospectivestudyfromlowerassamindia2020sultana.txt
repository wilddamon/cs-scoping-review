PE cases {N = 145, mild-PE (n = 108), severe-PE (n = 37)} and 192 normal full-term delivery cases were evaluated for Hcy expression and Vitamin B12 levels and evaluated for association with MTHFR and TYMS polymorphisms in PE pathogenesis. Within the PE delivery group, the combined variant genotypes showed association with higher homocysteine levels both in live birth cases as well in the PE cases with negative pregnancy outcomes. Increased homocysteine levels were associated with combined variant genotypes and were consistent in both term and preterm delivery in PE cases.
