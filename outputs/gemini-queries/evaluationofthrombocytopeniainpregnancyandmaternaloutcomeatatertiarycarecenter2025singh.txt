"Aim and background:Thrombocytopenia poses significant risks during pregnancy, demanding thorough evaluation and management to ensure maternal and fetal well-being. Method(s): We conducted a retrospective analysis of medical records from a cohort of 378 pregnant women. Platelet counts were categorized, and etiologies of thrombocytopenia were identified. We evaluated the frequency of maternal complications and delivery mode. Regression analysis assessed the relationship between specific causes and platelet count. Result(s): Our study revealed that approximately 49.86% of pregnant individuals experienced mild thrombocytopenia, while 42.41 and 16.05% had moderate and severe thrombocytopenia, respectively. Conclusion(s): Thrombocytopenia is prevalent in pregnancy, with gestational thrombocytopenia being the leading cause.
