OBJECTIVE: To compare the accuracy and HR acquisition time of portable Doppler ultrasound (PDU) versus electrocardiogram (ECG) in newborns. METHODS: This multicenter, prospective, observational study across five centers in India between January and September 2017 included neonates more than 34 weeks of gestation (n = 131) delivered by cesarean section.
