"Objective: To compare the frequency of diagnosis, induction of labour, and caesarean section in patients with isolated oligohydramnios diagnosed by amniotic fluid index versus single deepest vertical pocket. Study Design: Randomized controlled trial Place and duration of study: Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Holy Family Hospital, Rawalpindi from March 2020 to August 2020. Materials & Methods: A total of 110 (55 in each group), 18 to 35 years of age of parity <5 were included. Group A females were evaluated by using AFI and group B females were evaluated by using SDVP. Conclusion: This study concluded that the frequency of diagnosis, induction of labor, and caesarean section in patients with isolated oligohydramnios diagnosed by the amniotic fluid index is higher as compared to single deepest vertical pocket.
