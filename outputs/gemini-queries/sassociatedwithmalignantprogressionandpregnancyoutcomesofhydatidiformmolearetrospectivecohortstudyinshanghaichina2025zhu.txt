Descriptive analyses were performed to describe the demographic characteristics, progression to postmolar gestational trophoblastic neoplasia (pGTN), and reproductive outcomes of patients. Univariate and multivariate logistic regression analyses were conducted to evaluate risk factors and develop predictive models for pGTN. Result(s): Of 506 patients with HM, the average age and gestational age at diagnosis were approximately 33 years and 10 weeks, respectively.
