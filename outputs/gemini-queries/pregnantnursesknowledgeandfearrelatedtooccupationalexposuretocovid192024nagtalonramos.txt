Pregnant nurses working during the pandemic were at a higher risk of exposure to COVID-19 and consequent pregnancy complications. Objectives: This study aims to better understand pregnant nurses' knowledge and self-reported fear related to occupational exposure to COVID-19. Key independent variables assessed included age, occupational setting, gestational age, and presence of other children living at home. Dependent variables assessed included infection control knowledge, and self-reported fear in contracting COVID-19 related to their occupation as a nurse and being pregnant. Methods: This study used a quantitative cross-sectional correlational design to investigate pregnant nurses' knowledge and fear about COVID-19 exposure.
