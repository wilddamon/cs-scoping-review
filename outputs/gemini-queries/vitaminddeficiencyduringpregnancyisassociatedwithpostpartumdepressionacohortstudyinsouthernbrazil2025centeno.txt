Methods: This longitudinal study followed mother-child dyads in the city of Pelotas, RS, Brazil. A total of 983 pregnant women were initially recruited, of whom 713 had complete data available for this analysis. Conclusions: These findings highlight the potential role of VitD in maternal mental health and support the importance of monitoring VitD status during pregnancy.
