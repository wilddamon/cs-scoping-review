"Aims: To evaluate the role of triple vessel (umbilical, middle cerebral and uterine artery) color Doppler study in normal and high risk pregnancy in relation to perinatal outcome. Materials and Method: A prospective study was done including ninety-two women with high risk pregnancy and ninety-two normal pregnant women during the year 2012-2013. Doppler examination was done after recording patients history, clinical examination and ultrasound. Mode of delivery, perinatal outcome including birth weight, perinatal death, Apgar score at 1 and 5 minutes were compared. Results: There was significant difference between the Doppler indices of the triple vessels in the study group and control group. In women with abnormal Doppler indices, there was high incidence of caesarean section, low birth weight babies, low Apgar score and high incidence of the neonatal deaths as compared to that of the control group. Conclusion: Triple vessel color Doppler sonography is very useful in high risk pregnancy diagnosis and in predicting perinatal outcome.
