"Neonatal mortality can be define as a death of the new born within the first 28 days of life. The risk of death of child in the first four weeks of life is approximately fifteen times more than any other time before child first birthday. Aim of the study: To predict the risk factors and the causes of neonate mortality among the neonates admitted to neonatal care units in ALDiwaniya maternity and children teaching hospital during the study period. A retrospective study was conducted to review the records of neonatal deaths that took place in neonatal care units in AL- Daiwynia maternity and children teaching Hospital during the year 2019. the demographic data, data concerning pregnancy and delivery and any complication during pregnancy, delivery and puerperium, mode of delivery, place of delivery and causes of neonatal death were taking by data form that designated for this purpose. Among the causes of neonatal death the respiratory distress syndrome was the highest which was found in (57.48%) of cases, followed by birth asphyxia (19.32%), sepsis (13.52%), complications of high hyper bilirubinemia (4.83%), pneumonia (2.17%), cold injury (1.44%) and diarrhea (1.24). Preterm delivery was noticed in (57.49%) of the deceased neonates, 38.16% of them were with appropriate weight for age and gestation, 33.81% with low birth weight, 15.94% with very low birth weight and 12.07% were with extremely low birth weight. The most common causes of neonatal deaths were respiratory distress syndrome, birth asphyxia and sepsis.
