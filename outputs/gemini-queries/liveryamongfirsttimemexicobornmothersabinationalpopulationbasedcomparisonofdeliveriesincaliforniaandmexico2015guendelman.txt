"BACKGROUND: While studies have attributed the favourable birth outcomes of Mexico-born mothers in the USA to a 'healthy immigrant effect' that confers protection to immigrants, a comparison of immigrants with the source population in Mexico has been lacking. Similar patterns were observed when comparing women in the sending states.;CONCLUSIONS: We found no evidence of a 'healthy immigrant effect'.
