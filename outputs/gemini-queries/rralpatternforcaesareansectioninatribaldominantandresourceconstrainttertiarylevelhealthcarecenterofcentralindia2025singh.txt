METHODS: From May to July 2023, Pregnant women requiring caesarean section were invited to participate (estimated sample size: 224). Significant predictors included caesarean section duration < 30 min (p = 0.03) and subcutaneous skin closure (p = 0.04). Pregnant women from Shahdol (aHR = 1.38), Umaria (aHR = 1.10), and other districts (aHR = 2.35) had higher SSI risk than those from Anuppur.
