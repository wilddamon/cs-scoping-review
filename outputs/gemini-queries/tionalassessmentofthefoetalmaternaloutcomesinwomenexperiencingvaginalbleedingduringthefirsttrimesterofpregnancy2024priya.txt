Material(s) and Method(s): The present study is a retrospective study which was conducted in Department of obstetrics and Gynecology, JLNMCH, Bhagalpur, Bihar, India for 9 months. 50 pregnant women with vaginal bleeding in the first trimester of pregnancy were admitted in. All patients whose pregnancy was confirmed were studied chemically. Exclusion criteria was women with diabetes and hypertension with an infertility history or missed obstetrical history. Sonography in 8-10 weeks was conducted on all patients. Follow up of all patients was taken every two weeks in the first 6 months of pregnancy, weekly in 7th and 8th months and twice per week in last month of pregnancy.
