"Background: One of the most widely utilised substances toaugmentlabour is oxytocin. However, there is no universal agreement on the best dosage schedule. Method(s): This prospective comparative observational study was conducted in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Durgapur Steel Plant Hospital, West Bengal, over a period of two years (2020-2022). The mean induction-to-delivery interval was significantly shorter with high-dose oxytocin (4.05 vs 9.21 hours; p<0.01). Maternal and neonatal complications, including postpartum haemorrhage, tachysystole, Apgar scores, and NICU admissions, were comparable between the groups.
