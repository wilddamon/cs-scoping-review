"Background: The aim of our study was to evaluate the accuracy of Three Dimensional Tomographic Ultrasound Imaging (3D-TUI) and 3D power Doppler using Alalfy simple criteria in the diagnosis of placenta previa and PAS (Placenta accrete spectrum) with differentiation of placenta previa (non-adherent placenta) from PAS disorders and determination of its subtypes (Accreta, increta, and percreta). Method(s): A prospective observational study that included 90 pregnant women was made at Algezeera Hospital, Egypt.
