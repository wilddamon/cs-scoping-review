"Background: Postpartum hemorrhage (PPH) is one of the important causes of maternal morbidity and mortality in all parts of the world. Cesarean section is common but it is accompanied by a lot of blood loss. Tranexamic acid (TXA), an antifibrinolytic Drug, has demonstrated potential in helping to decrease surgical blood loss during operations. The purpose of the study was to assess the efficacy and safety of prophylactic TXA in the reduction of intraoperative and postoperative blood loss during cesarean sections. Method(s):One hundred women who were having elective cesarean section were put through a prospective, double-blind, randomized controlled trial. Participants would be randomly grouped into Group A (n=50), in which 1g of intravenous TXA would be administered 10-15 minutes before skin incision, or Group B (n=50), where the placebo would be used instead of TXA. The main outcome measures included intraoperative, postoperative, and total blood loss. Secondary outcomes comprised transfusion needs, hemoglobin decrease, operation time, recovery measures, and adverse events occurrence. SPSS v25 was used to analyze statistics. Result(s):Group A (TXA) demonstrated significantly lower intraoperative (452.3 mL vs. 682.5 mL), postoperative (185.6 mL vs. 302.8 mL), and total blood loss (637.9 mL vs. 985.3 mL) compared to placebo (p<0.001). Transfusion requirements and mean hemoglobin drop were also significantly reduced in the TXA group. No thromboembolic events were observed in either group. TXA was associated with shorter hospital stays, faster ambulation, and comparable rates of minor complications, indicating a favorable safety and efficacy profile. Conclusion(s):The prophylactic use of tranexamic acid is efficient in decreasing both intraoperative and postoperative blood loss during cesarean sections. This decreases the transfusion requirements substantively and enhances postoperative recovery. It has evidence of a good safety profile and does not increase thromboembolic risk. Prophylactic administration of TXA during cesarean section especially in high-risk women of PPH could be an advantageous approach to improve maternal outcomes and perioperative care.Copyright (c)2025Int.
