"OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the effectiveness and risks of probiotics among infants born before 34 weeks' gestation and with a birth weight less than 1000 g. METHODS: A population-based retrospective cohort study of infants born before 34 weeks' gestation and admitted to 33 Canadian Neonatal Network (CNN) units between January 1, 2016, and December 31, 2022. Probiotic sepsis occurred in 27 (1.4/1000) infants born before 34 weeks' gestation and 20 (4/1000) infants with a birth weight less than 1000 g. Three infants with probiotic sepsis died, with probiotic sepsis deemed a possible cause in 2 cases.
