Pain at the injection site of lumbar puncture (OR 4; CL 95% CI 1.2-13; p = 0.025) and the atmosphere in the theatre (OR 4.1; CL 95% CI 1.1-15.5; p = 0.02) were determinant for future choice of spinal anaesthesia.
