"OBJECTIVE: To identify risk factors for cesarean scar defect (CSD)-associated postmenstrual spotting and the possible underlying mechanisms. STUDY DESIGN: A retrospective study. Transvaginal ultrasound was performed in patients with a history of cesarean section to identify CSDs and other uterine diseases. Clinical data of patients with CSDs were collected, including measurements of serum sex and thy- roid hormones. In patients with CSD-associated postmenstrual spotting, hysteroscopy was performed. RESULT(S): Enrolled were 121 women with CSDs detected by transvaginal sonography without other uterine diseases or endocrine disorders; 91 of these patients had postmenstrual spotting. Binary logistic regression analyses determined that CSD-associated postmenstrual spotting was related to a retroflexed uterus, closed cervix prior to cesarean, and cesarean performed at a town/ county-level hospital as opposed to a province-level hos pital. Hysteroscopic findings of CSDs in patients with postmenstrual spotting included polyps, dilated superficial capillaries, residual suture materials, thickened and folded endometrium in the scar, blood accumulation in the uterine cavity, and scar oozing.
