Methods: We performed a retrospective cohort study of singleton pregnancies. Results: Group F had an earlier PE diagnosis (32.5 +- 4.9 vs. 36.7 +- 3.5 weeks, p < 0.01) and delivery (33.7 +- 4.5 vs. 37.5 +- 3.1 weeks, p < 0.01) than Group M. Conclusions: Our results suggest that the presence of FGR on PE diagnosis is associated with the development of severe maternal symptoms as much as that of maternal organ dysfunction at diagnosis, and it may be reasonable to include FGR in PE diagnostic criteria.
