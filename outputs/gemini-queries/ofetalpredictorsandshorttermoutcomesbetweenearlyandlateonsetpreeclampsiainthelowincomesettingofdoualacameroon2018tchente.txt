"Objective: To describe and compare materno-fetal predictors and short-term outcomes of early onset pre-eclampsia (EOPE) and late onset pre-eclampsia (LOPE) in Douala, Cameroon. To determine maternal predictors, sociodemographic and medical data were recorded using a pretested questionnaire. Pregnancy outcomes, and maternal and fetal adverse events were recorded. Conclusion: In the low-income setting of Douala, there was a higher rate of LOPE than EOPE. Factors associated with EOPE and LOPE varied, and outcomes were worse for women with EOPE.
