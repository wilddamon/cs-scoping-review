"Background: The aim is to evaluate the retroplacental Hematoma in first trimester hemorrhage. Material(s) and Method(s): First group included 100 pregnant women, and second group as controls included 400 pregnant women. In contrast, the uneventful pregnancy was more frequent in subchroionic group (66%) compared to those with retroplacental hematomas (38%) (p<0.001).
