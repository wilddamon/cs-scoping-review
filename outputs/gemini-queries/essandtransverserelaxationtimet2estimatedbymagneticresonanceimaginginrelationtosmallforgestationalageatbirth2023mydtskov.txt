Methods: A mixed cohort of 85 pregnancies was retrieved from the placental MRI database at the study hospital. MRI was performed in a 1.5 T system at gestational weeks 15-41. Conclusion: Among the MRI parameters investigated in this study, placental T2* was the only independent predictor of SGA in a multi-variate model.
