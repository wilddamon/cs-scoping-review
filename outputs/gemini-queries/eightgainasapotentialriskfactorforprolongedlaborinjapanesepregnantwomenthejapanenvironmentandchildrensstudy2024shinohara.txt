"AIM: This study aimed to determine whether excessive maternal weight gain during pregnancy was associated with a higher risk of prolonged labor.;METHODS: We analyzed the data regarding maternal weight gain during pregnancy for the participants of Japan Environment and Children's Study (JECS), which is an ongoing nationwide prospective birth cohort study in Japan.
