"Background: During pregnancy, hyperfiltration and other factors are hypothesized to contribute to the progression of kidney disease in women with Alport syndrome. To evaluate the status of kidney disease, clinical data from mothers with Alport syndrome in China and Europe over the pregnancy were analyzed. Methods: This retrospective observational study collected data to evaluate proteinuria, kidney function and Alport stage prior to, during, and after pregnancy, respectively. Results: A total of 74 women were enrolled, 82% of them with X-linked Alport syndrome and 11% with autosomal Alport syndrome (unknown in 5 patients). Progression of Alport stage after pregnancy was observed in 42% of the women, 31% remained in stage 0-1, and 23% remained in stage 2.
