"Objectives: The aim of the study was to determine biochemical factors and maternofetal outcome in women with jaundice during pregnancy and to study its association with jaundice in pregnancy. Method(s): It was an observational study done among 60 pregnant patients with abnormal liver functioning. Laboratory parameters were studied which mainly includes liver function tests. SPSS (Version 22.0) was used for analysis. Result(s): HELLP syndrome was the most common etiology (45%). Decreased levels of hemoglobin observed in 75%, increased total leukocyte count observed in 26%, and low platelet count observed in 34%, respectively. Among mothers, most common adverse outcome was requirement of emergency lower segment cesarean section (45%).
