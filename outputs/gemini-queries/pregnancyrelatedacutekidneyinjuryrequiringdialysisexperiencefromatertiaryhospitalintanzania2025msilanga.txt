Despite this burden, data on pregnancy-related acute kidney injury requiring dialysis (PRAKI-D) in Tanzania are limited. This study describes the clinical characteristics and in-hospital outcomes of these patients at a tertiary hospital. Methods: We conducted a retrospective cohort study at Muhimbili National Hospital, including all pregnant and post-partum women, defined as those who were pregnant, in labor, or within six weeks postpartum with PRAKI who received hemodialysis between January 2022 and December 2024.
