Cardiac rhabdomyomas are the most frequent neonatal primary cardiac tumors in TSC, often resolving spontaneously but sometimes necessitating intervention due to arrhythmias or flow obstruction. This case report describes a term male neonate with TSC alternating between baseline bradycardia and supraventricular tachycardia (SVT) episodes. Imaging revealed multiple cardiac rhabdomyomas and cortical tuberomas, confirming the TSC diagnosis with a TSC2 gene mutation.
