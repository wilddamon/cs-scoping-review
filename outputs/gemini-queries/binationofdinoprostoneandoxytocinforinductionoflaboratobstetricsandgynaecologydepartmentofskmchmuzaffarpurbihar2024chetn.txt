"Objective: To compare the safety, efficacy, cost and fetal outcome of misoprostol with that of combination of dinoprostone and oxytocin for induction of labor. Fetal distress was more common in the study group than in the control group but not significantly so. Neonatal outcome was comparable in the two groups. The cost of therapy was significantly less in the study group. (P<0.001).
