"True experimental pretest-posttest control group research design was conducted in the antenatal clinic of the host institution among 60 postpartum caesarean mothers. The pretest and posttest mean scores of newborn feeding adequacy in the interventional group were 2.23+-0.67 and 7.73+-1.70. The mean difference score was 5.50. The calculated paired 't' test value of t = 17.544 was statistically significant at p<0.001 level.
