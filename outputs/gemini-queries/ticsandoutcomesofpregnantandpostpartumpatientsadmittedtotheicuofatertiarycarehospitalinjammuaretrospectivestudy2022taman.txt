Pregnancy-related physiological changes and certain disorders can quickly deteriorate a patient's health, necessitating intensive care unit (ICU) treatment. The present study has been objectively conducted to examine risk factors, clinical characteristics, and outcomes of pregnant and postpartum patients admitted to the ICU. Method(s): The present retrospective study was conducted at the post graduate Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, SMGS Hospital Jammu w.e.f August 2021 to July 2022. A total of 138 obstetric patients were admitted to our ICU between August 2021 and July 2022, accounting for 0.7% of hospital deliveries. The maternal mortality rate was 8.6%. Conclusion(s): The present study demonstrated that the commonest risk factors for ICU admissions are obstetric haemorrhage and hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, severe anemia, heart disease, sepsis, rupture uterus and rupture ectopic pregnancy.
