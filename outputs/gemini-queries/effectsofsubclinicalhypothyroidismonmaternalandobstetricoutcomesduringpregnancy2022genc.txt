Lower gestational age at delivery (OR: 1; 95% CI, 0.93-1.88, p = 0.016), and higher preterm delivery rates (OR: 0.99; 95% CI, 0.96-1.01, p = 0.003) were found to be statistically significant in multivariate risk analysis.
