Multivariate logistic regression confirmed that menstrual period duration (OR = 2.074, p = 0.003), oligomenorrhea (OR = 12.487, p < 0.001), and total testosterone (OR = 1.067, p = 0.003) were independently associated with MA risk in PCOS patients. Conclusion: This study identifies menstrual period duration, oligomenorrhea, and total testosterone as independent risk factors for MA in women with PCOS.
