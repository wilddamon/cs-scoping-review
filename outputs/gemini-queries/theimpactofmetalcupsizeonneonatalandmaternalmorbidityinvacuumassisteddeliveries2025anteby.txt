Primary neonatal outcomes were a birth trauma composite outcome (brachial plexus injury, cephalohematoma, subgaleal or subdural hematoma) and a severe neonatal composite outcome (neonatal intensive care unit admission, 5-min Apgar score less than 7, umbilical artery pH less than 7.1, intraventricular hemorrhage, hypoxic ischemic encephalopathy, or neonatal convulsions).
