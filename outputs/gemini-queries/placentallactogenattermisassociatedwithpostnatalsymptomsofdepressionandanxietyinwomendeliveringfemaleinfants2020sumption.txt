"Background: Placental endocrine insufficiency may increase the risk of depression and anxiety during pregnancy and/or after birth. Method: Caucasian women with singleton, term pregnancies recruited at their pre-surgical appointment prior to an elective caesarean section (ELCS) were studied. Serum hPL levels were measured by ELISA in maternal blood collected at the pre-surgical appointment. In mothers who had boys, there were no associations between hPL and mood scores at any time point. Conclusion: Low hPL at term associated with postnatal depression and anxiety symptoms exclusively in mothers of girls. Insufficiency in hPL may contribute to maternal mood symptoms.
