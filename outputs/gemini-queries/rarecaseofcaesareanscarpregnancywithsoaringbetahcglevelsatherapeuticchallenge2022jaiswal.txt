"Caesarean Scar Pregnancy (CSP) is a life-threatening form of ectopic pregnancy characterised by abnormal implantation of embryo within the myometrium and fibrous tissues of a previous scar on the uterus. This is the case report of a 24-year-old pregnant lady who underwent previous caesarean section presented at eight weeks gestation with bleeding per-vaginum. Ultrasound of pelvis revealed an intrauterine gestational sac with a live foetus at the site of previous caesarean scar with very high levels of serum beta-human Chorionic Gonadotropin (beta-hCG) of 88789 mIU/mL. To prevent surgical complications, a conservative approach was employed, involving injection methotrexate and uterine artery embolisation. Serial measurements of serum beta-hCG and transvaginal sonography were used for monitoring. Treatment was successful and the beta-hCG levels returned to normal on day 48.
