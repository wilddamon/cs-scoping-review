Methods: This is a single-center retrospective study on infants born at 22-25 weeks of gestation, all of whom received AC. The survival rates at discharge were 83%, 86%, 93%, and 93% at 22, 23, 24, and 25 gestational weeks, respectively. The overall percentage of normal DQ was 26/47 (55%).
