"Background: Though there have been many studies comparing the combination of hyperbaric bupivacaine with fentanyl for spinal anaesthesia for caesarean section, there is still the requirement for further standardization of doses of these drugs, promising single best combination of drugs in providing optimal anaesthesia and minimal to nil adverse effects. Hence, this is the study of comparison of 3 different doses of hyperbaric bupivacaine which is 8mg, 9mg and 10 mg of 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine with fentanyl 15 microg with regards to synergistic effect on sensory and motor block, recovery profile, quality of intraoperative and postoperative analgesia, haemodynamic changes, adverse effects. Result(s): This study has shown that, the onset of analgesia was longer in lower doses of bupivacaine, the duration of analgesia was longer in higher doses.
