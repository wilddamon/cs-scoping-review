Place and Duration of Study: This study was conducted at the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Jinnah Hospital, Lahore from January 2014 to July 2014. Materials and Methods: A total of 500 cases (250 cases in each group). Patients were randomly divided in two equal groups.
