METHODS: This retrospective cohort study included 24 966 women with PROM at a single university-affiliated tertiary medical center (2011-2023). Secondary outcomes were maternal obstetric complications, including assisted operative vaginal delivery, intrapartum fever, postpartum hemorrhage, need for blood products transfusion and need for re-admission within 30 days postpartum.
