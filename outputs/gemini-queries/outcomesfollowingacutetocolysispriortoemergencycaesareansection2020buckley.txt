MATERIALS AND METHODS: This was a prospective audit between February 2018 and June 2019 among women who underwent a category one or two caesarean section. RESULTS: Among 423 women in the pre-policy change cohort and 253 post-policy change, there was no difference in arterial cord pH (median pH = 7.24 before the policy and median pH = 7.24 after the policy was introduced, P = 0.88).
