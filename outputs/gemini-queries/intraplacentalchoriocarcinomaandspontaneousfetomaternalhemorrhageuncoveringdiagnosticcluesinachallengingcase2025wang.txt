Data from these cases were combined to evaluate clinical characteristics, diagnostic approaches, treatment options, and outcomes. RESULTS: A 31-year-old woman presented with decreased fetal movements at 34 weeks and 2 days of gestation. The patient received chemotherapy with EMA/CO and showed no evidence of metastasis or recurrence over 12 months of follow-up. The systematic review identified 35 cases of IC associated with FMH.
