"An analysis of placental chorionic villous and decidual basalis tissue immunoreactivity in patients after cesarean section due to a placenta accreta spectrum disorder and elective cesarean section followed by a depressed mood.;RESEARCH BACKGROUND: Over the past few years, interest in investigating immune dysfunction in patients with psychiatric disorders has increased. The aim of this study was to compare the B7-H4 immunoreactivity levels in patients after cesarean section.;METHODS: Placental and decidual tissue samples were obtained from 49 women who delivered at Bielanski Hospital in Warsaw between 2009 and 2015.
