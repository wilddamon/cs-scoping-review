METHODS: Retrospective cohort study of women who underwent herniorrhaphy during June 2000-December 2014 in the United States. Women aged 18-50 who underwent umbilical, incisional/ventral, or inguinal/femoral herniorrhaphy in the Truven Health Analytics MarketScan((r)) Commercial Claims and Encounters database were included.
