The differences in demographic, obstetric characteristics, peripartum complications, and fetal outcome between patients with no previous cesarean and those with previous cesarean section were analyzed. Result(s): Ninety patients with non-adherent placenta previa were included, 54 patients with no previous cesarean and 36 patients with previous cesarean section.
