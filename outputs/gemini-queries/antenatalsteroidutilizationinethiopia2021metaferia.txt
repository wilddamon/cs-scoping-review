In this study we assessed antenatal steroid utilization by the mother and its effect on preterm babies. Method. Result. Out of 4919 participants, 1575 preterm babies whose gestational ages were below 35 weeks were included in the study. Use of antenatal dexamethasone was 37.5% among study participants.
