CASE PRESENTATION: 23-week fetus referred because of severe cardiomegaly on screening obstetric ultrasonography. Fetal echocardiography revealed fistulous connection between dilated left pulmonary artery and left atrium with high velocity continuous flow at the left atrial orifice of fistula and retrograde flow from the ductus arteriosus to the pulmonary artery. DISCUSSION: Pulmonary artery-to-left atrial fistula is a rare anomaly and is frequently described between the right pulmonary artery and the left atrium. Presentation of age depends on the size of the fistulous connection. Patients with large connections are presented in fetal age with cardiomegaly and heart failure or presented in early infancy with profound cyanosis.
