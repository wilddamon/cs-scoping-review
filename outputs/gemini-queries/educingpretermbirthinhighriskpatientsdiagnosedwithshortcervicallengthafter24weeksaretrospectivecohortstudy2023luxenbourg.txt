"Objective: To assess the impact of progesterone treatment on maternal and neonatal outcomes in women with a history of preterm birth and short cervical length diagnosed after 24 weeks of gestation. Method(s): A retrospective cohort study included women with a history of preterm birth and a transvaginal sonographic cervical length measurement of <= 25 mm, diagnosed between 24+0 and 33+6 weeks of gestation.
