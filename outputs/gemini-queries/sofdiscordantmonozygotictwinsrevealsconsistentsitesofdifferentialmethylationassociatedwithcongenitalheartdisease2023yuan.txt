"Background: Despite being essentially genetically identical, monozygotic (MZ) twins can be discordant for congenital heart disease (CHD), thus highlighting the importance of in utero environmental factors for CHD pathogenesis. Methods: Cord blood of CHD-discordant MZ twins from the Chongqing Longitudinal Twin Study Cohort was subjected to whole-genome bisulfite sequencing, then validated by MeDIP-qPCR and qRT-PCR.
