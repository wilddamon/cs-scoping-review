"AIM: To determine the prevalence of adverse clinical outcomes, the rates of healthcare utilization, and the incidence of post-partum Type 2 diabetes in refugees with gestational diabetes (GDM), compared with other immigrants and non-immigrants.;METHODS: A population-based cohort study was conducted using healthcare databases in Ontario, Canada.
