"OBJECTIVES: To evaluate, in a French multicenter cohort, the risk of C-section based on a high pre-pregnancy body mass index (BMI). STUDY DESIGN: This historical cohort study analyzed records from the French AUDIPOG perinatal database. Inclusion criteria were deliveries>=22 weeks (or with a birth weight>=500g). RESULTS: A C-section occurred in 16.4%, 22.7%, 28.8% and 39.4% of normal BMI, overweight, obese and class III obese women, respectively (P<10(-4)). aRR of C-section increased with BMI: 1.26 [95%CI: 1.22-1.30] for BMI between 25-29.9kg/m(2); 1.39 [95%CI: 1.34-1.45] for BMI between 30-39.9kg/m(2) and 1.72 [95%CI: 1.57-1.90] for BMI>=40kg/m(2); but not the elective C-section.
