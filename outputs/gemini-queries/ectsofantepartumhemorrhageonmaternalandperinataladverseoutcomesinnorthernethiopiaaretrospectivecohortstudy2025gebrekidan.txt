METHODS: An institutional-based retrospective cohort study was conducted among 539 mothers who gave birth between September 2019 and August 2021 at Ayder Comprehensive Specialized Hospital, Tigray, Ethiopia. Mothers who gave birth with and without antepartum hemorrhage were categorized as exposed and nonexposed groups, respectively. A systematic sampling method was used to select participants from medical records. Data were collected through a retrospective review of medical records.
