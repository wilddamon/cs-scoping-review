"Objective: To explore the value of color Doppler ultrasonography in the diagnosis of heterotopic pregnancy (HP). Methods Fifty-nine cases diagnosed as HP by ultrasound and underwent systematic treatment in Obstetrics and Gynecology Hospital,Fudan University from Feb.,2013 to Apr.,2017 were retrospectively analyzed.The clinical features and characteristics of ultrasound images in HP patients were summarized. Results Among 59 cases,57 cases were proved by operation and pathology as HP,2 cases were misdiagnosed.Of the confirmed 57 cases,51 cases (89.5%)were conceived with in vitro fertilization and embryo transfer (IVF-ET),4 cases (7.0%) resulted from ovulation induction and 2 cases (3.5%) were the result of natural conception.The majority of the ectopic pregnancies were located in the fallopian tubes (44 cases,77.2%),10 cases (17.5%) were sited in the cornual region,2 cases (3.5%) in the ovary and 1 case (1.8%) in the previous cesarean section scar.Two types of ultrasonographic presentation of ectopic pregnancy were seen in HP patients:gestational sac type (29 cases,50.9%) and complex mass type (28 cases,49.1%).In the case with a gestational sac,the diameter of the ectopic gestational sac was less than that of the intrauterine gestational sac. Conclusions With the widespread use of assisted reproductive technology (ART) and ovulation induction,the incidence of HP had significantly increased and was no longer a rare event.Combined with clinical features,the transvaginal color Doppler ultrasound could be helpful in early detection of HP and could contribute to making clinical decision timely and accurately.
