METHODS: Inclusion criteria were patients in the National Anesthesia Clinical Outcomes Registry undergoing CD. Unadjusted rates of hypothermia and odds ratios for hypothermia were determined. CONCLUSION: NA was associated with a higher rate and risk for hypothermia.
