"Background: Because of the widespread use of oral contraceptives (OCs) and the devastating effects of depression both on an individual and a societal level, it is crucial to understand the nature of the previously reported relationship between OC use and depression risk. Methods: Data from the prospective cohort study TRacking Adolescents' Individual Lives Survey, among women aged 13-25 years were used.
