Material(s) and Method(s): This is a hospital based prospective study done on 120 neonates (>37 weeks) with perinatal asphyxia were admitted to Department of Paediatrics and Neonatology Rashtriya Mahila Chikitsalaya (RMC) JLNMC during one year period.
