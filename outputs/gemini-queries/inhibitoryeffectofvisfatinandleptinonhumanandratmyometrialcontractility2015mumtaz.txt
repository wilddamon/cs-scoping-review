"AIMS: The purpose of this study was to investigate the effect of visfatin on in vitro myometrial contractility in human and rat, and compare it to leptin.;MAIN METHODS: Myometrial strips from term pregnant women having a caesarean section or rats were dissected, superfused with physiological saline and the effects of visfatin (500pM-25nM) or leptin (1nM-1mcM), on spontaneous and oxytocin-induced contractions were studied. After establishment of regular contractions, tissues were incubated for control and test response at 37degreeC for 20min, and then contractility was assayed.;KEY FINDINGS: In human and rat myometrium, visfatin had similar dose dependent effects on contractility. In rat tissue the decrease was also significant (spontaneous, 76+/-7%, n=7; oxytocin-induced 68+/-6%, n=3). Leptin at this concentration (10nM) had no effect in rat or human, and even at a higher concentration (1mcM) produced only a small inhibitory effect (~ 80%) on contractions.;SIGNIFICANCE: These data are the first to show that visfatin inhibits myometrial contractility and does so more potently than leptin.
