Methods: This cross-sectional study used data claims of ADSW ages 17-49 with births during 2016-2023. International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision (ICD-10) diagnostic, Medicare Severity Diagnosis Related Group, and Current Procedural Terminology codes were used to identify births, perinatal outcomes, and maternal comorbidities.
