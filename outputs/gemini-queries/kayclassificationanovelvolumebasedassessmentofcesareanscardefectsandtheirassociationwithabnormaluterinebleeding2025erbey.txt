"Objective: This study aimed to determine the prevalence of isthmocele in patients who had undergone cesarean delivery and to investigate its association with abnormal uterine bleeding (AUB). Material and Methods: This retrospective, hospital-based cohort study was conducted at Ankara Etlik Zubeyde Hanim Women's Health Training and Research Hospital between October 2017 and March 2018.
