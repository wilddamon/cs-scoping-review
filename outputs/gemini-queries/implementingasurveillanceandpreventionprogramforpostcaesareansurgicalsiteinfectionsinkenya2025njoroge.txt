METHODS: Facility infection prevention and control (IPC) committees were established, trained, and resourced on SSI surveillance and prevention in two large hospitals in Kenya. A surgical checklist and monitoring form assessing surgical care bundle implementation and wound status before discharge was included in the medical charts of women who had cesarean section (CS) deliveries.
