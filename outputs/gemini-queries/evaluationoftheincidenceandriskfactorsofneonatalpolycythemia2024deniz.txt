Material(s) and Method(s): We conducted a retrospective study in newborn polisitemi patients with data in NICU over a 1-year-period (december 2013-december 2014). Result(s): This study was carried out on a total of 50 infants hospitalized in our unit with the diagnosis of polycythemia.
