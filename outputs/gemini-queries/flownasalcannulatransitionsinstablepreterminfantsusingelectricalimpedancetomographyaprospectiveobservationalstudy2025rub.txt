"Objective: To assess lung aeration changes during clinically directed transitions from CPAP to HFNC in preterm infants using Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT). Patient(s): Infants born < 32 weeks gestational age (GA) undergoing a clinically indicated transition from CPAP to HFNC following >= 2 weeks of respiratory support. Intervention(s): EIT data were recorded for 30-60 min before and after transition. Main Outcome Measure(s): The primary outcome was change in end-expiratory lung impedance (DELTAEELI).
