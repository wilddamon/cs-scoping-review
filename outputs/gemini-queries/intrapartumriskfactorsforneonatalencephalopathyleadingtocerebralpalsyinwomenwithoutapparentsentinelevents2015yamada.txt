"AIM: To determine intrapartum factors associated with neonatal encephalopathy leading to cerebral palsy (NE-CP).;METHODS: A total of 70 NE-CP patients who fulfilled study criteria (cephalic singleton pregnancy with attempted vaginal delivery [AVD] at gestational week [GW] >=36; intrapartum occurrence of non-reassuring fetal status without apparent cause following reassuring fetal status on admission; and development of NE-CP) were compared with 210 AVD controls who had 1- and 5-min Apgar score >=8 matched for GW, maternal parity, and use of uterotonics.
