"Introduction: Postpartum hemorrhage is one of the leading cause of maternal mortality. Carbetocin is a uterotonic drug used to prevent postpartum hemorrhage. This study compares the effectiveness of Carbetocin administered intramuscularly versus intravenously after delivery in preventing postpartum hemorrhage. Objective(s): To assess the efficacy of intramuscular (IM) versus intravenous (IV) Carbetocin in preventing postpartum hemorrhage (PPH). Methodology: A one year prospective comparative study was conducted among Antenatal women who undergo deliveries between 37 to 40 weeks of gestation in department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology in a tertiary care centre at Kancheepuram district. Data will be measured based on the amount of blood loss during vaginal or cesarean delivery. Hemoglobin and hematocrit levels will be measured pre-delivery and 24 hours post-delivery. Data entered in MS excel &analysed by chi square, anova test. Result(s): A total of 180 participants were included, 90 participants receiving intravenous Carbetocin and 90 participants receiving intramuscular Carbetocin. The mean blood loss was significantly lower in the intravenous group ([210 +/-44.4] mL) compared to the intramuscular group ([260 +/- 111.1] mL) (p<0.05). Post-delivery hemoglobin and hematocrit drop were also less in the intravenous group. Conclusion(s): This study concludes that IV carbetocin is more effective than IM carbetocin in preventing PPH in AMTSL by offering faster uterine contraction, better hemodynamic stability and lower risk of severe blood loss.Copyright (c)2025 Int.
