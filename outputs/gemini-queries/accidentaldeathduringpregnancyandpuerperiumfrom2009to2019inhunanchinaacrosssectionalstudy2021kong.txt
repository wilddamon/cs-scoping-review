SETTING: Hunan Province, with a population of 74 million, has an area of 210 000 km(2) and 123 counties/districts. PARTICIPANTS: A collection of 239 cases of accidental death during pregnancy and puerperium in Hunan Province from 2009 to 2019, including 181 cases of rural pregnancy and puerperium and 58 cases of urban pregnancy and puerperium.
