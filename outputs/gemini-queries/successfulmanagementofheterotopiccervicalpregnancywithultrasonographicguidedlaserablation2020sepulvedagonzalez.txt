"Heterotopic pregnancy refers to the simultaneous presence of ectopic pregnancy and intrauterine pregnancy. We report a case of a 36-year-old woman with type 2 diabetes mellitus and 1-year of primary infertility. Following oral ovulation induction cycles and intrauterine insemination, diagnosis of heterotopic cervical pregnancy was made by ultrasonography at 7.4 weeks of gestation.
