"Background: The most successful technique for reducing labor pain is still epidural analgesia.Conventional concentrations of local anaesthetics may, however, cause significant motor block and delay labour progression. Low-dose epidural methods that combine diluted opioid and local anesthetic adjuvant have been proposed to provide effective analgesia while minimizing these effects. This study compared the results of traditional and low-dose epidural analgesia on labour outcomes and maternal satisfaction. Method(s): At the Shree Narayan Medical Institute and Hospital in Saharsa, Bihar, 88 parturients in active labor participated in a prospective, randomized controlled research. Either low-dose epidural analgesia with bupivacaine 0.0625% + fentanyl 2 microg/ml (Group L) or conventional epidural analgesia with bupivacaine 0.125% + fentanyl 2 microg/ml (Group C) was given to participants at random. We measured and compared maternal satisfaction, side effects, labor time, delivery method, and maternal pain (VAS ratings). Result(s): After 30 minutes of epidural treatment, the analgesia experienced by both groups was similar. Group L consumed considerably less local anesthetic on average (58.4 +/- 10.7 ml) than Group C (74.6 +/- 11.2 ml; p < 0.001). Group L's second stage of labor lasted 41.5 +/- 12.3 minutes, while Group C's was 52.8 +/- 14.1 minutes (p = 0.002). Group L had higher maternal satisfaction levels (9.1 +/- 0.6 vs. 8.4 +/- 0.7; p < 0.001). The low-dose group experienced a decreased incidence of motor block (9.1% vs. 25%; p = 0.04). The birth outcomes for the groups did not differ significantly. Conclusion(s): Bupivacaine 0.0625% combined with fentanyl 2 microg/ml for low-dose epidural analgesia produces analgesia comparable to the traditional regimen, with less motor block, increased maternal satisfaction and a shorter second stage of labor. It is safe, effective, and better suited for patient-centred obstetric care, particularly in resource-limited settings.Copyright (c)2025Int. J. Life Sci. Biotechnol. Pharma. Res."""
