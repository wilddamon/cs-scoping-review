Results: Of the 1302 births in SUH in 2018, 42 women met the criteria resulting in a suspected incidence of 3.2% (n = 42). 9.5%(n = 4) of patients had a history of OC in a previous pregnancy. Conclusion: Suspected incidence of 3.2% (n = 42) is significantly higher than the stated 0.7% across multi-ethnic populations.
