"Objective: To find out the success rate of Uterovaginal packing of Atonic Uterus in a tertiary care hospital, in low resource setting. Method(s): This was a cross-sectional study conducted in Gynaecology and Obstetrics department of Lady Reading Hospital from January 2019 to December 2019. Our study included those patients with PPH not responding to medical treatment in vaginal delivery. Patients in shock with PPH and PPH after Caesarian section were excluded from the study.
