"This study explored IgG N-glycosylation pattern differences in maternal and infant serum in the context of gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM). Serum samples from 15 mother-infant pairs were collected at 12 weeks postpartum and categorized according to maternal body mass index (BMI) and GDM status. The N-glycosylation patterns of the isolated IgG pools were analyzed by capillary electrophoresis with laser-induced fluorescence detection (CE-LIF). Descriptive comparison of the relative area percentage of IgG N-glycan structures revealed differences between the groups. Comparison of the maternal and infant sialo-form/neutral-form ratio (SF/NF) of the N-glycans suggested differences between control mothers and their children, as well as between obese mothers and their children. The SF/NF ratios of IgG from the infants showed variation between infants of control mothers and infants of obese mothers, between infants of obese and infants of obese GDM mothers, and between infants of GDM with normal-weight and GDM with obese mothers.
