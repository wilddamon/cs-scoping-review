"Objective: This study aims to evaluate the impact of the implementation a mifepristone-misoprostol protocol (MIFE/MISO) on the induction-to-expulsion interval in the context of second- and third-trimester pregnancy termination or intrauterine fetal death (IUFD) compared with misoprostol alone (MISO), and to share the experience of a Canadian tertiary hospital concerning the feasibility and safety of such a protocol. Methods: This is a single-centre retrospective pre-post cohort study carried out at the Centre Hospitalier Universitaire (CHU) Sainte-Justine between 2017 and 2019. Women in the MIFE/MISO group were instructed to take mifepristone 24-48 hours before induction. Induction in the MIFE/MISO group was performed with misoprostol dosages adjusted to gestational age and the presence of previous uterine scars, while, in the MISO group, all patients received 400 mg of misoprostol vaginally every 4 hours. Results: Ninety-four patients were included in the MIFE/MISO group and 103 patients, in the MISO group. Median time to expulsion was significantly lower in the MIFE/MISO group than the MISO group (13.5 and 19.5 h respectively; P < 0.001). The total dose of misoprostol administered was significantly lower in the MIFE/MISO group than the MISO group, and adverse effects were reported in 60% and 82% of patient records, respectively (P < 0.001). Complication rates were similar between the 2 groups. Conclusion: The MIFE/MISO protocol is highly effective for second- and third-trimester induction for pregnancy termination or IUFD, without increasing complication rates and with fewer reported adverse effects.
