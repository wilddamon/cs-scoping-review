This research examines the feto-maternal outcome in women who experienced preterm premature rupture of membranes (PPROM) between 28 and 36 weeks+6 days of pregnancy. Material(s) and Method(s): This prospective study was conducted between January 2022 to December 2022 involving 120 singleton pregnancies between 28wks and 36week+6days gestation with PPROM.
