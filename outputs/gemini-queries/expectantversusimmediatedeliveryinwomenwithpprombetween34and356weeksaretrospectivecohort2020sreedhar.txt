AIMS: This study compared women with PPROM between 34 and 35 (+6) weeks, managed expectantly with women who were delivered immediately. SETTINGS AND DESIGN: Large tertiary center and retrospective cohort. METHODS AND MATERIALS: Data of 206 women with PPROM between 34 and 35(+6) weeks managed with immediate delivery in the years 2014 and 2015 were compared with seventy-five women with PPROM managed expectantly in the years 2016 and 2017.
