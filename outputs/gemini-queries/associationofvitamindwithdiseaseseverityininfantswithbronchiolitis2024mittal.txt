Baseline characteristics were recorded, including clinical details, bronchiolitis severity, and course during hospital stay. Bronchiolitis severity score (BSS) was used to score the severity. Although a negative correlation between BSS and vitamin D levels was noted, it did not reach statistical significance [r(s) = (-)0.17; p = 0.16]. CONCLUSION: Infants with Vitamin D insufficiency experienced more severe bronchiolitis with elevated BSS scores. It highlights the potential role of vitamin D deficiency in severe bronchiolitis.
