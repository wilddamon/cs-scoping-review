This study aimed to determine the prevalence of overweight/obesity and its determinants among under-five children in Marrakesh, Morocco. Material(s) and Method(s): A community-based cross-sectional study design was used to determine the prevalence of overweight/obesity and its associated factors among children under five years of age.
