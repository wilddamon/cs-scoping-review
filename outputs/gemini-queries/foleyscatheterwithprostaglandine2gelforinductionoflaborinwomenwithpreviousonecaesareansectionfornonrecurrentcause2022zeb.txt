"Objective: To compare the efficacy of Foley's Catheter versus Prostaglandin E 2 GEL for IOL in women with previous one Caesarean Section for a non-recurrent cause. Conclusion: In this study, both the methods of induction in the women with a previous cesarean section were safe and effective.
