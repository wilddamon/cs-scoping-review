Objectives: To assess prenatal exposure to AA and associations with fetal size and size at birth. Methods: Prenatal exposure to AA was measured using a high-throughput method evaluating hemoglobin adducts from AA (HbAA) and glycidamide (HbGA) in blood from pregnant women participating in the Danish Fetal Origins 1988-89 cohort (N = 991).
