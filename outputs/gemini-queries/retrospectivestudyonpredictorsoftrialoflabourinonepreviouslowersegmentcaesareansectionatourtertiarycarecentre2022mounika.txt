"Background: In the current health scenario, ever increasing Caesarean rates is a potential threat to the maternal health. This study was done to identify those factors that governed success of trial of labour in one previous lower segment caesarean section for appropriate patient selection and increase TOLAC rate as a strategy to reduce C-Section. Material(s) and Method(s): Retrospective observational study conducted on antenatal women with one previous lower segment caesarean section who attended antenatal clinics and emergency room at our hospital, with spontaneous onset of labour who have given consent for trial of labour after 1 previous LSCS.
