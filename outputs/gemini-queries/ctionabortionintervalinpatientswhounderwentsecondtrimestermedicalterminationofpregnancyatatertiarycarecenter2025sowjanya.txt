Objective To evaluate the clinical outcomes and induction-abortion interval in patients undergoing secondtrimester medical termination of pregnancy using various induction methods at a tertiary care center. Methods This retrospective hospital-based study was conducted over 18 months from October 2023 to March 2025 in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at Government General Hospital, Vijayawada.
