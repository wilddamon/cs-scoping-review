"As infertility is regarded as one of the major gynecological and medical problems all over the world. In vitro fertilization /Intracytoplasmic injection (IVF/ICSI) plays a major part in infertility management. So during this long journey, ovarian monitoring or close continuous observation of ovarian response to controlled ovarian stimulation (COS) protocols should be cheap, applicable, easy, less invasive with high sensitivity, to choose the best appropriate protocol, or to modify the dose to improve the pregnancy outcome, which is the main goal of IVF/ICSI and avoid complications of therapy. This study is a prospective observational study. The sample size was 75 infertile women who admitted to in vitro fertilization programs in the institute and 62 patients completed the study. The age of enrolled women was between 20-39 years old and the first IVF/embryo transfer treatment. Triggering of Ovulation: It involved ovarian stimulation with rFSH (Gonal f Merck, Serono) on the second day of the menstrual cycle followed by the administration of a GnRH antagonist (Cetrotide 0.25 mg, Serono) in fixed or flexible doses. At a day of ova pick up vaginal ultrasound examination was done to each patient. 2 D and color Doppler ultrasound were performed for intra-ovarian blood flow were assessed by examining ovarian stroma vascularity that is any small arteries in the ovarian stroma, not adjacent to the ovarian surface or situated near the wall of a follicle (Zaidi et al, 1995). Pulsatility index (PI) and resistance index (RI) were measured of the right and left side ovarian stromal blood flow. Outcome measures are oocyte number and maturity, embryo grade, and pregnancy rate. The mean of age of our study group is 29.7+/-6.0 years. The results reveal a significant negative correlation between RI level and retrieved oocyte number (r= -0.476, P=0.0.0001), MI oocyte number (r = -0.356 and p = 0.005), MII oocyte number (r = -0.441 and p= 0.0001), PI has a significant negative correlation with retrieved oocyte number (r = -0.365, P = 0.004) and MII oocyte number(r= -0.364 and p= 0.004), reflecting the effect of ovarian blood vascularity with oocyte maturation. The correlation between ovarian stromal RI and embryo grade is shown as nonsignificant, no correlation with embryo grade I (r = 0.06, P-value 0.624). The correlation between PI and embryo grade shows a non-significant, negative correlation with grade I embryo, and embryo grade reflecting the effect of sperm quality on fertilization and embryo grade. The mean RI among pregnant women (0.57+/-0.14) is non-significantly lower than non- pregnant women (0.61+/-0.1). The mean PI is non-significantly lower among pregnant women (0.95+/-1.04) than non- pregnant women (1.06+/-0.45). In conclusion, it is generally accepted that high E2 and low PI of ovarian blood vessels during the day of ova pickup can be useful predictive methods for the higher number of collected oocyte and good quality oocyte.
