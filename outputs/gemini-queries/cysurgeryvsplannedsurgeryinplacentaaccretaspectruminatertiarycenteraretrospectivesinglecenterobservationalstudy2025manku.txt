Aim(s): To evaluate and compare the clinical outcomes in planned and emergency surgery performed in PAS cases. Material(s) and Method(s): We conducted a retrospective cohort study on 36 cases of PAS managed from October 2023 to December 2024 at a tertiary care hospital at Government Medical College, Amritsar.
