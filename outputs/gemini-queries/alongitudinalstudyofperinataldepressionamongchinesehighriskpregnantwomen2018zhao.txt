Methods: A sample of 167 Chinese pregnant women with medically defined complications and an Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale >= 9 and/or a Postpartum Depression Screening Scale >= 60 were followed throughout early pregnancy (<28 weeks), late pregnancy (>28 weeks), 3-days and 6-weeks after delivery.
