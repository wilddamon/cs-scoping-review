Material and Methods: A total of 1640 consecutive pregnant women between 9+5 and 13+6 weeks of pregnancy were recruited. Results: During the study period, 130 (7.92%) women had low PAPP-A and were considered as cases and 200 women with normal PAPP-A were controls.
