Although colloid solutions have shown some benefit, crystalloids remain widely used due to their safety and availability. The prevention of spinal-induced hypotension continues to be a challenge in obstetric anaesthesia. Objective(s):To compare the effectiveness of crystalloid preloading versus co-loading in preventing spinal anaesthesia-induced maternal hypotension during elective caesarean section.
