"OBJECTIVE: To determine the effectiveness of closed incision negative pressure wound therapy (NPWT) compared with standard dressings in preventing surgical site infection (SSI) in obese women undergoing caesarean section.;DESIGN: Multicentre, pragmatic, randomised, controlled, parallel group, superiority trial.;SETTING: Four Australian tertiary hospitals between October 2015 and November 2019.;PARTICIPANTS: Eligible women had a pre-pregnancy body mass index of 30 or greater and gave birth by elective or semi-urgent caesarean section.;INTERVENTION: 2035 consenting women were randomised before the caesarean procedure to closed incision NPWT (n=1017) or standard dressing (n=1018). Post hoc sensitivity analyses to explore the effect of missing data found the same direction of effect (closed incision NPWT reducing SSI), with statistical significance.
