The aim of this study was to examine the performance of fetomaternal Doppler for APO prediction in pregnancies with GDM at term. Materials and Methods This is a retrospective cohort study of singleton, non-anomalous fetuses of women with GDM, who primarily had a vaginal delivery attempt. Study inclusion also required no other major fetomaternal abnormalities that make placental dysfunction likely.
