Objective(s): To determine survival and neurologic outcomes at 2 years after extremely preterm birth. Method(s): It is a retrospective multicentric study in 5 neonatal intensive care units (NICU) in 2012-2013. All live births less than 28 weeks gestation were included. Result(s): A total of 109 births were recorded. Prenatal corticosteroids were given in 47% of cases. Mean weight was 989g and mean age was 26 weeks gestation.
