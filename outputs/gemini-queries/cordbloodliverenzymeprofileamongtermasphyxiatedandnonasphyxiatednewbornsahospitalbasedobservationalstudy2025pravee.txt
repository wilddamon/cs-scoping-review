"Background: Birth asphyxia remains a leading cause of neonatal morbidity and mortality, significantly impacting organ function, including the liver. Method(s): A hospital-based observational study was conducted over 18 months at Adichunchanagiri Hospital and Research Centre. A total of 130 term neonates were included, with 65 asphyxiated and 65 non-asphyxiated newborns as controls.
