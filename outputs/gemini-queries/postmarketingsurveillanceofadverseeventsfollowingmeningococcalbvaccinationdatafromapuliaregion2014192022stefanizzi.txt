"Since the multicomponent meningococcal B vaccine introduction, the Apulian Regional Health Authority implemented postmarketing surveillance program, as provided by Italian laws. From National Pharmacovigilance Network, we selected 4CMenB AEFIs reported in Apulia from 01 January 2014 to 31 December 2019, while the number of 4 cMen B doses administered per year was obtained from the regional immunization database (GIAVA). For each subject who experienced an adverse event following meningococcal B vaccine (AEFIs), a predefined form was filled in. A total of 214 AEFIs (26.5 x 100.000 doses) were reported after any dose of MenB-4 c vaccination of which 58/214 (27.1%) were classified as serious (7.2 x 100,000 doses), 145/214 (67.8%) as not serious (180 x 100,000 doses), and 11/214 (5.1%) as undefined (1.3 x 100,000 doses). A total of 31/58 (3.8 x 100,000 doses; 53.4%) serious AEFIs were reported as having a 'consistent causal association' with vaccination. A total of 13/31 (41.9%) serious AEFIs classified as 'consistent causal association' were reported after the first dose of 4cMenB, of these 5/13 (38.5%) children did not complete the vaccination schedule.
