This study aimed to evaluate the antepartum, intraoperative, postoperative, and neonatal outcomes in women undergoing repeat caesarean sections. Method(s): A prospective observational study was conductedon400pregnantwomenwithahistoryof one or more previous caesarean sections admitted to Bebe Nanki Hospital, Government Medical College, Amritsar. Result(s): The majority (56.25%) of the women were aged 21-25 years, and 67.3% underwent emergency caesarean deliveries. Neonatal complications included jaundice (74.35%), prematurity (83.76%), and birth asphyxia (26.5%). Conclusion(s): Repeat caesarean sections are associated with increased maternal and neonatal morbidity, especially in emergency settings and in patients with multiple prior CS.
