"Objective: To test the association between Prenatal Care Coordination (PNCC) participation during pregnancy and maternal health outcomes among Medicaid beneficiaries in Wisconsin. Participants: Beneficiaries with live births funded by Medicaid from 2011 to 2019 (full sample: N = 192,511 births; sibling sample: n = 91,329 births). Methods: We used conventional and sibling fixed effects (FE) linear probability models to test associations between PNCC (none, assessment/care plan only, or service receipt) and maternal health outcomes, including severe maternal morbidity (SMM), any postpartum visit (within 30 days after birth or within 90 days after birth), emergency department (ED) admission within 30 days after birth, and the Kotelchuck Index of prenatal care adequacy.
