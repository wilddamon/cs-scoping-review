"Background: Aim: To use cord blood TSH as a marker for screening congenital hypothyroidism in term neonates. Methodology: It was a prospective observational study. The study was conducted at Department of Paediatrics, Gayathri Vidya Parishad Institute of Health care and Medical Technology, Visakhapatnam.
