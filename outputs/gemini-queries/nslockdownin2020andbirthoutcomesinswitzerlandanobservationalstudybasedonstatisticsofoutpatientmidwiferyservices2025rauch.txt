METHODS: We included singleton births recorded in the nationwide statistics of outpatient midwifery services in Switzerland (2018-2020). This was driven by an OR for preterm birth of 2.05 (95% CI: 1.10-3.85) for the canton of Ticino, which accounted for most COVID-19 infections during this time, but with no meaningful change in risk of preterm birth in other regions of Switzerland.
