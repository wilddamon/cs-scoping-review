PARTICIPANTS: Of 280 eligible women, 21 were normal weight, 134 were overweight, and 125 were obese at labor admission. METHODS: Data on women who received oxytocin for postdates induction between January 1, 2013 and June 30, 2013 were extracted from medical records. RESULTS: Women who were obese received more oxytocin than women who were overweight in the unadjusted analysis of variance (7.50 units compared with 5.92 units, p = .031).
