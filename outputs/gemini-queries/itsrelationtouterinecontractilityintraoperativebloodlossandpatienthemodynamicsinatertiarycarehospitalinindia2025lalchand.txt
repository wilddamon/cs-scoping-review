We aimed to study the efficacy of carbetocin, the new synthetic analog of oxytocin, over the gold standard, oxytocin. Material(s) and Method(s): We undertook a prospective randomized controlled study, with 100 women in each group. One group received 100 microg of heat-stable carbetocin IV, and the other 10 IU of oxytocin IM, according to the institutional protocol, within 60 seconds after extraction of the fetus. Result(s): The use of heat-stable carbetocin was associated with significantly less blood loss during the intraoperative period and better uterine contractility.
