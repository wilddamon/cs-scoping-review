AIMS: To examine informed consent and disclosure of material risks in birth in a prospective qualitative study of midwives and obstetricians. MATERIALS AND METHODS: Qualitative study using semi-structured interviews to examine practices and perspectives of obstetricians and midwives. RESULTS: Twenty-two telephone interviews were concluded. Five sub-themes were identified: (1) non-compliance with the NSW Consent Manual, (2) risk communication/informed consent in maternity care, (3) consent practices in instrumental birth, (4) who should deliver risk information and when (5) barriers to change in consent practice (obstetricians only). Ninety-four per cent (17/18) of obstetricians believe that maternity care consent practices need to be improved.
