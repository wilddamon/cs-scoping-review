"Myoma periodically leads to infertility by causing impaired blood flow, aseptic inflammation in the endometrium, increased uterine contractility, impaired sperm migration with cervi?al myoma nodes and obstruction of the proximal fallopian tubes. Material and Methods - The retrospective study included 37 IVF cycles from 37 patients that had infertility combined with myoma.
