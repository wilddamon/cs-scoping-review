Multivariable logistic regression models assessed associations between clinical/laboratory parameters and APOs, including miscarriage, preterm birth, low birth weight (LBW), and cesarean delivery. RESULTS: APOs were observed in the following descending order of incidence: cesarean delivery (78.6%), preterm birth (30.7%), miscarriage (26.4%), and LBW (24.9%).
