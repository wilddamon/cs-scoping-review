"Background: We aimed to investigate the association between maternal neutrophil ratio and histological chorioamnionitis (HCA) risk in pregnant women with premature rupture of membranes (PROM) in late pregnancy. Method(s): A retrospective analysis was conducted on 95 cases of women with PROM in their late pregnancy between March 2018 and August 2021. These women were divided into two groups based on the presence of HCA. General clinical data and laboratory indicators were compared between the two groups. Result(s): After adjusting for confounding factors, the curve fitting showed a "U"-shaped curve relationship between the neutrophil ratio and the risk of HCA. Furthermore, we equally divided the neutrophil ratio into three groups. The risk of HCA was significantly increased in the low-ratio group (OR = 4.292, 95% CI: 1.247- 14.706, P = 0.021) compared with the middle-ratio group, which was used as the reference group. Similarly, the HCA risk of the high-ratio group (OR = 13.145, 95% CI: 1.796-96.233, P = 0.011) was also significantly enhanced.
