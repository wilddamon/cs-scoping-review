This quality improvement initiative aimed to work towards achieving 100% compliance with perioperative antibiotic administration.;METHODS: We examined the workflow in our Anaesthesia Information Management System (AIMS) and proposed interventions using cause-and-effect analysis of anonymised anaesthetic records from eligible surgical cases extracted from AIMS.
