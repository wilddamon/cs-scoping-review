"Background: Newborns with ductal-dependent congenital heart disease (CHD) are at increased risk for developing necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). Study Design: Single-center retrospective cohort study of newborns with ductal-dependent CHD before and after implementation of oral dual-strain probiotics containing Bifidobacterium infantis and Lactobacillus acidophilus, on each day of exposure to prostaglandin E1 (PGE1).
