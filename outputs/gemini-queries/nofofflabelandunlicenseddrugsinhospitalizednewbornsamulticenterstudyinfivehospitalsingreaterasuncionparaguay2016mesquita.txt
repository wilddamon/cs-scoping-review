Objective(s): To determine the prevalence of the use of drugs not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in newborns hospitalized at care facilities of various levels in Asuncion and Greater Asuncion. Methodology: We conducted a cross-sectional observational and descriptive study with an analytic component. Included were newborns in intensive care, intermediate care, and minimal care wards of five hospitals.
