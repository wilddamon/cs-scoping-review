"The aim of this manuscript is to compare the pregnancy and surgical outcomes and costs of an elective cystectomy of a benign ovarian tumour during pregnancy treated by laparotomy and laparoscopy. We completed a retrospective cohort study, and the related data was collected and analysed between September 2009 and September 2014 in the Obstetric & Gynecology Hospital of Fudan University. A total of 65 pregnant women were included, 30 cases for laparoscopy and 35 cases for laparotomy. The laparoscopy group had higher costs (P < 0.001) and lower postoperative febrile rates (P = 0.005), a shorter postoperative hospital length of stay (P < 0.001) and a shorter antibiotic prophylaxis (P < 0.001). The gestational age at delivery, caesarean delivery rate, preterm delivery rate, length of prophylactic tocolytic therapy, rate of postoperative uterine contractions, newborn weight and rate of neonatal disorders were not significantly different between the 2 groups. Compared with laparotomic surgery for the elective cystectomy of a benign ovarian tumour during pregnancy, laparoscopy had an equal effect on the mothers and foetuses. The costs of laparoscopic surgery were higher, but it is minimally invasive.
