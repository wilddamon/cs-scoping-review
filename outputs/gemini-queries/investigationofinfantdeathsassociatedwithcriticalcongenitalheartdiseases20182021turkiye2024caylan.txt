METHODS: In a cross-sectional study, data for the study were obtained through Death Notification System, Birth Notification System and Turkish Statistical Institute birth statistics. There was at least one non-cardiac congenital anomaly/genetic disorder in 26.1% of all cases. CONCLUSION: CCHD holds a significant role in neonatal and infant mortality in Turkiye.
