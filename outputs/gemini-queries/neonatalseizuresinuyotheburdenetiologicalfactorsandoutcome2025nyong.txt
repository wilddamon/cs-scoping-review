Neonates with seizures, suspected seizures or with conditions associated with seizures were identified in the admission register. Information extracted included age at admission, age on onset, gestational ages of neonates, birth weight, mode of delivery, APGAR score, family history of seizures, social status of the parent, significant findings on physical examination.
