Method(s): A observational study including 5529 gestational diabetes mellitus patients was performed. No additive interaction was found. Conclusion(s): Pre-pregnancy overweight or obesity, excessive gestational weight gain, two or more abnormal items of OGTT parameters contribute to adverse pregnancy outcomes independently among women with gestational diabetes mellitus.
