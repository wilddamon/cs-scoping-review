"OBJECTIVE: To correlate between intraoperative findings, placental histopathology and neonatal outcome in emergent cesarean deliveries (ECD) for non-reassuring fetal heart rate (NRFHR).;STUDY DESIGN: Data on ECD for NRFHR were reviewed for labor, documented intraoperative findings, neonatal outcome parameters and placental histopathology reports. Placental lesions were classified to those related to maternal underperfusion or fetal thrombo-occlusive disease, and those related to maternal (MIR) and fetal (FIR) inflammatory responses.
