This study recruited 221 PE patients with their partners and infants and 345 normotensive women with their infants. The current study found that, in both maternal and fetal dominant model (GC + CC/GG), VEGF-A rs2010963 polymorphism was associated with an increased risk of PE (OR = 1.85, 95% CI: 1.25-2.75; OR = 1.90, 95% CI: 1.28-2.83, respectively).
