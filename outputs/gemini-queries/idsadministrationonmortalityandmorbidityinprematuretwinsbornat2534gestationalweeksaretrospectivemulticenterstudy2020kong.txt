"OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the effects of antenatal corticosteroids (ACS) administration on mortality and major neonatal complications in early life of preterm twins. STUDY DESIGN: This study retrospectively enrolled 1 662 twins delivered at 25~34(+6)gestational weeks in China from January 2013 to December 2014. RESULTS: A total of 910 neonates (54.8 %) received one or more doses of ACS, and 752 neonates (45.2 %) did not receive any ACS. However, the incidence of mild ROP was higher in the ACS group than that in the no-ACS group (P = 0.047) at 25~27(+6) weeks. Multivariable logistic regression analysis demonstrated a decreasing risk of RDS (aOR = 0.661, 95 %CI:0.506-0.863, P = 0.002) after adjusting the gestational week, birth weight, small for gestational age, delivery mode, 5 min Apgar score, and maternal perinatal complications. However, our data suggest that it may not have a beneficial effect on mortality and other short-term morbidities."""
