"Background: There has been an exponential increase in the rate of cesarean section (CS) over the last decade. This is a matter of international public health. The aim of the present study was to evaluate in our hospital which all clinical situations contributed and led to cesarean deliveries as per Robson's classification system and to audit the increasing rate of CS. Method(s): This study was performed in V N Desai Hospital, Santa Cruz from 1st October 2022 to 31st October 2023. For each case, we collected data regarding maternal characteristics and pregnancy-related information. The dependent variable was Robson's classification group. Results and observation: Out of a total of 1,141 women delivered, 702 underwent CS (61%). It was observed that most CSs belonged to group II and group V of Robson criteria. Conclusion(s): Women with a previous cesarean delivery represent an increasing proportion of cesarean deliveries. Use of the Robson criteria allows standardized comparisons of data and identifies clinical scenarios driving changes in cesarean rates. Hospitals and health organizations can use the Robson 10-Group Classification System to evaluate quality and processes associated with cesarean delivery.
