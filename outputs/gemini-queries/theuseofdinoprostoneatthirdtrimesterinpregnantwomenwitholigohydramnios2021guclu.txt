This retrospective study included 187 pregnant women between January 2015-December 2020. Four quadrant technique was used to determine amniotic fluid index. It was found in the analysis that the findings of premature rupture of membrane (PROM), oxytocin augmentation need, tachysystole, vaginal delivery rate, caesarean delivery rate, meconium stained amnion rate, neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) need, postpartum hemorrhage, and transfusion need (p = 0.394) were similar between two groups (p> 0.05).
