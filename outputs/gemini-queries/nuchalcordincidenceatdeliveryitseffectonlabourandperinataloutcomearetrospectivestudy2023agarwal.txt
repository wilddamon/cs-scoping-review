Material(s) and Method(s): A retrospective case control study, conducted over a period of 1year, 1000 ANC patient were enrolled, STUDYGROUP -comprised of 200 ANC patients in which nuchal cord was found incidentally at the time of delivery (NVD/LSCS). CONTROL -800 matched controls were selected in which nuchal cord was not found at delivery.
