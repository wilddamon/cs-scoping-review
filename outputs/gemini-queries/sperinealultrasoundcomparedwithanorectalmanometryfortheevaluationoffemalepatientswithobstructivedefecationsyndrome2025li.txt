"Background and aims: The utility of transperineal ultrasound (TPUS) in combination with anorectal manometry (ARM) in the evaluation of women with obstructive defecation syndrome (ODS) remains unclear. Methods: Female patients with ODS were evaluated by TPUS and ARM, and binary logistic regression models were exploited to evaluate the clinical association of abnormal TPUS variables with abnormal ARM variables.
