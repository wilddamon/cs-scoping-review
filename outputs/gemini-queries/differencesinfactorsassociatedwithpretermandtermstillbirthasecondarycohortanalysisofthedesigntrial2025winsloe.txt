"Objective: To identify whether maternal and pregnancy characteristics associated with stillbirth differ between preterm and term stillbirth. Design: Secondary cohort analysis of the DESiGN RCT. Setting: Thirteen UK maternity units. Population: Singleton pregnant women and their babies. Methods: Multiple logistic regression was used to assess whether the 12 factors explored were associated with stillbirth. Interaction tests assessed for a difference in these associations between the preterm and term periods. Main Outcome Measure: Stillbirth stratified by preterm (<37+0 weeks') and term (37+0-42+6 weeks') births. Results: A total of 195 344 pregnancies were included. A weaker or lack of association with term, compared to preterm, stillbirth was observed for older maternal age, smoking and pre-eclampsia. Conclusion: Differences in association exist between mothers experiencing preterm and term stillbirth. These differences could contribute to design of timely surveillance and interventions to further mitigate the risk of stillbirth. (c) 2024 The Author(s).
