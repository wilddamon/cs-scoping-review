While metabolic dysfunction is recognized as a driving factor in various diseases, the precise characteristics of lipid metabolism dysfunction in HDP remain inadequately understood. Methods: Full-spectrum metabolomic profiling was performed on peripheral blood samples from 30 patients with HDP and 30 healthy controls, with targeted metabolic validation using placental tissues.
