"Objective: To determine the association between maternal obesity and fetal glycated albumin (GA) levels among pregnant women. Methods: A comparative, cross-sectional study of 59 consenting, normoglycemic, pregnant women, who met the criteria for maternal obesity, attending the antenatal clinic of the University College Hospital, Nigeria, from June 2019 to December 2019. At delivery, blood samples were taken from the mothers and from the umbilical cords of their newborns for serum GA assay.
