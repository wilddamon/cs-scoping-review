"Objective: To evaluate the prevalence of risk factors of cerebral palsy in the mothers of children with cerebral palsy. Method: The cross-sectional study was conducted in 2018 at the Helping Hand Institute of Rehabilitation Sciences, Mansehra, Pakistan, and comprised mothers of cerebral palsy children aged 1-18 years from the Hazara Division. Data was collected by using a modified form of Surveillance of cerebral palsy in Europe questionnaire regarding prenatal, natal and postal natal risk factors. Data was analysed using SPSS 21. Results: Of the 300 children, 190(63.3%) were males and 110(36.7%) were females.
