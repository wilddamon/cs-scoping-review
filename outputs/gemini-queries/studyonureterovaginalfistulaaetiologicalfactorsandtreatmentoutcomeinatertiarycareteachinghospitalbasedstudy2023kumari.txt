"Background: A ureterovaginal fistula is a connection between the distal ureter and the vagina. Although rare, it is a typical side effect of pelvic surgery. The etiological factors play a significant role in the management of ureterovaginal fistulas. Regarding this issue, we don't have a lot of knowledge. Material(s) and Method(s): A total of 38 women with ureterovaginal fistulas at the centre were included in the study group on the basis of non-probability purposive sampling.
