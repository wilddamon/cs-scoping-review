"INTRODUCTION: In case of term prelabor rupture of membranes (PROM), expectant management is a reasonable option. We aimed at assessing the frequency of spontaneous onset of labor after two days of term PROM and its associated factors.;MATERIAL AND METHODS: Women delivering at a tertiary center of a singleton in cephalic presentation, after a term PROM with an unfavorable cervix and with an expectant management period of at least two days were included during a 2-year period. The frequency of spontaneous labor was assessed, then maternal characteristics at admission and at D2 were compared between women with a spontaneous onset of labor before D3, and women with an induced labor at D3.
