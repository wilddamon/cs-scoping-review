This study compares the efficacy and safety of intracervical PGE2 and transcervical Foley's catheter for preinduction cervical ripening in women undergoing VBAC. Method(s): Women who have had one lower segment caesarean surgery in the past are participating in this prospective randomised controlled trial to test out vaginal birth.
