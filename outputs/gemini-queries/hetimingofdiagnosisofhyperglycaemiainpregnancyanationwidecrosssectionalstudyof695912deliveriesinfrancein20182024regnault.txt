METHODS: We used data from the French National Health Data System (Systeme National des Donnees de Sante), which links individual data from the hospital discharge database and the French National Health Insurance information system. RESULTS: The study population of 695,912 women who gave birth in 2018 included 84,705 women (12.2%) with hyperglycaemia in pregnancy: overt diabetes in pregnancy, 0.4%; GDM(<22), 36.8%; GDM(22-30), 52.4%; and GDM(>30), 10.4%.
