"Objective: We reviewed outcomes of 52 pregnancies in 45 women with immune thrombocytopenic purpura who delivered at Auckland Hospital with an antenatal platelet count of <100 x 109/L. Result(s): Most women had thrombocytopenia at delivery. Treatment with prednisone was given in 14 (27%) pregnancies with responses considered safe for delivery in 11 pregnancies (79%).
