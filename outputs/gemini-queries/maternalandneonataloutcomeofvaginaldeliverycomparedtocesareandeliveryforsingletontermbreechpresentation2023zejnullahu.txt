"Objective: The aim of the present study was to compare short-term maternal and neonatal outcomes in breech presentations at term according to the planned mode of delivery. The data were collected from the birth register and pediatric records. The main outcome measures were neonatal and maternal mortality and morbidity, compared according to the mode of delivery. Result(s): For 217 wormen (47.9%) a vaginal delivery was planned, of whom 83 (18.3%) were delivered vaginally. In 236 (52.1%) cases elective cesarean section was performed as planned. 134 (29.5%) were delivered by emergent cesarean section. Difference in mothers age between the three groups was statistically significant (p=0.011). Nulliparous women were more common in the elective cesarean group compared to planned vaginal group (35.5% vs. 5.96%; p < 0.0001).
