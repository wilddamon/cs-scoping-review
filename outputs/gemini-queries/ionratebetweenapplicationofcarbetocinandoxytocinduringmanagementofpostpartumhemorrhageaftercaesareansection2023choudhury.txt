"Background: Adverse effects can be produced by carbetocin and oxytocin during management of postpartum hemorrhage after caesarean section. Objective: The purpose of the present study was to compare the adverse effects among women with post-partum hemorrhage after caesarean section. Results: A total number of 96 pregnant women were recruited for this study of which 48 cases were enrolled in group I and the rest of 48 case were enrolled in group II.
