"Aim: To report the reproductive results and symptomatic relief of patients with adenomyosis who attempted to conceive after ultrasound-guided radiofrequency ablation (RFA). Methods: Of 182 consecutive patients with adenomyosis who had undergone RFA, only 81 patients were eligible for this study. Results: Of these 81 patients, 74 were infertile and seven were single at the time of RFA. The mean age at the time of operation was 35.8 years. Twenty-two (84.6%) of the 29 patients delivered 24 (66.7%) live babies (nine vaginal deliveries and 15 cesarean sections). Twelve (33.3%) pregnancies ended in spontaneous abortions. Paired sample t-tests revealed that preoperative pictorial blood loss assessment chart and pain scores were significantly (P < 0.05) different from the postoperative scores at 1-, 3- and 6-months.
