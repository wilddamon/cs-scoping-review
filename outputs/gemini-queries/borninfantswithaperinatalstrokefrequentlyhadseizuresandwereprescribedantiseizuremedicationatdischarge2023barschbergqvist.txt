The controls were all Swedish infants born during those years. Results: There were 76 infants with a confirmed perinatal stroke: 51 ischaemic and 25 haemorrhagic. Seizures were documented in 66/76 (87%) of infants with a stroke and 0.2% of the controls. Anti-seizure medication was administered to 64/66 (97%) infants with a stroke and seizures.
