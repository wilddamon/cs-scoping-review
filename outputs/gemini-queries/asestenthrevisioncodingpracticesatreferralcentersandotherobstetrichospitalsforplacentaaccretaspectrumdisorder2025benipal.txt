The most common reason (42.9%) for a false-positive diagnosis was hemorrhage due to another etiology not meeting clinical or histopathologic criteria for PAS. In 22.4% of false-positive diagnoses, there was antenatal suspicion of PAS, but PAS was not confirmed at delivery.
