"BACKGROUND: Labor induction is the process of initiating labor by pharmacologic agents or other measures. The advantages and disadvantages of labor induction are still a controversy that is much debated. AIM: The aim of the study was to determine the characteristics of vaginal delivery with intravaginal misoprostol induction. METHOD(S): This is a retrospective descriptive study at Sanglah General Hospital, Denpasar, during the period of June 1st, 2016-May 31st, 2019. Data for the eligible cases were obtained from the medical records. RESULT(S): A total number of 114 deliveries with intravaginal misoprostol induction were obtained during the study period. Seventy-one cases (61.4%) met the inclusion criteria and 43 cases (38.6%) were excluded from the study. Of the 71 cases, 52 cases (73.23%) succeeded in vaginal delivery and 19 cases (26.76%) failed to be induced and underwent a cesarean section. Most cases of vaginal delivery with intravaginal misoprostol induction occurred in nulliparous women, term pregnancy, without a history of rupture of the membranes, and with a pelvic score (PS) of 4.
