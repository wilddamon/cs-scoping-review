Secondary outcomes included time from start of induction to complete dilation, cesarean delivery rates, doses of misoprostol used, combination of induction agents used, and incidence of chorioamnionitis and postpartum hemorrhage. A secondary analysis was performed on nulliparous patients.;RESULTS: A total of 3046 individuals met the inclusion criteria. As body mass index increased, the indications for induction were more likely to be maternal.
