All patients with tachyarrhythmia or bradyarrhythmia and a minimum follow-up of six months after delivery were included. Adverse neonatal outcomes were observed in 24.3% of newborns.;CONCLUSIONS: Arrhythmias were the most common cause of referral to a dedicated cardio-obstetric clinic; most had a benign course.
