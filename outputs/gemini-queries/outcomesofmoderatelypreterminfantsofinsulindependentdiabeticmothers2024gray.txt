"Objective Little is known about the hospital outcomes of moderately preterm (MPT; 29 0/7-33 6/7 weeks gestational age) infants born to insulin-dependent diabetic mothers (IDDMs). Study Design Cohort study of infants from 18 centers included in the MPT infant database from 2012 to 2013.
