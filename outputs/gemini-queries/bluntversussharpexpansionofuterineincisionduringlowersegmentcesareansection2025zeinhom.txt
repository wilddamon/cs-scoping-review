METHODS: Comparative prospective research was performed at Ain Shams University Maternity Hospital. RESULTS: The blunt expansion group demonstrated significantly lower intraoperative blood loss, as indicated by a smaller decline in HCT levels (mean difference: 0.9%) compared to the sharp expansion group.
