"MEA (microwave endometrial ablation) is a treatment that can control hypermenorrhea. Here we compared the incidence of complications and recurrence between groups with or without previous uterine surgery. 35 patients who underwent MEA were enrolled in the study. We assessed the thickness of uterine myometrium by MRI and transvaginal ultrasonography (TV-US). 12 patients (34%) had previous uterine surgery; Among 12 patients with previous uterine surgery, 6 (50%) showed thinning of the myometrium.
