"BACKGROUND: Large-for-gestational-age (LGA) fetuses are often associated with increased maternal and neonatal morbidity. PURPOSE: To assess maternal and fetal factors associated with the mode of delivery in pregnancies with LGA fetuses. BASIC PROCEDURES: Retrospective, single-center study (2018-2021) including term, singleton pregnancies with birthweight >= 90th percentile (AUDIPOG).
