METHODS: This retrospective cohort study included 507 women admitted to the labour ward at Lagos State University Teaching Hospital (LASUTH) from May 2019 to April 2022. CONCLUSION: High-risk pregnancies are associated with increased maternal and neonatal complications, highlighting the importance of early risk stratification and adherence to WHO guidelines for adequate antenatal care.
