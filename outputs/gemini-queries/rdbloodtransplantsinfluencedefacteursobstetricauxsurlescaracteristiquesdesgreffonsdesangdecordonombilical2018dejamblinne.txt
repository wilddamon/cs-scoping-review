The purpose of this analysis is to identify obstetric factors that may influence the quality of a cord blood unit taken during delivery. Methods: A retrospective study was carried out at the Liege cord blood bank on a sample of 7.463 cord blood units collected between 2000 and 2016. Eight obstetric factors were analyzed in relation to two dependent variables; the total nucleated cells (TNC) and the volume of one unit of cord blood.
