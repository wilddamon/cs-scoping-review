"OBJECTIVES: To analyze the motor development of late preterm newborn infants (LPI) from birth to termcorrected age using the Test of Infant Motor Performance (TIMP) and to compare the obtained results with those of term infants at birth. METHODS: Prospective cohort study, 29 late preterm newborn infants were evaluated by the TIMP at birth and every two weeks until term-corrected age.
