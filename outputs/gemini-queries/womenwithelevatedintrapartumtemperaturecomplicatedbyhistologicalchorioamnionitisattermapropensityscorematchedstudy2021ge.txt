Thus, we aimed to explore whether the combination of HCA would further affect the pregnancy outcomes in those with intrapartum temperature >= 37.5degC. Methods: This retrospective cohort study was conducted at the International Peace Maternity and Child Health Hospital (IPMCH), including all full-term women with intrapartum temperature >= 37.5degC from Jan 2017 to Jan 2019.
