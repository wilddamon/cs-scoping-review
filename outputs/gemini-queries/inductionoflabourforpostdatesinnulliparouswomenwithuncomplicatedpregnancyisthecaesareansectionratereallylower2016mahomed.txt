Systematic reviews all conclude that induction of labour does not increase the risk of caesarean section (CS). We report in a retrospective cohort study involving only nulliparous women with uncomplicated singleton pregnancy at 40deg to 41(6) weeks that compared to spontaneous labour, incidence of CS was significantly higher in the induction group, 22.2% versus 12.1% (OR 2.06; 95% CI 1.93-2.20) at 40deg to 41(6) weeks versus spontaneous labour at 40deg to 41(6) weeks; and also higher at 21.0% versus 14.9% (OR 1.52; 95% CI 1.34-1.73) at 40deg to 40(6) weeks versus spontaneous labour at 41deg to 41(6) weeks (expectant management)."""
