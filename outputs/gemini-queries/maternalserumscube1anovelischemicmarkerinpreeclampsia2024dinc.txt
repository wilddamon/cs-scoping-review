The aim of the study is to measure serum SCUBE-1 levels and investigate their association with uteroplacental blood flow in patients with preeclampsia. Method(s): The study was conducted on patients with preeclampsia. Maternal serum SCUBE1 and IMA levels were the main outcomes. The control group consisted of gestational-age-matched pregnant women.
