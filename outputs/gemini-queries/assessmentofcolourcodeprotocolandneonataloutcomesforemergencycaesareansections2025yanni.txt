OBJECTIVES: Our main objective was to evaluate changes in neonatal morbidity and mortality over time for orange-code (decision-delivery interval <= 30 mins) and red-code caesareans (decision-delivery interval <= 15 mins), since the introduction of the colour-code protocol. The secondary objectives were to describe maternal complications and types of anaesthesia. METHODS: This retrospective single-centre observational study was carried out in the maternity ward of the Rennes University Hospital and included all patients for whom an emergency caesarean was performed with an orange or red code between 1st January 2015 and 31st December 2021.
