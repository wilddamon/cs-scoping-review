Material(s) and Method(s): We retrospectively evaluated 15-19 years old (55 patients) and 20-25 years old (55 patients) patients who gave birth between June 2016 and June 2017 in Hakkari State Hospital Obstetrics and Gynecology Clinic. Age, parity, blood pressure, pulse, gestational week, delivery type, birth complications, cesarean indications, maternal hemoglobin (HB), hematocrit (HTC) and platelet (PLT) levels, preeclampsia and preterm delivery at the birth were recorded. Increase in the frequency of fetal anomaly, development of preeclampsia, number of low birth weight and a decrease in maternal hemogram and hematocrit values were found to be significantly higher. Discussion(s): Adolescent pregnancies display increased fetal and maternal perinatal risks. For this reason, they are evaluated in the group of high-risk pregnancies.
