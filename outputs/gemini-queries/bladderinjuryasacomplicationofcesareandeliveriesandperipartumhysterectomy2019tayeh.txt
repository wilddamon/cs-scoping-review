"Objective: review the incidence of iatrogenic bladder injuries (IBI) in obstetrical procedures and to identify the risk factors for these injuries. Material(s) and Method(s): a retrospective research involve all patients that had cesarean deliveries (CS) and peripartum hysterectomies at Fatima Al-Zahraa Administrative hospital, from the 1st of January 2015 till the 30th of September 2018. Result(s): A total of 35,212 CS performed during the study period, 68(0.19%) CS and peripartum hysterectomies were complicated by bladder injury of them 41% were type 1, 53% were type 2, and 6% were type 3 complications. Adhesions documented in 73%, while 12% had placenta accreta and 15% had ruptured uterus. Elective CS performed in 47% of the cases. Most cases of IBI occurred during reflection of bladder flap 56% and are mainly in type 1 and type 2 while 30% occurred during delivering the baby. All cases of type 3 (6%) occurred during opening the peritoneum.
