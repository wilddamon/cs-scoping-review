METHODS: Prospective observational cohort study (March 2023-March 2024, King's College Hospital). All infants requiring admission immediately after birth were included. BCs were offered to all infants if safety criteria were met (stable observations, temperature >= 36.5degC, safe airway). Feedback questionnaires were given to parents. RESULTS: Two hundred and twenty-two infants had BCs (70%); 68% were preterm, 16% had congenital surgical anomalies, 70% were delivered by caesarean section and 72% were cuddled while on respiratory support. BCs were not performed in 30% of infants with 42% delivered under general anaesthetic.
