Methods: Umbilical cord blood for native thiol, total thiol, disulfide, albumin, and IMA analysis was obtained from 44 pregnant women over 34 weeks gestation undergoing cesarean section due to non-acute FD, and from 61 healthy pregnant women who underwent elective cesarean section Results: Native thiol and total thiol levels were significantly lower in the FD group (p = 0.02 and p = 0.014, respectively).
