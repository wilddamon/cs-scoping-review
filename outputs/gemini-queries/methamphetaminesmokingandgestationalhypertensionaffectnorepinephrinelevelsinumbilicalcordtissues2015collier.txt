MATERIALS AND METHODS: Umbilical cords (54) were collected prospectively at birth, washed of blood, and stored at -80degC. RESULTS: Catecholamine levels in umbilical cords were not associated with maternal or gestational age, gravidity, parity, neonatal or placental weight. Neither smoking nor METH affected dopamine or epinephrine. However, smoking (two-fold) and METH (four-fold) decreased norepinephrine and together a 60-fold reduction occurred (p = 0.025).
