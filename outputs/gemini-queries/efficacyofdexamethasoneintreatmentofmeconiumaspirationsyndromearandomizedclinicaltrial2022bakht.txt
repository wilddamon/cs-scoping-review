"Objective: Neonatal mortality is very high in Pakistan for which Meconium Aspiration Syndrome (MAS) is a significant cause. Objective is to determine efficacy of dexamethasone in treatment of Meconium Aspiration Syndrome. Study Design: Randomized clinical trial. Place and Duration of Study: Services Hospital Lahore, 01-01-2021 to 30-06-2021 Material and Methods: 100 neonates were randomly divided in Group A [n=50]/Cases (treated with dexamethasone) and Group B [n=50]/Control. Dexamethasone (0.2 mg/kg/BD) was given for 7 days after confirmation of diagnosis. Infants present in clinical trial were evaluated by ventilation duration (invasive and non-invasive), oxygen therapy and hospital stay. SPSS version 23 used for data analysis. Mean and standard deviation were calculated for ventilation duration, hospital stay and oxygen therapy. Independent sample t test was applied (p-value <0.05 taken as significant). Frequency and percentages were taken for gender, gestation, mode of delivery and chi-square test was applied (p-value <0.05 taken as significant). Results: For Group A (56% males and 46% females) and group B (60% males and 40% females), Mean duration of invasive ventilation (days) in group A is 0.2 and group B is 2.8 (p-value 0.002) and mean duration of non-invasive ventilation (days) in group A is 2.92 and group B is 4.46 (p-value 0.001). Mean duration of hospital stay (days) in group A is 6.02 and B is 10.56 (p-value 0.001) Conclusion: Both the duration of hospital stay and ventilation are significantly reduced in steroid group A as compared to control group B.
