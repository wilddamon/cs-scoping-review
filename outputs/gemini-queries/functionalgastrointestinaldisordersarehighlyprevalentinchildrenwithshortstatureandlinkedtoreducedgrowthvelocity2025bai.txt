Methods: Confirmed children with short stature without treatment were consecutively enrolled in a tertiary hospital from November 1 2020 to June 30 2021, and from March 1 2023 to April 30 2023. The participants were divided into two groups according to whether FGIDs were combined, and the differences between the two groups were analyzed. Results: One hundred three children with short stature were included, and the overall prevalence of FGIDs was 48.5% (n = 50).
