"Aim: This study investigated whether trophoectoderm (TE) biopsy adversely impacts serum b-human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) level on the 15th day of embryo transfer (ET), delivery week and birthweight, between biopsied and unbiopsied embryo groups, in a cohort of women who delivered a singleton baby, following frozen-thawed ET. Methods: All women having had a live birth after blastocyst ETs following frozen ET cycles with preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) were included.
