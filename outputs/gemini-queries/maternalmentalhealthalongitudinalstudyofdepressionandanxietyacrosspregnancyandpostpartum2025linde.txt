METHODS: A longitudinal design with four assessment points (second trimester, third trimester, three and six months postpartum) was used, including 136 women from a German university hospital. FINDINGS: Prevalence rates for depression ranged from 14.7 % to 20.6 %, and for generalized anxiety from 9.6 % to 14.7 %, with no significant changes over time.
