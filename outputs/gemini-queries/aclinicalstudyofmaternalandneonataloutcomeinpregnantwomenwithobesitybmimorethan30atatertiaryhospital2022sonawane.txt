"Background: Obesity is one such pre-existing maternal morbidity that puts a pregnancy at risk. This study aimed to analyze maternal and fetal outcome in obese pregnant women (BMI more than 30) at our tertiary care teaching hospital. Material(s) and Method(s): This study was prospective & observational study, conducted in pregnant women, gestational age > 28 weeks, with BMI > 30, delivering at our labour room. Maternal and neonatal outcomes were analysed. Result(s): Among 453 pregnant women with BMI > 30kg/m2, majority were from 19-25 years age group (45.1 %), 48.03% were primigravida, 82.33% had > 37 weeks of gestation.
