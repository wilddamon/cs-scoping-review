"Background: Iron deficiency anemia (IDA) is the most common micronutrient deficiency globally, particularly prevalent among pregnant women in developing countries, and is a significant contributor to adverse feto-maternal health outcomes. Method(s): This was a prospective cohort study conducted at Kanachur Institute of Medical Sciences, Mangalore, from January 2024 to December 2024. Significant predictors of IDA included lower socio-economic status (p<0.001), higher parity (p=0.015), and delayed initiation of antenatal care (p=0.005).
