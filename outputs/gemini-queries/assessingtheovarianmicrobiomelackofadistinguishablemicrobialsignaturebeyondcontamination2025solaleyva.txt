DESIGN: Twenty-two ovarian tissue samples (cortex and medulla), six follicular fluid samples from gender-reassignment patients (GRP, n = 11) and five ovarian cortex tissue samples from Caesarean sections (n = 5), together with positive and negative controls, were analysed by bacterial 16S rRNA gene sequencing.
