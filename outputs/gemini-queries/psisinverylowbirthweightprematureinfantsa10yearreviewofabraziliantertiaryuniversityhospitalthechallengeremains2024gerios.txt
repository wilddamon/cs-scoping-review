"Objectives: This study aimed to assess the incidence of late-onset sepsis (LOS), associated risk factors, and short-term prognosis in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants in a 10-year period. Study variables included maternal, birth, and neonatal data, morbidities, procedures, etiological agents, and outcome-death, bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD), severe intraventricular hemorrhage, and retinopathy of prematurity (ROP). In comparing the groups, septic VLBW infants had lower gestational age and birth weight, presented more morbidities, and underwent more invasive procedures. The risk factors for proven and clinical LOS were days of mechanical ventilation and parenteral nutrition. LOS was associated with increased risk of death, BPD, and ROP.
