"Background: Macrosomia may adversely affect feto-maternal outcomes and needs its risk factors to be evaluated during pre-conceptional and antenatal work up. Material(s) and Method(s): An analytical case control study was conducted for one and half years in a Tertiary Referral Centre of India with mothers (n=47) giving birth to macrosomic babies weighing 4000 gm or above and they were compared with controls (n=47) with babies weighing between 2500-3999 gm in relation to the risk factors and pregnancy outcomes.
