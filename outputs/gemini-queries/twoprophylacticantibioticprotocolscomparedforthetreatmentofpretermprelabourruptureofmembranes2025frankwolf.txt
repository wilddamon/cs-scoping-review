METHODS: This retrospective study included 170 women with PPROM at <37(0) weeks gestation, managed expectantly and treated with antibiotic prophylactic protocols: 83 received ampicillin and roxithromycin from February 2022 to March 2023, and 87 received cefuroxime and azithromycin from April 2023 to May 2024.
