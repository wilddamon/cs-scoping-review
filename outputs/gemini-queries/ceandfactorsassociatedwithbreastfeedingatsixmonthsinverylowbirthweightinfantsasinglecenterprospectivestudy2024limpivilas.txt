"Objective: Primary objective was to determine success rate of breastfeeding (BF) at 6-month-postnatal age (PNA). We conducted telephone interviews to assess feeding type and volume every 2 months until 12-month-PNA and reasons for BF cessation. Result(s): Eighty-nine VLBW infants were included.
