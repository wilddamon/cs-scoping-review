STUDY DESIGN: Retrospective cohort study of pregnancy outcomes of patients who underwent cervical cerclage, performed according to ACOG guidelines, between January 2006 and December 2014. Emergency cerclage was not performed in patients with uterine contractions, vaginal bleeding, or signs of chorioamnionitis.
