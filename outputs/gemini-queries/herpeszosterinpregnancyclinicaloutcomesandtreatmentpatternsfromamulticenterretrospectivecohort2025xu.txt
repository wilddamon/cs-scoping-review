Pregnant women, who experience natural immunosuppression, may face more severe symptoms or longer recovery. AIM: This multicenter study evaluates herpes zoster outcomes and treatment patterns in Chinese pregnant women. METHODS: This retrospective multicenter study analyzed real-world data from the Big Data Center at PLA General Hospital, examining medical records of pregnant women with herpes zoster from January 1, 2010, to June 30, 2024, across four hospitals.
