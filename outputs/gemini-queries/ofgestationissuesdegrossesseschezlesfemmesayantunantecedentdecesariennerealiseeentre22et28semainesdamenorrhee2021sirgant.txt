We included women who had a caesarean delivery between 22 and 28 weeks from December 1, 2014 to December 31, 2017. We then retrospectively collected data on subsequent pregnancies of these patients up to March 2020. We described the subsequent pregnancy rate and the outcomes of these pregnancies. Results: Among the 186 women who had a caesarean between 22 and 28 weeks, data from 103 of them could be collected, including 47 (45.6%) women who had 64 new pregnancies.
