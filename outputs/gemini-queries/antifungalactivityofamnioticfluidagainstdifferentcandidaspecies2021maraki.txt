AIMS: In the present work we evaluated whether amniotic fluid (AF) possesses direct antifungal activity against clinical isolates of Candidaalbicans and other Candida species. METHODS: A total of 23 AF samples from pregnant women with gestational age of 38-41 weeks were obtained under aseptic conditions by the aspiration of the amniotic sac during cesarean section.
