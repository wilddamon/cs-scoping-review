"AIM: The aim of this study was to evaluate the validity of gadolinium-enhanced magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) in diagnosing cesarean scar pregnancy (CSP) and to compare this with ultrasound results.;MATERIAL AND METHODS: Forty-two consecutive patients underwent both gadolinium-enhanced MRI and ultrasound to evaluate CSP before treatment from May 2010 to September 2013. Misdiagnosis occurred in seven cases in the first ultrasound, and report was inconclusive in one case. However, correct and definite diagnosis was achieved in repeated ultrasound in seven of the eight cases. No contrast-agent-related complications occurred in our series.;CONCLUSIONS: Gadolinium-enhanced MRI is highly specific in the diagnosis of CSP.
