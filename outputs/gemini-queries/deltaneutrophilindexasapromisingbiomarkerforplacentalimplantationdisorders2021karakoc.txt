"OBJECTIVE: We aimed to investigate the value of maternal serum delta neutrophil index (DNI) levels in predicting placenta accreta spectrum in patients with placenta previa.;METHODS: The patients who were found to have placenta previa totalis were included in our study. Significant efficiency of DNI value [area under the curve 0.899 (0.814-0.984)] was observed in differentiating patients with previa only and PAS.
