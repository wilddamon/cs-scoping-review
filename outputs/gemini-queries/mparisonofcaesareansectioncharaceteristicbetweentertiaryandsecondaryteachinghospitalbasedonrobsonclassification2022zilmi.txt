"This study aims to compare the characteristics of CS in tertiary and secondary teaching hospital based on Robson classification. This study is an observational analytical study using medical record data and maternal annual reports at Dr. Soetomo General Hospital and Universitas Airlangga Hospital Surabaya in January-December 2019. Robson Classification in Dr. Soetomo General Hospital showed that group 10 had the highest percentage of CS and largest relative contribution, while at Universitas Airlangga Hospital, group 5 had the highest percentage of CS and biggest relative contribution. Dr. Soetomo General Hospital has a larger relative contribution and population of group 10 related to the availability of more adequate NICU and ICU facilities, while Universitas Airlangga Hospital requires special attention in induction of labour, either in nulliparous or multiparous women. In summary, different types of hospital provide different characteristics of CS in Robson classification.
