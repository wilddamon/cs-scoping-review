"Objective: We investigated the mechanism whereby interleukin-6 (IL-6), an important inflammatory marker, influences trophoblast function during preeclampsia. Methods: Quantitative PCR and enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay were used to determine the IL-6 mRNA and protein levels, respectively. Seventy differentially expressed IL-6 downstream proteins were identified; these were enriched with various biological processes, molecular functions, cellular components, and biological pathways. Conclusions: IL-6 regulates trophoblast function by interacting with multiple proteins and pathways.
