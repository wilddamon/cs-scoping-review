METHODS: Totally, 90 parturient with American Society of Anesthesiology physical status grade I-II, undergoing spinal anesthesia with 2.5 mg sufentanil and 10 mg bupivacaine 0.5% were enrolled to this randomized, prospective, double-blind study.
