Objective of the study is to find the association between the deficiency of Vitamin D and preeclampsia development in primigravida. METHODOLOGY: This is a prospective observational study done at a tertiary care hospital-Government Maternity Hospital (GMH), Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India. 200 antenatal women attending OPD were included in the study.
