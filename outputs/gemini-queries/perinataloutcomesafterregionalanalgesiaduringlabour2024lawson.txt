AIMS: To examine the association between regional analgesia and maternal and neonatal outcomes. MATERIALS AND METHODS: A retrospective population-based cohort study of singleton term births in Victoria, Australia, between 2014 and 2020. RESULTS: There were 107 013 women who received regional analgesia and 214 416 women who did not.
