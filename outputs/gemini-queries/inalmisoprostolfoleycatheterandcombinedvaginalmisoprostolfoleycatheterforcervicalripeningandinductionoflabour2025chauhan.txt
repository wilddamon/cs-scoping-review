"Background: Induction of labour is needed when termination of pregnancy is obligatory. For induction, various methods are used, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. Combining two methods can have synergistic effects and may help overcome the shortcomings of the other method. Thus the present study was conducted to assess the safety and efficacy of combination of foley catheter and misoprostol vis a vis foley catheter and misoprostol alone. Method(s): It was a prospective randomized interventional study conducted on 300 patients who were randomly distributed in three groups of 100 patients each . Tablet misoprostol was used in group I, Foley catheter in group II and a combination of both in group III. The results were analysed using ANOVA and chi square test. Result(s): The induction to delivery interval was 13.91+/-5.94 hours in group I, 21.94+/-9.26 hours in group II and 11.69+/-7.67 hours in group III. The least time was taken by women in group III (p=0.001). Maximum adverse effects were noted with women who were given misoprostol while it was significantly less in the women induced with Foley alone (p=0.001). Difference in fetal and neonatal complications was not found to be statistically significant in all the three groups (p=0.53, p=0.88 respectively). Conclusion(s): The combination of two methods resulted in significantly early cervical ripening and a short induction to delivery time and the complications and side effects did not increase .The technique can thus be considered in situations where there is a need to hasten delivery with an unfavourable cervix.Copyright (c)2025Int.
