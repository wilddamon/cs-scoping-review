"""abstract ID: womensexperiencesofinductionoflabourwithmisoprostoltabletscomparedwithvaginalinsert2023hansen
"INTRODUCTION. The aim of this study was to compare nulliparous women's experiences with induction of labour using two different regimens of misoprostol. METHODS. We adapted a validated questionnaire regarding experience with induced labour. In two different hospitals, 123 women undergoing medical induction of labour completed a questionnaire after delivery. An independent-samples T-test was used for comparison of parametric continuous variables and Pearson's chi2 test was used for categorical data. The two groups differed regarding BMI and pregnancy complications. No adjusted estimates were calculated. RESULTS. Women induced with oral misoprostol experienced more painful induction of labour (p = 0.019) and described feeling that their length of stay at hospital was excessive (p = 0.028). Overall, the experience of giving birth after induction of labour was reported as "good" among 87.8% of women induced with oral misoprostol compared with 72.7% of the women induced with a slow-release misoprostol vaginal insert (p = 0.039). CONCLUSION. In two departments characterized by several differences, including whether vaginal or oral misoprostol was used, induction with oral misoprostol in an out-patient setting was associated with a better experience of labour than induction with a slow-release misoprostol vaginal insert.
