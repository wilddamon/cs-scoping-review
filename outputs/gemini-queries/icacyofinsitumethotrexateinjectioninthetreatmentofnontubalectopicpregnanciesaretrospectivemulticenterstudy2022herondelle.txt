PATIENTS: A total of 106 patients with nontubal ectopic pregnancies, including 59 interstitial, 39 cesarean scar, and 8 cervical or isthmic. INTERVENTIONS: Overall, 58 patients received methotrexate via intramuscular injection (IM group), 35 received methotrexate via in situ injection (IS group), and 13 received a combination of both in situ and intramuscular injections of methotrexate (IS + IM group). MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: The main end point of this study was measured via the primary success rate (defined as a negative b-human chorionic gonadotropin level without recourse to any additional treatment) of treatment with methotrexate according to injection site. CONCLUSION: Solely an intramuscular injection of methotrexate is a less efficient first-line treatment strategy for the conservative management of nontubular ectopic pregnancy.
