Method(s): It was a prospective, observational study conducted in 100 obstetric patients diagnosed with PIH who got admitted to the CCU in Department of Anaesthesiology and Critical Care at SNMMCH, DHANBAD, Jharkhand, India for one year. after approval from the Institutional Scientific and Ethics Committee after meeting the inclusion and exclusion criteria.
