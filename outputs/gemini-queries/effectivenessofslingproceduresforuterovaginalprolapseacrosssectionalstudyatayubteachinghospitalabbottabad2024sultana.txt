Setting(s): Ayub Teaching Hospital, Abbottabad. Population or sample: We included 17 patients aged 30-59 years who underwent sling procedures over a 2-year period, with varying degrees of prolapses. Method(s): A retrospective analysis of case records from 2022 to 2024 was conducted to assess improvements in quality of life, prolapse symptoms, complications and patient satisfaction.
