"Objective: To explore the incidence and factors associated with maternal near-miss. Design: Cross-sectional study with an embedded case-control study. Setting: Three tertiary referral hospitals in southern Ghana. Population: All women admitted to study facilities with pregnancy-related complications or for birth. Methods: An adapted version of the WHO Maternal Near Miss Screening Tool was used to identify maternal near-miss cases. These were compared with unmatched controls (uncomplicated deliveries) in a ratio of 1:2. Main outcome measures: Incidence of maternal near-miss, maternal near-miss to maternal mortality ratio, and cause of and factors associated with maternal near-miss.
