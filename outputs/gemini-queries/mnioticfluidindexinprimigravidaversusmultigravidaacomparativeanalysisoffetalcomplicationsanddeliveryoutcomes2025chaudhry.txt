"Objective: To compare fetal complications and delivery outcomes linked with low amniotic fluid index (AFI) in primigravida and multigravida women. Methodology: This cross-sectional comparative study included 120 pregnant women with low AFI (<=5 cm) in the third trimester. They were grouped into primigravida (n=60) and multigravida (n=60). Result(s): Primigravida patients were younger (mean age 24.8 +/- 3.6) compared to multigravida women (29.1 +/- 4.1; p<0.001). No significant difference was found between groups in mean AFI values. The rate of cesarean section was higher among primigravidas; 34 (56.7%) vs 26 (43.3%) and NICU admissions increased, 14 (23.3%) vs 8 (13.3%). Moreover, 5-minute Apgar score less than 7 was observed in 9 (15.0) primigravidas as opposed to 5 (8.3) multigravidas. Conclusion(s): Low AFI was not statistically significant, but it correlates with the higher incidence of fetal complications and the tendency to conduct inter-based deliveries in primigravida women.
