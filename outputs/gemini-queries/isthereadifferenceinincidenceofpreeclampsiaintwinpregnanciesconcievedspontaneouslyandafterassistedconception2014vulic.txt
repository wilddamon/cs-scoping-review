"We aimed to compare perinatal outcome and prevalence of preeclampsia in twins conceived spontaneously and after assisted reproductive technologies (ART). Material and methods. We conducted a retrospective study that included twin pregnancies in period 01.01.2011. - 31.12.2012. Conclusion. Incidence of preeclampsia in twin pregnancies conceived after ART is increased.
