Aim(s): The study aimed at finding out the incidence and the etiological risk factors responsible for preterm labor that could be targeted to reduce its risk. Material(s) and Method(s): In this prospective cohort study of 1 year, a total of 440 antenatal patients diagnosed with preterm labor were included and were followed up.
