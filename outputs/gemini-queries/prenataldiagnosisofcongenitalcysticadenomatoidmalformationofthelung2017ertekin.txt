"Congenital cystic adenomatoid malformation (CCAM) is the hamartomatous proliferation of terminal respiratory bronchioles and represents a quarter of congenital lung diseases. On prenatal ultrasonography, uniform hyperechoic mass, variable echogenic cysts, or multicystic mass surrounded by echogenic stroma may be observed. This case report illustrates prenatal diagnosis of a microcystictype CCAM in a 30-year-old female patient, pregnant with twins (week 20), and confirmed by the microscopic examination of autopsy.
