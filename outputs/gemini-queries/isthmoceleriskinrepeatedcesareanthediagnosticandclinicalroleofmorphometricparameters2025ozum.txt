METHODS: A cross-sectional study was conducted with 116 symptomatic and asymptomatic women aged 18-45 years who had undergone one or more cesarean sections. RESULTS: Isthmocele was identified in 71.6% of participants, with prevalence rising from 42.9% after the first cesarean to 100% after the fourth. Niche length had the highest diagnostic accuracy for symptomatic isthmocele (AUC 0.700; 95% CI 0.589-0.796; cutoff 5 mm).
