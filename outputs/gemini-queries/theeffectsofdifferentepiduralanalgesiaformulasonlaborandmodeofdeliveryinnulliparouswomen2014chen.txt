MATERIALS AND METHODS: A total of 480 nulliparous women were randomized into four groups, with 120 in each. Group A received a loading dose of 10 mL of 1 mg/mL ropivacaine with 2 mg/mL fentanyl, then an intermittent bolus of 5 mL with a background infusion of 5 mL/hour by PCEA. CONCLUSION: The C/S rate was higher in Groups C and D; however, the instrumental delivery rate was lower in Groups A and C.
