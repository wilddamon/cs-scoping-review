"Objective: To evaluate the maternal and fetal outcome in pregnant women with sickle cell disease (SCD) compared with healthy women. Setting(s): Department of Gynecology and Obstetrics, Salmaniya Medical Complex, Bahrain. Design(s): Retrospective Case-Control Study. Method(s): Patients with SCD who delivered from 1 January 2011 to 31 December 2012 were reviewed. The matched controls had neither SCD nor sickle cell trait. Result(s): Patients with sickle cell disease required significantly more admissions during their pregnancy, 135 (78.4%) compared to the control, 74 (37.4%).
