Material(s) and Method(s): This prospective study was conducted on pregnant women who were admitted with PPROM. The patients were divided into two groups based on their gestational age. The first group consisted of women with less than 30 weeks of gestation and the second group included those with 30 to 33 weeks and 6 days of gestation.
