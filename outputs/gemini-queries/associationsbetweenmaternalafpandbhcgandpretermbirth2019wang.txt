"""abstract ID: associationsbetweenmaternalafpandbhcgandpretermbirth2019wang
"Objective a Preterm birth (PTB) is a significant public health problem. We aimed to explore whether alpha fetal protein (AFP) or b-human gonadotropin (b-HCG) levels during pregnancy were associated with PTB in Chinese population. Study Design a The clinical data collected Nanjing Medical University Affiliated Suzhou Hospital and Wuxi Maternity and Child Health Care Hospital between January 2006 and December 2011 were analyzed retrospectively. A total of 64,999 pregnant women were registered. In addition, 13,828 pregnant women were collected serum from the second trimester. The maternal serum AFP and b-HCG were measured by enzyme immunoassay. Results a In our study, the rate of PTB is 6.23%. With each unit increase of maternal AFP concentration, the adjusted odds of PTB was increased by 69.3% (odds ratio = 1.693, 95% confidence interval: 1.434-1.999, p = 0.00). We set AFP concentrations as high, medium, and low levels. When comparing with low concentration of AFP, high concentration of AFP (>=1.179 M) was positively associated with PTB with adjustment for potential confounders (p < 0.05). Nevertheless, no statistically significant associations were observed between maternal b-HCG and PTB. Conclusion a In this study, maternal AFP concentration was associated with increased risk of PTB.
