Our previous study reported that initiating early antiretroviral treatment at three days post-SIV infection resulted in approximately eighty percent of pediatric virologic remission. In this study, we investigated treatment outcomes in postnatally SHIV-exposed infant macaques when early intervention was delayed by two days, as well as the mechanisms underlying virologic control. The results showed that, although initiating treatment at five days post-exposure effectively suppressed viral replication, only one of the three infant macaques achieved a sustained state of virologic remission following analytical treatment interruption.
