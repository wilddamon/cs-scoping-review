Method(s): After ethical approval, this questionnaire-based observational study was carried out for a period of 1.5 year in Department of Paediatrics, SMS Medical College and Attached Hospitals, Jaipur. Twenty-five questions related to knowledge, attitude, and practices were used to get data in a questionnaire. Result(s): Out of total 500 study participants, maximum 156 (31.2%) were in age group of 26-30 years. Mean score of knowledge and attitude was 11.55+/-4.17.
