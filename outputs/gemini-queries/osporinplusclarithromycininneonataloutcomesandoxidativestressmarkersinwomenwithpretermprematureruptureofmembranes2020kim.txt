Material(s) and Method(s): The present study included patients with singleton pregnancies with PPROM at earlier than 34 gestational weeks who were admitted to the Chonnam National University, Gwangju, Korea, between February 2007 and December 2015. There was no significant difference in oxidative stress markers after the administration of antibiotics between regimens A and B. Conclusion(s): The present results show that there was no difference between the two regimens on the latency period and improvement of neonatal outcomes.
