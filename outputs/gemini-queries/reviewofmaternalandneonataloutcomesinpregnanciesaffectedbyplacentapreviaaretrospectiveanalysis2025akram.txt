"Background: Placenta previa is a critical pregnancy complication marked by the placenta covering the cervical os, increasing the risk of severe maternal and neonatal morbidity and mortality. This condition poses significant challenges in obstetric management due to the high risks of hemorrhage and preterm delivery. Aim(s): This study aims to analyze the maternal and neonatal outcomes in pregnancies complicated by placenta previa, to better understand the implications of current management strategies. Method(s): A retrospective cohort study was conducted at Anugrah Narayan Magadh Medical College and Hospital, involving 100 pregnancies diagnosed with placenta previa between August 2023 and December 2024. Neonatal complications were also notable, with 25% of newborns requiring NICU admission and 30% suffering from low birth weight. Statistical analysis revealed significant associations between placenta previa and adverse delivery outcomes (p < 0.01). Conclusion(s): Placenta previa significantly increases the likelihood of Cesarean delivery and is associated with high rates of maternal and neonatal complications. These findings emphasize the need for stringent monitoring and proactive management in pregnancies complicated by placenta previa. Recommendations: Enhanced prenatal screening for placenta previa should be implemented to allow for early diagnosis and tailored management plans.
