"Objective: To determine if visceral adipose tissue (VAT) and subcutaneous adipose tissue (SAT) depth in early pregnancy differs by maternal ethnicity. Results: SAT did not differ between groups, but VAT did. Compared with Canadian-born women (3.9 cm, 95% CI 3.7-4.1), mean VAT depth was higher among Latin American (4.6 cm, 95% CI 4.1-5.2), Sub-Saharan African (5.0 cm, 95% CI 4.0-6.1), and Caribbean (6.0 cm, 95% CI 4.8-7.3) women.
