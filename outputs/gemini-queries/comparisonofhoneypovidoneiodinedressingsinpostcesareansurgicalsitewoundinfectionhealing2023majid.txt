METHOD: This prospective randomised cohort study was carried out at at OBGYN Department Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Center from 1(st) August 2022 till 31(st) December 2022. Patients were randomly allocated into groups A and B. Group-A was dressed with honey povidone-iodine, while Group-B only had povidone-iodine. On day tenth the mean ASEPSIS score of Group-A was 18.26, while that of Group-B was 23.86 [t (68) = -2.021, p = .047].
