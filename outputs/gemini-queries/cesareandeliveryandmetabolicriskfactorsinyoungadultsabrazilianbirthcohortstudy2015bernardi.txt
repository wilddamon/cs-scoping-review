OBJECTIVE: We investigated the association of CD and metabolic risk factors in young adults in a cohort study who were 23-25 y of age at follow-up. DESIGN: We used data from a cohort study in Ribeirao Preto, Brazil. Baseline data on 6827 singleton pregnancies were collected in 1978-1979, and a sample of 2063 subjects were followed up 23-25 y later (2002-2004). Information on the type of delivery, birth weight, maternal age, parity, maternal schooling, and maternal smoking was obtained after birth. Anthropometric data, biochemical measurements, and information on participant schooling and smoking history were collected at 23-25 y of age.
