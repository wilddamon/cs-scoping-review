METHODS: A cross-sectional study was conducted from February 2018 to July 2022, involving 252 participants from the Shanghai Donor Human Milk Bank, Shanghai Children's Hospital. 16S rRNA sequencing was performed, and the microbiota composition was analyzed using K-means clustering, multiple linear regression, and sparse estimation of correlations among microbiomes.
