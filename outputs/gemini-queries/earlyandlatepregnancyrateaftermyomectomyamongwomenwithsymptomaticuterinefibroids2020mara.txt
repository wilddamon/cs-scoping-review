"OBJECTIVE: To determine the number of spontaneous-ly conceived pregnancies achieved during the first and second year after laparoscopic or open myomectomy. STUDY DESIGN: A multi-centric, nonrandomized, prospective clinical study. The study cohort consisted of 392 reproductive-age women with symptomatic intramural uterine fibroids desiring pregnancy who underwent laparoscopic or open myo-mectomy and were monitored for 2.5 years after sur-gery. The number and rate of pregnancies during the first and second year after myomectomy were evaluated, and the results between the groups were compared. RESULT(S): During a follow-up period of 2.5 years, no significant difference in total pregnancy rate was recorded in the 2 groups.
