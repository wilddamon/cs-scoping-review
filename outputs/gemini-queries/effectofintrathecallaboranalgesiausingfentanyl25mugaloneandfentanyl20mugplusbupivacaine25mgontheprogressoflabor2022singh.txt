Materials and Methodology: Patients in the group SA (n = 33) were administered with an intrathecal injection of 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine 2.5 mg (0.5 mL) and fentanyl 25 microg (0.5 mL), volume made to 1.5 mL with normal saline.
