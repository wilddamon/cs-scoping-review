"Introduction: Adenomyosis is a benign uterine disorder in which endometrial glands and stroma present ectopically in myometrium. Adenomyosis is one of most common cause of AUB. Most common in age group of 40-50 years, with risk factors like multiparity, menstrual irregularities, caesarian section, dilation curettage and smoking. Aim and Objective: Aim of this study was to find association of adenomyosis with other gynecological condition and secondly to assess the functional state of endometrium. Material(s) and Method(s): Study conducted in tertiary care center RNTMC Udaipur Rajasthan over a period of 1 year. All patients who underwent hysterectomy (Vaginal/abdominal) due to abnormal uterine bleeding were evaluated. Clinically Data recorded and analyzed by SPSS software. Result(s): Total 150 hysterectomy specimen were used, majority were in between 41-51 years of age, mostly were nulliparous, Most Common endometrial pattern was proliferative. Most common associated pathology was ovarian pathology. Serous cyst (47 cases) only 2 cases of serous papillary adenocarcinoma noted. Conclusion(s): Adenomyosis has very important role in decreasing the quality of women life. Careful examination of gross and microscopic foci of adenomyosis and associated pathologies can help in improving the quality of life for female and will help in the betterment of patient presented with abnormal uterine bleeding.Copyright (c) 2024, Dr. Yashwant Research Labs Pvt.
