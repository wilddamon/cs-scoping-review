"Objective: To compare maternal and neonatal outcomes in twin pregnancies with previous cesarean section according to the mode of delivery. Methods: A retrospective study from 1st January 2002 to 31st of December 2016 at the maternity center of Nancy (France) included all twins pregnancies > 24 weeks of gestation with previous cesarean section. Maternal and neonatal dates were compared between the group "planned cesarean section" (PCS) and the group "trial of labor after cesarean" (TOLAC). Results: In all, 99 women were included in this study: 66 of them in the "PCS" group and 33 in the "TOLAC" group. In the "TOLAC" group, the labor was induced for 39.4% (n = 13) of the success rate of TOLAC was 54.5% (n = 18). There was no significant increase in maternal complications in case of success or failure of TOLAC. There was a statistically significant difference between the groups about the blood loss: 225 +- 106 mL ("TOLAC" group) versus 800 +- 141 mL ("PCS" group) (P = 0,01). The duration of hospitalization was significantly shorter in the "TOLAC" group 4.9 +- 2 days versus 6.0 +- 1.9 days (P = 0.01).
