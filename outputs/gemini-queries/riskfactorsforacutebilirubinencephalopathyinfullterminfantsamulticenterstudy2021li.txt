Method(s): We analyzed the general clinical data and risk factors as determinants of ABE in a total of 329 full-term newborns treated with total serum bilirubin (TSB) at a level of 20 mg/dL (342 mumol/L) to 7 neonatal intensive care units in Hebei Province, China over the past 18 months.
