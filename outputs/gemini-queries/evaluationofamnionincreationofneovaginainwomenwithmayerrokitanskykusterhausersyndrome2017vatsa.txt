"Objective To assess the outcome of amnion vaginoplasty in cases of vaginal agenesis due to Mayer-Rokitansky-Kuster-Hauser (MRKH) syndrome managed at the authors' institution. Design Retrospective study. Setting Tertiary care hospital. Patient(s) Fifty women with MRKH who underwent neovaginoplasty. Intervention(s) Modified McIndoe's vaginoplasty was done in all the patients, using human amnion graft.
