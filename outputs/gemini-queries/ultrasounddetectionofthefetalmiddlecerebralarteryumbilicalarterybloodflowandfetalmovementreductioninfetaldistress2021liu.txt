"Objective: To evaluate the diagnostic value of ultrasound detection of the fetal middle cerebral artery, umbilical artery blood flow and fetal movement reduction in fetal distress. Method(s): A total of 30 cases of pregnant women with fetal distress (FIUD) in our hospital were selected as the observation group, and 60 cases of normal pregnant women in the same period were selected as the control group.
