"OBJECTIVE: To describe our experience with adnexal masses >=5 cm in diameter removed at cesarean delivery during a 10-year-period. There were 2 (3.3%) borderline tumors and 1 malignancy (1.6%, mucinous cystadenocarcinoma). CONCLUSION: Removal of an adnexal mass >=5 cm in diameter during cesarean operation is not associated with increased morbidity except for longer operative length.
