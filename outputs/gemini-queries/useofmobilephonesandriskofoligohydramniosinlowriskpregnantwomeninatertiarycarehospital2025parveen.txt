"Background and objectives: It has been established that oligohydramnios strongly correlates with low birth weight, growth inhibition, and newborn mortality and morbidity. Excessive mobile phone usage in the postnatal period has also been linked in some studies to behavioral issues in children as they age; however, it is unclear how mobile phone use during pregnancy affects neonatal birth weight. The current study sought to ascertain the relationships between oligohydramnios and neonatal birth weight and excessive mobile phone use during pregnancy. At Hamdard University Hospital, Karachi, we conducted our study on pregnant women who had been admitted during their pregnancy. Methods: A sample of 182 mothers participated in a survey on maternal characteristics and maternal mobile phone usage information during pregnancy. This was a cross-sectional analytical study performed from 1st January 2022 to 30th October 2023. Results: The most common complication was oligohydramnios, with a rate of 73.6%, and the least common was preterm birth, with a rate of only 0.5%. Comparing modes of delivery, the rates of elective cesarean and emergency cesarean were almost similar, with 48.4 and 41.8%, respectively. Only 9.9% of women had a normal vaginal delivery. Most of the women preferred to keep their phones on the side table during sleep (73.1%). Conclusion: According to this study, using a cell phone excessively while pregnant may increase the risk of oligohydramnios, lower birth weight, and a high rate of emergency C-sections.
