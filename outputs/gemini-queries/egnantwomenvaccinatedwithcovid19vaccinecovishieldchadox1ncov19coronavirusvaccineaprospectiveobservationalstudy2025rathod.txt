"Objectives: (1) To estimate the frequency of side effects and adverse reactions in pregnant women vaccinated with Covishield (ChAdOx1 nCoV-19 coronavirus vaccine). (2) To estimate the obstetric-related side effects and adverse reactions in pregnant women vaccinated with Covishield. Material(s) and Method(s): This is a prospective observational study conducted in Vani Vilas Hospital, Bengaluru, from 1st August 2021 to 28th January 2022 among pregnant women who received the Covishield vaccine. They were followed up to 4 weeks after the second dose. Result(s): None of the patients had any symptoms or signs of adverse vaccine reaction within half an hour of the first dose of the vaccine. About 40.30% had symptoms and signs in the next 4 weeks after the first dose. The most common symptom was fever (29.84%). Fever with chills was seen in 10.44% of participants. Total 30 out of 67 delivered during the follow-up period. All were term deliveries. There was one intrauterine fetal demise, two spontaneous abortions, and four low-birth-weight babies. Conclusion(s): Vaccination of pregnant women with the Covishield vaccine, which included pregnant women from all three trimesters, was found to be safe when followed up for a period of 4 weeks after two doses. Clinical significance: Vaccination of pregnant women with the Covishield vaccine, encompassing participants from all three trimesters, demonstrated safety during a follow-up period of 4 weeks after the administration of two doses.
