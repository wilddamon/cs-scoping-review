"Background: Obesity is a huge concern and a significant lifestyle-related public health issue worldwide. The objective of study was to calculate the percentage of overweight and obese children among under five who attended the Burdwan Medical College immunization clinic in the study period, as well as enumerate the risk factors associated with it. Material(s) and Method(s): An institution based descriptive type cross sectional study was conducted in the immunization clinic of Burdwan Medical College from 1st February to 1st March 2024.Final data was collected from 68 subjects.
