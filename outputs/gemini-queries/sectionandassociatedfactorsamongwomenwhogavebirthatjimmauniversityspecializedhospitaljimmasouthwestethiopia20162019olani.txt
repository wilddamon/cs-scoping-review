"Background:-Cesarean section is an emergency obstetric care that is designed to reduce maternal and newborn morbidity and mortality. However, there are still complications associated with this procedure. Maternal morbidity associated with Caesarean sections is five to ten times greater than that associated with vaginal delivery. Objective(s): The aim of this study is to assess the prevalence of cesarean section and associated factors among women who gave birth at Jimma University Specialized Hospital, South West Ethiopia. Method(s): Institution based cross-sectional study design was employed from March 25 to April 08, 2016.The consecutive-sampling technique was used to select 250 subjects.
